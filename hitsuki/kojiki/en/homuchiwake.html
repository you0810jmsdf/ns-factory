Prince 本牟智和気御子* and 多遅摩毛理*

本牟智和気御子と多遅摩毛理

Prince 本牟智和氣御子*, who did not speak even when his long beard had grown down to his chest, was found to be under a curse of the Great Deity of Izumo (出雲大神); he went to worship at Izumo and, on the way back, spoke for the first time. At the end of the reign of Emperor Suinin, 多遲摩毛理* brought back the "登岐士玖能迦玖能木實"* (the present-day tachibana, 橘) from the Eternal Land (常世國), but the emperor had already died.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textPrince 本牟智和氣御子* did not speak even when his eight-fist-long beard reached his chest. Through the emperor's dream and the great divination (太占) it was found that this was a curse arising from the will of the Great Deity of Izumo, so the prince was sent to worship the Great Deity at Izumo.
  2. Main textOn the way back from worshipping at Izumo, on the bank of the 肥河*, the prince saw the leafy green "mountain" decoration set up by 岐比佐都美* and spoke for the first time. The emperor rejoiced and had 菟上王* build the shrine of the deity at Izumo.
  3. Main text山邊之大鶙* pursued a swan (鵠) through many provinces, from 木國 to 高志國*, caught it at 和那美之水門* and presented it, but even on seeing the bird the prince still did not speak.
  4. Main text多遲麻毛理* brought back the "登岐士玖能迦玖能木實"* from the Eternal Land (常世國), but the emperor had already died, and he wept and wailed before the imperial tomb and died. The Kojiki records that this fruit is the present-day tachibana (橘).
  5. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki (the 23rd year of Emperor Suinin), 譽津別命* sees a swan and says "是何物耶"; he is said to have gained speech by playing with the swan that 天湯河板擧* caught and presented. The curse of the Great Deity of Izumo and the pilgrimage to Izumo are not told.
  6. CommentaryThe Kokugakuin University deity-name database treats the Great Deity of Izumo in this story as another name of Ōkuninushi-no-Kami (大国主神), and treats 葦原色許男大神*, which appears in the prince's words, as a name of the same deity. It then introduces the view that the whole story, including the night with 肥長比賣*, is connected with the rituals of Izumo, and a theory that sees it as a story telling how the problem of the worship of the Izumo deity was resolved. The story

The emperor had a two-hulled small boat (二俣小舟) made from a forked 榲 tree that stood at 相津 in Owari*, floated it on the 市師 Pond and the 輕 Pond in Yamato*, and let the prince play in it. But this prince still did not speak, even when his eight-fist-long beard reached down in front of his chest. One day, hearing the cry of a swan (鵠) flying high in the sky, the prince for the first time made "阿藝登比"*. The emperor therefore sent a man named 山邊之大鶙* to catch that bird.

大鶙* pursued the swan from 木國 to 針間國*, then over 稻羽國 to 旦波國 and 多遲麻國*, and, circling east, over Ōmi (近淡海國) and 三野國*, and from Owari (尾張國) to 科野國*. At last he spread a net at 和那美之水門 in 高志國*, caught the bird, and brought it back to the capital to present it. But even on seeing the bird, the prince did not speak as the emperor had hoped.

When the emperor fell asleep troubled, he was told in a dream, "If you put my shrine in order like the emperor's palace (御舍), the prince will surely come to speak." When he sought through the great divination (太占) which deity's will this was, it was a curse arising from the will of the Great Deity of Izumo. 曙立王* was chosen by divination to accompany the prince; through ukehi-style oath divination (宇氣比), 曙立王 made a heron in a tree by the 鷺巣 Pond fall dead and then come back to life, and made a broad-leaved 熊白檮 oak in front of the 甜白檮* wither and then come back to life. The emperor gave 曙立王 the name "倭者師木登美豐朝倉曙立王"*.

曙立王 and 菟上王* set out accompanying the prince. Divination showed that from both the 那良戸 and the 大坂戸* gates they would meet the lame or the blind, and that only the 木戸* gate was auspicious, so they left by it, establishing 品遲部* groups wherever they went. After arriving in Izumo and finishing their worship of the Great Deity, on the way back they built the 黒巣 Bridge* in the 肥河 river and a temporary palace, and lodged the prince there. When 岐比佐都美*, ancestor of the governors of Izumo (出雲國造), set up downstream a decoration like a mountain of green leaves and was about to offer a meal, the prince said, "That thing downstream like a mountain of green leaves looks like a mountain but is not a mountain. Is it not the great ritual ground of the priests (祝) who worship 葦原色許男大神* in Izumo?"

The princes accompanying him rejoiced on hearing this, moved the prince to the 檳榔之長穂宮*, and sent a fast-horse messenger to the capital. The prince spent one night with 肥長比賣*, but when he secretly peeped, that beautiful person was a snake, and he fled in fear. As 肥長比賣 pursued him by boat, lighting up the sea, the prince grew even more afraid, hauled his boat over "山多和"* and fled up to the capital. The princes reported, "Because he worshipped the Great Deity, the prince has spoken," and the emperor rejoiced, sent 菟上王 back to Izumo to build the shrine, and for the prince established the 鳥取部, 鳥甘部, 品遲部, 大湯坐 and 若湯坐*.

In accordance with the words of his former consort, the emperor summoned four daughters of 美知能宇斯王*: 比婆須比賣命, 弟比賣命, 歌凝比賣命 and 圓野比賣命*. But he kept only two of them, 比婆須比賣命 and 弟比賣命, and sent the other two back to their home province as very ugly. 圓野比賣*, ashamed, tried to die by hanging herself from a tree branch at 相樂 in 山代國*, so that place is called 懸木 (the present 相樂); and when she reached 弟國 she fell into a deep pool and died, so that place is called 墮國 (the present 弟國)*.

The emperor also sent 多遲麻毛理*, ancestor of the 三宅連* and others, to the Eternal Land (常世國) to seek the "登岐士玖能迦玖能木實"*. 多遲摩毛理 reached that land, picked the fruit, and brought back eight 縵 and eight 矛 (縵八縵, 矛八矛)*, but in the meantime the emperor had already died. 多遲摩毛理 presented four 縵 and four 矛 to the empress, offered the remaining four 縵 and four 矛 at the door of the emperor's tomb, and, holding up the fruit and weeping and wailing, reported that he had brought it; then, still weeping and wailing, he died. That fruit is the present-day tachibana (橘). The emperor died at the age of 153, and his tomb is in the 御立野 of 菅原*.

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

八拳鬚至于心前。眞事登波受。

Even when his long beard reached down in front of his chest, he still did not speak.

修理我宮如天皇之御舍者。御子必眞事登波牟。

The revelation in the emperor's dream: "If you put my shrine in order like the emperor's palace (御舍), the prince will surely come to speak."

因拜大神。大御子物詔故。參上來。

The report of the accompanying princes: "Because he worshipped the Great Deity, the prince has spoken. Therefore we have returned."

其登岐士玖能迦玖能木實者。是今橘者也。

The fruit that 多遲摩毛理* brought back from the Eternal Land (常世國) is the present-day tachibana (橘).

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 6 — the 23rd year of Emperor Suinin, autumn, 9th month (丙寅朔丁卯)

In the Nihon Shoki, 譽津別王* reached thirty years of age, and even with a beard eight hand-spans long he wept like a child and did not speak, so the emperor had his ministers deliberate on it.

是生年既卅。髯鬚八掬。猶泣如兒。常不言何由矣。
Nihon Shoki, Book 6 — the 23rd year of Emperor Suinin, 11th month (甲午朔乙未)

In the Nihon Shoki, 天湯河板擧* pursues the swan, catches it in Izumo (or, according to an alternative statement (或曰), in Tajima) and presents it, and 譽津別命* gains speech by playing with the swan. For this service he is granted the title 鳥取造, and the 鳥取部, 鳥養部 and 譽津部* are established. The curse of the Great Deity of Izumo and the pilgrimage to Izumo are not written.

譽津別命弄是鵠。遂得言語。
Nihon Shoki, Book 6 — the 90th year of Emperor Suinin, spring, 2nd month (庚子朔) / the year after his death, spring, 3rd month (辛未朔壬午)

In the Nihon Shoki, 田道間守* is sent to the Eternal Land (常世國) in the 2nd month of the 90th year of Emperor Suinin to seek the 非時香菓*, and he returns with eight rods and eight strings (八竿八縵) in the 3rd month of the year after the emperor's death. 田道間守 wept and wailed facing the emperor's tomb and died. The Kojiki's statement that half was presented to the empress does not appear in this entry.

非時香菓八竿八縵焉。
