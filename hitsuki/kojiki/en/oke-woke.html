Emperor Seinei and 意祁命 and 袁祁命 (readings unconfirmed): the Song Gathering with 志毘臣*

清寧天皇と意祁命・袁祁命（志毘臣との歌垣）

After Emperor Seinei (白髪大倭根子命)* died without children, the brothers 意祁命 and 袁祁命 (readings unconfirmed), sons of 市辺之押歯王* who had been hiding in Harima Province (針間国), revealed themselves at a house-warming banquet and were found. The younger brother, 袁祁命, exchanged songs with 志毘臣* at a song gathering (歌垣) and brought him down, and, at his elder brother’s yielding, he ruled the realm first.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textAt the house-warming banquet of 志自牟* in Harima Province (針間国), the younger brother 袁祁命*, who had been working as a boy tending the fire, declared his identity while dancing, and it became known that they were sons of 押歯王 of 市辺 (readings unconfirmed). 山部連小楯*, astonished, sheltered the two and sent word to the capital.
  2. Main textEmperor Seinei (白髪大倭根子命)* had neither empress nor children, and the 白髪部* was established as his namesake group (御名代). It is recorded that after his death there was no prince to succeed him, and that 飯豊王*, younger sister of 市辺忍歯別王*, was at the 高木角刺宮 palace at 忍海 in Katsuragi (readings unconfirmed).
  3. Main textAt a song gathering (歌垣), 袁祁命* exchanged songs over 大魚* with 志毘臣*, ancestor of the 平群臣*; the next morning he consulted with his elder brother 意富祁命*, raised an army and killed 志毘臣.
  4. Main textThe elder brother, 意祁命*, yielded the realm, saying it was his younger brother’s merit that he had revealed their names and shown their rank, and the younger brother, 袁祁命*, ruled the realm first.
  5. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki, it is Emperor Buretsu in his time as crown prince who exchanges songs at a song gathering with 鮪*, son of 平群真鳥*, over a woman (影媛)*, and a song closely resembling the one 袁祁命* composes in the Kojiki appears as the crown prince’s song.

The story

白髪大倭根子命* ruled the realm from the 甕栗宮 palace at 伊波礼 (readings unconfirmed). Because he had neither empress nor children, he established the 白髪部* as his namesake group (御名代). When the emperor died there was no prince to rule the realm, so they searched for a prince to take up the succession (日継); it is recorded that 忍海郎女*, younger sister of 市辺忍歯別王*, also called 飯豊王*, was at the 高木角刺宮 palace at 忍海 in Katsuragi (readings unconfirmed).

When 山部連小楯* was appointed governor of Harima Province (針間国), he went to the banquet celebrating the new house of a man of that province named 志自牟*. As the banquet grew lively, everyone danced in turn, and the two boys who were tending the fire beside the hearth were also made to dance. The two deferred to each other, “You first, elder brother,” “You first, younger brother,” and the people laughed at them; but when the elder had finished dancing, the younger recited while dancing, declaring that they were descendants of 押歯王 of 市辺 (readings unconfirmed), child of Emperor 伊邪本和気 (伊邪本和気天皇)*.

On hearing this, 小楯* was astonished and tumbled from his seat; he drove the people of the house outside, set the two princes on his left and right knees and wept. He gathered people, built a temporary palace, had the two live there, and sent a messenger by fast horse to the capital. Their aunt, 飯豊王*, rejoiced at this and had the two come up to the palace.

Before the two had begun to rule the realm, 志毘臣*, ancestor of the 平群臣*, stood at a song gathering (歌垣) and took the hand of 大魚*, daughter of the 菟田首* and others, whom 袁祁命* intended to marry. 袁祁命 also stood at the song gathering, and 志毘臣 and 袁祁命 exchanged songs in turn. It is recorded that as he sang, 志毘臣 grew more and more angry.

The two sang to each other until dawn and then each withdrew. The next morning 意富祁命 and 袁祁命 (readings unconfirmed) conferred: the people of the court come to the court in the morning but gather at 志毘’s gate at midday; now 志毘 will be asleep and there will be no one at his gate; if not now, it will be hard to carry out a plan. They then raised an army, surrounded the house of 志毘臣* and killed him.

After that, the two princes each tried to yield the realm to the other. The elder brother, 意祁命*, firmly yielded it, saying that if his younger brother had not revealed their names when they were in the house of 志自牟* in Harima, they would never have become lords of the realm; this was his younger brother’s achievement, so although he himself was the elder, his younger brother should rule the realm first. For this reason 袁祁命* could not refuse, and he ruled the realm first.

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

伊邪本和氣。天皇之御子。市邊之。押齒王之。奴末。

The younger prince’s declaration: we are descendants (末) of 押歯王 of 市辺 (readings unconfirmed), child of Emperor 伊邪本和気*.

小楯連聞驚而。自床墮轉而。

On hearing the declaration, 小楯* was astonished and tumbled from his seat.

故吾雖兄。猶汝命。先治天下而。堅讓。

The words of the elder brother, 意祁命*: although I am the elder, you should still rule the realm first; he yielded it firmly.

Deities and people, and the shrines that enshrine them

  • 白髮大倭根子命 * (Emperor Seinei)The emperor who ruled the realm from the 甕栗宮 palace at 伊波礼 (readings unconfirmed). He had neither empress nor children, and after his death there was no heir.
  • 飯豐王, also 忍海郎女*Younger sister of 市辺忍歯別王*. She was at the 高木角刺宮 palace at 忍海 in Katsuragi (readings unconfirmed), and has the two princes, once found, come up to the palace.
  • 山部連小楯*Governor of Harima Province (針間国). He finds the two princes at the house-warming banquet of 志自牟* and sends word to the capital.
  • 志自牟*A man of Harima Province (針間国). The two princes hid and worked in his house, where the house-warming banquet is held.
  • 意富祁命, also 意祁命*Son of 市辺王* and the elder brother. He dances first at the house-warming banquet, and later yields the realm to his younger brother.
  • 袁祁命*Son of 市辺王* and the younger brother. He declares his identity while dancing, exchanges songs with 志毘臣* at a song gathering, and rules the realm first.
  • 市邊之押齒王, also 市邊忍齒別王*Father of the two princes. His name is mentioned in the younger brother’s declaration.
  • 伊邪本和氣天皇*Mentioned in the younger brother’s declaration as the father of 押歯王 of 市辺 (readings unconfirmed).
  • 志毘臣*Ancestor of the 平群臣*. At a song gathering he takes the hand of 大魚* and exchanges songs with 袁祁命*, but is struck down the next morning.
  • 大魚*Daughter of the 菟田首* and others. The woman 袁祁命* intended to marry.

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 15, the 2nd year of Emperor Seinei, winter, 11th month

In the Nihon Shoki, in the 11th month, winter, of the 2nd year of Emperor Seinei, the provincial governor of Harima (播磨国司), 伊予来目部小楯*, finds 億計 and 弘計 (readings unconfirmed; corresponding to 意祁 and 袁祁) at the new house of the head of the 縮見 granary (縮見屯倉首), 忍海部造細目 (readings unconfirmed), in Akashi District (赤石郡). In the Kojiki, the master of the house is written “志自牟”*.

於赤石郡縮見屯倉首忍海部造細目新室
Nihon Shoki, Book 15, Emperor Kenzō, record before accession (the 5th year of Emperor Seinei)

In the Kojiki, 飯豊王* is the “younger sister” of 市辺忍歯別王*, but in this entry of the Nihon Shoki she is 飯豊青皇女*, the “elder sister” of Emperor 弘計 (弘計天皇)*; after Emperor Seinei’s death, while the brothers were yielding the throne to each other, she conducted government at the 忍海角刺宮 palace*, and she died in the 11th month, winter, of the same year.

由是天皇姉飯豐青皇女於忍海角刺宮臨朝秉政。
Nihon Shoki, Book 16, Emperor Buretsu, record before accession

In the Nihon Shoki, it is the crown prince (later Emperor Buretsu) who exchanges songs with 鮪* over 影媛* at the song place (歌場, a song gathering) of 海柘榴市*, and a song closely resembling “志毘賀波多傳爾。都麻多弖理美由”, which the prince composes in the Kojiki, appears as the crown prince’s song.

思寐我簸多泥儞、都摩陀氐理彌喩。
