Emperor Ōjin's Courtships and 髪長比売*

応神天皇の妻問いと髪長比売

品陀和気命* (Emperor Ōjin) asked 大山守命 and 大雀命* which they found dearer, an elder child or a younger child, and designated 宇遅能和紀郎子* as his successor. The emperor was united with 宮主矢河枝比売* at the village of 木幡*; 髪長比売*, whom he had summoned from Hyūga, was given to 大雀命 at 大雀命's request; and the 国主 of Yoshino (吉野之國主*) presented sake and songs.

Read in the Nihon Shoki: The birth and accession of Emperor Ōjin and the first half of his reign ›

Key points to remember

  1. Main textEmperor Ōjin asked 大山守命 and 大雀命* which was dearer, an elder child or a younger child, and approved the words of 大雀命, who, grasping the emperor's mind, answered "the younger child." As a result it was decided that 宇遅能和紀郎子* would be the one to inherit the heavenly succession (天津日継), 大山守命 the one to take charge of the government of the mountains and seas, and 大雀命 the one to take charge of the government of the realm (食国).
  2. Main textThe mother of 宇遅能和紀郎子* is 宮主矢河枝比売* (daughter of 比布礼能意富美 of 丸邇*), whom the emperor met at a fork in the road in the village of 木幡*. The next day the emperor was entertained at her house, accepted a cup, and sang a long song.
  3. Main text髪長比売*, who had been summoned from Hyūga, was given by the emperor to 大雀命*, because 大雀命, who had been charmed by her at the port of Naniwa (難波津), made a request through Takeuchi-no-Sukune (建内宿禰). At the 豊明* banquet the emperor had 髪長比売 hold the oak-leaf cup (柏) of great sake and hand it over.
  4. Main textThe text records a song by the 国主 of Yoshino* praising 大雀命's sword, and a song they sing while presenting sake brewed in a 横臼* mortar and drumming with their mouths (口鼓); the latter is said to be the song the 国主 still sing whenever they present great tribute offerings (大贄).
  5. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki (Book 10), the exchange about the elder child and the younger child takes place in the 1st month of the 40th year of Emperor Ōjin, and on the 甲子 day of the same month 菟道稚郎子* is made successor, 大山守命 is entrusted with the mountains, rivers, forests and fields, and 大鷦鷯尊* with assisting the crown prince. The Kojiki gives no year and places it directly after the emperor's genealogy.
  6. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki, the emperor himself notices the love of 大鷦鷯尊* and gives him 髪長媛*, and Takeuchi-no-Sukune's mediation is not written. The story of meeting 宮主矢河枝比売 at the village of 木幡 could not be confirmed in Book 10; only the fact that 宮主宅媛* became a consort and bore 菟道稚郎子 and others appears.

The story

品陀和気命* (Emperor Ōjin) ruled the realm from the 明宮 of 軽嶋*. He took many wives, beginning with three princesses, daughters of 品陀真若王: 高木之入日売命, 中日売命 and 弟日売命*. The text records that his children numbered twenty-six in all (eleven princes and fifteen princesses), and that among them 大雀命* ruled the realm.

The emperor wished to have 宇遅能和紀郎子* rule the realm, and asked 大山守命 and 大雀命*, "Which is dearer, an elder child or a younger child?" 大山守命 answered the elder child. 大雀命, perceiving the emperor's mind, answered, "The elder child is already grown, so there is no need to worry about him, but the younger child is not yet grown, so he is dearer." The emperor said, "The words of 佐邪岐阿芸* are just as I think," and ordered 大山守命 to administer the government of the mountains and seas and 大雀命 to administer the government of the realm (食国) and report to the emperor, and decided that 宇遅能和紀郎子 would inherit the heavenly succession (天津日継). 大雀命 did not go against the emperor's command.

Once, when the emperor went out to the province of Ōmi (近淡海国), he stood on the 宇遅野 plain, looked out toward 葛野*, and sang a song. On reaching the village of 木幡*, he met a beautiful girl at a fork in the road. When he asked her name, she answered that she was 宮主矢河枝比売*, daughter of 比布礼能意富美 of 丸邇*, so the emperor told her, "Tomorrow, on my way back, I will stop at your house." Her father, knowing he was the emperor and in awe, decorated the house and waited. At the reception the next day, when 矢河枝比売 offered a cup of great sake, the emperor sang a long song while having her hold the cup.

The child born of this union was 宇遅能和紀郎子*. The emperor heard of 髪長比売*, daughter of the 諸県君* of the province of Hyūga, whose looks were beautiful, and sent a messenger to summon her. The crown prince 大雀命* saw 髪長比売 when she arrived at the port of Naniwa (難波津), was moved by her appearance, and asked the great minister Takeuchi-no-Sukune (建内宿禰大臣) to request that the emperor grant her to him. On the day of the 豊明* banquet the emperor had 髪長比売 hold the oak-leaf cup (柏) of great sake, gave her to the crown prince, and sang a song.

The emperor sang one more song and gave 髪長比売* to him. After receiving 髪長比売, the crown prince also sang two songs.

Also, the 国主 of Yoshino (吉野之國主*), seeing the sword 大雀命* was wearing, sang a song. They made a 横臼* mortar above the 白檮 oaks of Yoshino, and when they brewed great sake in that mortar and presented it, they sang a song while drumming with their mouths (口鼓) and performing gestures. The text records that this song is the one the 国主 have always sung, down to the present, every time they present great tribute offerings (大贄).

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

兄子者。既成人。是無悒。弟子者。未成人。是愛

The elder child is already grown, so there is no need to worry. The younger child is not yet grown, so he is dear (the answer of 大雀命*).

宇遲能和紀郎子。所知天津日繼也

宇遅能和紀郎子* shall inherit the heavenly succession (天津日継) (one of the divisions of roles the emperor decided).

於髮長比賣令握大御酒柏。賜其太子

He had 髪長比売* hold the oak-leaf cup (柏) of great sake and gave her to the crown prince (大雀命).

國主等。獻大贄之時時。恒至于今。詠之歌者也

It is the song the 国主 have always sung, down to the present, every time they present great tribute offerings (大贄).

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 10 — the 40th year of Emperor Ōjin, spring, 1st month (辛丑朔戊申)

In the Nihon Shoki, the emperor asks 大山守命 and 大鷦鷯尊*, "Do you hold your children dear?" and then asks which, the elder or the younger. The emperor is displeased by 大山守命's answer and delighted by 大鷦鷯尊's answer. It differs from the Kojiki in that the year and month are recorded.

汝等者愛子耶
Nihon Shoki, Book 10 — the 13th year of Emperor Ōjin, autumn, 9th month

In the Nihon Shoki, the emperor, who had heard of the reputation of 髪長媛* in the 11th year, sent a messenger to summon her in the 3rd month of the 13th year, and she arrived in the 9th month and was lodged at 桑津邑*. The emperor learns of 大鷦鷯尊's love and, at a banquet in the inner palace, points to 髪長媛 and sings a song, giving her to him.

於是天皇知大鷦鷯尊感髪長媛而欲配
Nihon Shoki, Book 10 — the 19th year of Emperor Ōjin, winter, 10th month (戊戌朔)

In the Nihon Shoki, when the emperor went to the Yoshino palace (吉野宮), the 国樔* people presented sweet sake (醴酒) and sang, and when they finished singing they struck their mouths, looked up and laughed; it records that even now the 国樔 do the same on the day they present local products (土毛). This corresponds to the "吉野之国主等" (the 国主 of Yoshino) of the Kojiki.
