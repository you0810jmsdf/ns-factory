Kojiki, Part 2 · story 5 of 17

From Emperor Suizei to Emperor Kaika

綏靖天皇から開化天皇まで

For the eight reigns from Emperor Suizei to Emperor Kaika, the Kojiki records chiefly the names of the palaces, the wives and children, which lineages each was the ancestor of, and the ages and tombs. Among these are accounts that connect to later stories, such as the pacification of Kibi, the birth of Takeuchi-no-Sukune (建内宿禰), and the genealogy running from the descendants of 日子坐王* to 息長帶比賣命*.

Read in the Nihon Shoki: From Emperor Suizei to Emperor Kaika (Including the Rebellion of 手研耳命) ›

Key points to remember

  1. Main textThe accounts of these eight reigns center on genealogy, recording palaces, wives and children, the ancestors of lineages, ages and tombs, and there is hardly any narrative. Among them, the section on Emperor Kōrei records that 大吉備津日子命 (Ōkibitsuhiko-no-Mikoto) and 若建吉備津日子命* pacified the land of Kibi, using Harima (針間) as the entrance.
  2. Main textThe section on Emperor Kōgen records that Takeuchi-no-Sukune (建内宿禰) was born as the son of 比古布都押之信命*, and that his nine children became the ancestors of many lineages, such as the 波多臣, the 許勢臣, the Soga no Omi (蘇我臣), the 平群臣 and the 木臣 (readings unconfirmed).
  3. Main textThe section on Emperor Kaika records a long genealogy of the descendants of 日子坐王*, from 沙本毘古王 (Sahobiko; 王 reading unconfirmed) and 沙本毘賣命* down to the later 息長帶比賣命*, and people who appear in later stories of Part 2 are introduced here.
  4. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki, 彦太忍信命 * (比古布都押之信命) is said to be Takeuchi-no-Sukune's “grandfather,” a generation removed from the Kojiki's “father.” Also, Emperor Suizei's age is given as eighty-four, which differs from the Kojiki's forty-five.
  5. CommentaryThe Kokugakuin University database of deity names introduces a view that takes the marriage of 日子坐王* to the daughter of Ame-no-Mikage-no-Kami (天之御影神), the deity of Mount 三上* in Ōmi (近江), as showing a connection with the Ōmi region. [source, Japanese]

The story

神沼河耳命 * (Emperor Suizei) ruled the realm from the 高岡宮* in Katsuragi (葛城), and took as his wife 河俣毘賣*, ancestor of the 師木県主*, and 師木津日子玉手見命* was born. His age was forty-five, and his tomb is at 衝田岡*. 師木津日子玉手見命 (Emperor Annei) ruled from the 浮穴宮* at 片鹽*; he had three children with 阿久斗比賣*, and 大倭日子鉏友命* succeeded him. His age was forty-nine, and his tomb is at 美富登* on Mount Unebi (畝火山).

大倭日子鉏友命 * (Emperor Itoku) ruled from the 境岡宮* at 輕*; his age was forty-five, and his tomb is above 眞名子谷* on Mount Unebi. 御眞津日子訶惠志泥命 * (Emperor Kōshō) ruled from the 掖上宮* in Katsuragi, and his elder brother 天押帶日子命* is regarded as the ancestor of many lineages, such as the 春日臣, the 小野臣 and the 柿本臣 (readings unconfirmed). His age was ninety-three, and his tomb is on top of 博多山* at 掖上. 大倭帶日子國押人命 * (Emperor Kōan) ruled from the 秋津嶋宮* at 室* in Katsuragi and took as his wife his niece 忍鹿比賣命*; his age was one hundred and twenty-three, and his tomb is on top of 玉手岡*.

大倭根子日子賦斗邇命 * (Emperor Kōrei) ruled from the 廬戸宮* at 黒田*, and had eight children. Among them were 夜麻登登母母曾毘賣命*, 大吉備津日子命 (Ōkibitsuhiko-no-Mikoto; also called 比古伊佐勢理毘古命)* and Wakahiko-Takekibitsuhiko-no-Mikoto (若日子建吉備津日子命). The two, 大吉備津日子命 and 若建吉備津日子命*, set sacred jars (忌瓮) at the 氷河* cape in Harima (針間), and, using Harima as the entrance, subdued the land of Kibi by words and pacified it. His age was one hundred and six, and his tomb is on top of 馬坂* at 片岡*.

大倭根子日子國玖琉命 * (Emperor Kōgen) ruled from the 堺原宮* at 輕*; his children included Ōbiko-no-Mikoto (大毘古命), 若倭根子日子大毘毘命*, 比古布都押之信命* and 建波邇夜須毘古命*. Takenunakawawake-no-Mikoto (建沼河別命), son of Ōbiko-no-Mikoto, is regarded as the ancestor of the Abe no Omi (阿倍臣) and others. 比古布都押之信命 took as his wife 山下影日賣*, younger sister of 宇豆比古*, ancestor of the provincial chiefs of 木国 (木国造)*, and the child born was Takeuchi-no-Sukune (建内宿禰). The nine children of Takeuchi-no-Sukune are regarded as the ancestors of the 波多臣, the 許勢臣, the Soga no Omi (蘇我臣), the 平群臣, the 木臣 and the 玉手臣 of Katsuragi (readings unconfirmed), among others. His age was fifty-seven, and his tomb is on top of 中岡* at 劔池*.

若倭根子日子大毘毘命 * (Emperor Kaika) ruled from the 伊邪河宮* at Kasuga (春日), and took as his wife his father's consort (庶母) 伊迦賀色許賣命*, and 御眞木入日子印惠命* was born; this child succeeded him. 日子坐王*, his child by another wife, 意祁都比賣命*, had eleven children, among them 沙本毘古王 (Sahobiko; 王 reading unconfirmed) and 沙本毘賣命*, who became the empress of 伊久米天皇 * (Emperor Suinin). 日子坐王 also took as his wife 息長水依比賣*, daughter of Ame-no-Mikage-no-Kami, who is worshipped by the priest (祝) of 御上* in 近淡海 (Ōmi). Further, from 山代之大筒木眞若王*, whom 日子坐王 fathered by taking as his wife his mother's younger sister 袁祁都比賣命*, the genealogy continues through 迦邇米雷王 and 息長宿禰王 (readings unconfirmed), and 息長帶比賣命* is born as a child of 息長宿禰王. His age was sixty-three, and his tomb is on top of the slope of 伊邪河*.

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

神沼河耳命。坐葛城高岡宮。治天下也。

神沼河耳命 * (Emperor Suizei) dwelt at the 高岡宮* in Katsuragi and ruled the realm. Each of the following reigns begins with the same opening formula.

針間爲道口以言向和吉備國也。

Using Harima as the entrance, they subdued the land of Kibi by words and pacified it.

又娶木國造之祖。宇豆比古之妹。山下影日賣。生子建内宿禰。

比古布都押之信命* took 山下影日賣* as his wife, and Takeuchi-no-Sukune was born.

生子。息長帶比賣命。次虚空津比賣命。

As children of 息長宿禰王*, 息長帶比賣命* and others were born.

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 4, Emperor Kōgen, 7th year, spring, 2nd month (丙寅朔丁卯)

In the Nihon Shoki, 彦太忍信命* is said to be the grandfather of Takeuchi-no-Sukune (武内宿禰). In the Kojiki, Takeuchi-no-Sukune (建内宿禰) is the son of 比古布都押之信命*.

彦太忍信命。是武内宿禰之祖父也。
Nihon Shoki, Book 4, Emperor Suizei, 33rd year, summer, 5th month

In the Nihon Shoki, Emperor Suizei falls ill in summer, 5th month, of his 33rd year and dies at the age of eighty-four. In the Kojiki his age is given as forty-five.

時年八十四
Nihon Shoki, Book 5, Emperor Sujin, 10th year, 9th month (丙戌朔甲午)

In the Nihon Shoki, there is no story of the pacification of Kibi in the record of Emperor Kōrei, and it is in the 9th month of the 10th year of Emperor Sujin that 吉備津彦* is sent to the western circuit (西道). In the Kojiki, in the section on Emperor Kōrei, 大吉備津日子命 (Ōkibitsuhiko-no-Mikoto) and 若建吉備津日子命* pacify the land of Kibi.
