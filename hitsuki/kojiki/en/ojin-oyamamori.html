The Age of Emperor Ōjin and the Rebellion of 大山守命*

応神天皇の時代と大山守命の反乱

In the reign of Emperor Ōjin, groups such as the 海部 and 山部* were established, and from Paekche came horses, a sword and a great mirror, 和邇吉師* bearing the Analects (論語) and the Thousand Character Classic (千字文), and people skilled in smithing, weaving and sake-brewing. After the emperor's death, 大山守命* tried to kill his younger brother 宇遅能和紀郎子* and seize the realm, but he fell into a trap and sank in the river; later 宇遅能和紀郎子 also died young, and 大雀命* ruled the realm.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textWhen Emperor Ōjin died, 大山守命* defied his father's command, aimed at the realm, and tried to kill his younger brother 宇遅能和紀郎子*. 宇遅能和紀郎子, who had prepared after being warned by 大雀命*, disguised himself as the boatman at the steering oar, tipped the boat, and made 大山守命 fall into the river and sink.
  2. Main text大雀命 and 宇遅能和紀郎子* each yielded the realm to the other, and meanwhile the fishermen (海人) carrying the great tribute offerings (大贄) wept, worn out from going back and forth. Because 宇遅能和紀郎子 died young, 大雀命* came to rule the realm.
  3. Main textThe text records that from Paekche came 阿知吉師* with horses and 和邇吉師* with ten scrolls of the Analects (論語) and one scroll of the Thousand Character Classic (千字文), as well as the smith 卓素, the 呉服 西素*, the ancestors of the 秦造 and the 漢直*, and 須須許理*, who brewed sake.
  4. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki (Book 10), sea people (海人) and mountain wardens (山守部) were established in the provinces in the 8th month of the 5th year; in the 9th month of the 7th year Takeuchi-no-Sukune (武内宿禰) led the 韓人 who had come to court in making a pond, which was named the 韓人池*; and in the 10th month of the 11th year the 剣池, 軽池, 鹿垣池 and 廐坂池* ponds were made. In the Kojiki, the pond made using people of Silla is the Paekche Pond (百済池).
  5. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki, in the 8th month of the 15th year the king of Paekche had 阿直岐* present two fine horses, and on 阿直岐's recommendation 王仁* came in the 2nd month of the 16th year and became the teacher of the crown prince 菟道稚郎子*. The titles of the Analects and the Thousand Character Classic found in the Kojiki do not appear in this Nihon Shoki entry.
  6. Compared with the Nihon ShokiFor the rebellion of 大山守命 and the story of 大雀命 and 宇遅能和紀郎子* yielding the realm to each other, no corresponding entry could be confirmed in the book of Emperor Ōjin (Book 10). Book 10 ends with the emperor's death in the 2nd month of the 41st year and the entries of that month.

The story

In this reign the groups 海部, 山部, 山守部 and 伊勢部* were established, and the 剣池* pond was made. Because people of Silla came over, Takeuchi-no-Sukune-no-Mikoto (建内宿禰命) led them, assigned them to the work of building embankments and ponds, and made the Paekche Pond (百済池). 照古王*, ruler of Paekche, presented one stallion and one mare, sending them with 阿知吉師*, and also presented a sword and a great mirror. When the emperor ordered Paekche, "If there is a wise man, present him," a man named 和邇吉師* came bearing ten scrolls of the Analects (論語) and one scroll of the Thousand Character Classic (千字文), eleven scrolls in all (a note says 和邇吉師 is the ancestor of the 文首* and others).

In addition there came the craftsman 卓素*, a 韓鍛 smith; the 呉服 西素, two people*; the ancestor of the 秦造; the ancestor of the 漢直*; and 仁番 (also named 須須許理)*, who knew how to brew sake. The emperor enjoyed the great sake brewed by 須須許理 and sang a song. When he went out just as he was, the emperor struck a large stone on the 大坂* road with his staff, and the stone ran away, and so the proverb "Even a hard stone avoids a drunkard" arose.

After the emperor died, 大雀命* yielded the realm to 宇遅能和紀郎子* in accordance with the emperor's command. But 大山守命* defied the emperor's command and, wishing to take the realm, secretly prepared troops intending to kill his younger brother. When 大雀命 heard of this, he sent a messenger to inform 宇遅能和紀郎子. 宇遅能和紀郎子 hid troops by the river, put up a fence and curtains on the hilltop, seated an attendant (舎人) disguised as the prince on a seat (呉床), and had officials come and go respectfully, arranging it to look like the place where the prince was.

宇遅能和紀郎子* decorated the boat his elder brother would use to cross the river, and smeared the juice obtained by pounding the roots of 佐那葛* on the 簀椅* inside the boat so that anyone stepping on it would fall. He himself put on a cloth robe and trousers (褌), took on the appearance of a lowly person, took the steering oar and stood in the boat. 大山守命, who had come to the riverbank wearing armor under his clothes, assumed that the one sitting in the decorated place was his younger brother and, not realizing that the man at the oar was his brother, asked, "I hear there is an angry great boar on this mountain. I wish to take that boar; will I be able to?" The man at the oar answered, "You will not be able to take it, because it has been tried many times and it could not be taken," and tipped the boat in the middle of the river, so 大山守命 fell into the water and, floating and being carried along, sang a song.

The troops hidden by the river rose up all at once from both banks and aimed their arrows at him, so 大山守命* was carried along and sank at the cape of 訶和羅*. When they searched where he had sunk with hooks, the hooks caught on the armor inside his clothes and it rang "訶和羅," so that place is called 訶和羅前. When the bones were pulled up, his younger brother, the prince, sang a song. The bones of 大山守命 were buried on 那良山*, and 大山守命 is regarded as the ancestor of the 土形君, 弊岐君 and 榛原君* and others.

After that, 大雀命* and 宇遅能和紀郎子* each yielded the realm to the other. Meanwhile, when fishermen (海人) came to present the great tribute offerings (大贄), the elder brother declined and had them present them to the younger, and the younger declined and had them present them to the elder, so that, as many days passed, the fishermen wept, worn out from going back and forth. So arose the proverb "The fishermen weep because of their own goods." However, 宇遅能和紀郎子 died young, so 大雀命 ruled the realm.

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

論語十卷。千字文一卷。并十一卷

Ten scrolls of the Analects (論語) and one scroll of the Thousand Character Classic (千字文), eleven scrolls in all (the books brought by 和邇吉師*).

故諺曰堅石避醉人也

So there is the proverb, "Even a hard stone avoids a drunkard."

其王子者。服布衣褌。既爲賎人之形。執楫立船

That prince (宇遅能和紀郎子*) put on a cloth robe and trousers (褌), took on the appearance of a lowly person, took the steering oar and stood in the boat.

故諺曰。海人乎。因己物而泣也

So there is the proverb, "The fishermen weep because of their own goods."

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 10 — the 5th year of Emperor Ōjin, autumn, 8th month (庚寅朔壬寅)

In the Nihon Shoki, sea people (海人) and mountain wardens (山守部) were established in the provinces. The Kojiki lists four: the 海部, 山部, 山守部 and 伊勢部*.

令諸國定海人及山守部
Nihon Shoki, Book 10 — the 7th year of Emperor Ōjin, autumn, 9th month

In the Nihon Shoki, Takeuchi-no-Sukune (武内宿禰) led the 韓人 who had come to court in making a pond, and named it the 韓人池*. In the Kojiki, the pond that Takeuchi-no-Sukune-no-Mikoto made using people of Silla is the Paekche Pond (百済池).

時命武内宿禰，領諸韓人等作池，因以名池號韓人池
Nihon Shoki, Book 10 — the 15th year of Emperor Ōjin, autumn, 8th month (壬戌朔丁卯)

In the Nihon Shoki, the king of Paekche had 阿直岐* present two fine horses, which were kept in the stable at 軽坂上*. Because 阿直岐 could read the classics, he became the teacher of the crown prince 菟道稚郎子*, and he further recommended 王仁* as an excellent scholar. In the Kojiki, the one who brought the horses is 阿知吉師*, and the seeking of a capable man is done at the emperor's command.
