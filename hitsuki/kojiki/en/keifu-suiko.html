Kojiki, Part 3 · story 13 of 13

From Emperor Buretsu to Empress Suiko

武烈天皇から推古天皇まで

The ten reigns from Emperor Buretsu to Empress Suiko consist mainly of genealogical entries listing palaces, consorts, children, years of reign and tombs. The narrative entries are limited to two: after the death of Emperor Buretsu, who had no heir, 袁本杼命*, a fifth-generation descendant of Emperor 品太 (品太天皇)*, was brought from 近淡海国*; and in the reign of Emperor Keitai, 竺紫君石井* was subdued.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textEmperor Buretsu (小長谷若雀命)* had no crown prince, and after his death 袁本杼命 * (Emperor Keitai), a fifth-generation descendant of Emperor 品太*, was brought from 近淡海国*, married to 手白髪命*, and given the realm.
  2. Main textIn the reign of Emperor Keitai, because 竺紫君石井* did not obey the emperor’s commands and was often disrespectful, 物部荒甲之大連 and 大伴之金村連 (readings unconfirmed) were sent and killed 石井.
  3. Main textOf the twenty-five princes and princesses born to Emperor Kinmei, four ruled the realm: 沼名倉太玉敷命 (Bidatsu), 橘之豊日命 (Yōmei), 豊御気炊屋比売命 (Suiko) and 長谷部之若雀命 (Sushun) (readings unconfirmed).
  4. Main textIt is recorded that 上宮之厩戸豊聡耳命* was born to Emperor Yōmei and his half-sister 間人穴太部王*.
  5. Main textThe last entry of the Kojiki is Empress Suiko (豊御食炊屋比売命)*; it records that she ruled the realm for thirty-seven years from the 小治田宮 palace*, and that her tomb, at first on top of 大野岡 hill*, was later moved to the great tomb at 科長*.
  6. Compared with the Nihon ShokiEmperor Keitai’s age at death differs greatly: forty-three in the Kojiki and eighty-two in the Nihon Shoki.

The story

小長谷若雀命 * (Emperor Buretsu) ruled the realm for eight years from the 列木宮 palace at 長谷 (readings unconfirmed). Because he had no crown prince, he established the 小長谷部* as a group in place of a child (御子代). His tomb is at 石坏岡 hill at 片岡 (readings unconfirmed). When the emperor died there was no prince to take up the succession (日継), so 袁本杼命*, a fifth-generation descendant of Emperor 品太 (品太天皇)*, was brought up from 近淡海国*, married to 手白髪命*, and given the realm.

袁本杼命 * (Emperor Keitai) ruled the realm from the 玉穂宮 palace at 伊波礼 (readings unconfirmed). He married 若比売*, ancestor of the 三尾君* and others; 目子郎女*, younger sister of 凡連*, ancestor of the 尾張連* and others; and 手白髪命*, child of Emperor 意富祁 (意富祁天皇)*, as chief consort, among others; his children numbered nineteen in all (seven sons and twelve daughters). Of these, 天国押波流岐広庭命, 広国押建金日命 and 建小広国押楯命 (readings unconfirmed) ruled the realm, and 佐佐宜王* worshipped at the Ise Shrine. In this reign, because 竺紫君石井* did not obey the emperor’s commands and was often disrespectful, the two men 物部荒甲之大連 and 大伴之金村連 (readings unconfirmed) were sent and killed 石井. The emperor lived to the age of forty-three, and his tomb is the 藍 tomb at 三嶋 (readings unconfirmed).

広国押建金日命 * (Emperor Ankan) ruled the realm from the 金箸宮 palace at 勾 (readings unconfirmed); he had no children, and his tomb is at 高屋村 in 古市, Kawachi (readings unconfirmed). 建小広国押楯命 * (Emperor Senka) ruled the realm from the 廬入野宮 palace at 檜埛 (readings unconfirmed); he married 橘之中比売命*, child of Emperor 意祁 (意祁天皇)*, and 石比売命, 小石比売命 and 倉之若江王 (readings unconfirmed) were born; and from 川内之若子比売*, 火穂王 and 恵波王 (readings unconfirmed) were born.

Emperor 天国押波流岐広庭 (天国押波流岐広庭天皇; reading unconfirmed; Emperor Kinmei) ruled the realm from the 師木嶋大宮 palace*. He married 石比売命*, child of Emperor Senka (in the original text, “檜脾天皇之御子”); her younger sister 小石比売命*; 糠子郎女*, daughter of 日爪臣 of Kasuga (春日)*; 岐多斯比売*, daughter of the great minister 宗賀之稲目宿禰 (宗賀之稲目宿禰大臣)*; and her aunt 小兄比売*; his children numbered twenty-five in all. Of these, four ruled the realm: 沼名倉太玉敷命, 橘之豊日命, 豊御気炊屋比売命 and 長谷部之若雀命 (readings unconfirmed).

沼名倉太玉敷命 * (Emperor Bidatsu) ruled the realm for fourteen years from the 他田宮 palace*. He married his half-sister 豊御食炊屋比売命*; 小熊子郎女*, daughter of 伊勢大鹿首*; 比呂比売命*, daughter of 息長真手王*; and 老女子郎女*, daughter of 春日中若子*; his children numbered seventeen in all. It is recorded that, of these, the child born to Crown Prince 日子人 (日子人太子)* by his half-sister 田村王 * (also called 糠代比売命) was the emperor who ruled the realm from the 岡本宮 palace*. His tomb is at 科長 in 川内 (readings unconfirmed).

橘豊日命 * (Emperor Yōmei) ruled the realm for three years from the 池辺宮 palace*; he married 意富芸多志比売*, daughter of the great minister 稲目宿禰*, and his half-sister 間人穴太部王*, among others, and from 間人穴太部王 were born 上宮之厩戸豊聡耳命* and others. His tomb was at 掖上 in 石寸 (readings unconfirmed) and was later moved to the middle tomb at 科長*. Emperor 長谷部若雀 (長谷部若雀天皇; reading unconfirmed; Emperor Sushun) ruled the realm for four years from the 倉椅柴垣宮 palace*, and his tomb is on top of 倉椅岡 hill*. 豊御食炊屋比売命 * (Empress Suiko) ruled the realm for thirty-seven years from the 小治田宮 palace*; her tomb was on top of 大野岡 hill* and was later moved to the great tomb at 科長*.

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

品太天皇五世之孫。袁本杼命。自近淡海國。令上坐而。

袁本杼命*, a fifth-generation descendant of Emperor 品太*, was brought up from 近淡海国*.

竺紫君石井。不從天皇之命而。多无禮。

竺紫君石井* did not obey the emperor’s commands and behaved disrespectfully in many ways.

生御子。上宮之厩戶豐聰耳命。

上宮之厩戸豊聡耳命* was born to Emperor Yōmei and 間人穴太部王*.

坐小治田宮。治天下參拾漆歲。

Empress Suiko ruled the realm for thirty-seven years from the 小治田宮 palace*.

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 17, the 25th year of Emperor Keitai, spring, 2nd month (丁未)

The Kojiki gives Emperor Keitai’s age as forty-three, but the Nihon Shoki says that he died at the 磐余玉穂宮 palace* in the 25th year (the year 辛亥), at the age of eighty-two, and was buried in the 藍野 tomb*. The Nihon Shoki also notes that “a certain book” (或本) gives a view that he died in the 28th year (甲寅).

天皇崩于磐余玉穗宮。時年八十二。
Nihon Shoki, Book 17, the 22nd year of Emperor Keitai, winter, 11th month (甲寅朔甲子)

The Kojiki says that the two men 物部荒甲之大連 and 大伴之金村連 (readings unconfirmed) killed 石井*, but in the Nihon Shoki the great general 物部大連麁鹿火* fights 磐井* in 御井郡 district in Tsukushi (筑紫)* and beheads him, and it also records that his son 筑紫君葛子* offered the 糟屋屯倉 granary* to escape the death penalty.

大將軍物部大連麁鹿火、親與賊帥磐井、交戰於筑紫御井郡。
Nihon Shoki, Book 22, the 36th year of Empress Suiko, autumn, 9th month (己巳朔戊子)

The Kojiki places Empress Suiko’s tomb on top of 大野岡 hill* (later moved to the great tomb at 科長), but in the Nihon Shoki the empress left instructions that she was not to be buried lavishly but in the tomb of Prince 竹田 (竹田皇子)*, and in the 9th month of the 36th year she was buried in Prince 竹田’s tomb accordingly.

便宜葬于竹田皇子之陵。
