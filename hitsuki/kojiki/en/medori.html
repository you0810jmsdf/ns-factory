八田若郎女, 女鳥王 and 速総別王 (readings unconfirmed)

八田若郎女と女鳥王・速総別王

Using his younger brother 速総別王* as a go-between, the emperor sought 女鳥王*, but she refused, giving the strength of the chief consort as her reason, and became the wife of 速総別王. On hearing a song by 女鳥王, the emperor raised an army, and the two were killed at 蘇邇* in Uda. The general 山部大楯連*, who had taken 女鳥王’s jewelled bracelet, was also put to death by the chief consort.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textUsing his younger brother 速総別王* as a go-between, the emperor sought 女鳥王*, but she refused, saying, “Because the chief consort is so strong, the emperor has not even taken 八田若郎女* as his wife,” and she became the wife of 速総別王. 速総別王 gave the emperor no reply.
  2. Main textWhen the emperor visited 女鳥王, she was weaving at her loom. Learning her feelings through an exchange of songs, the emperor went back for the time being, but when he heard the song 女鳥王 sang when 速総別王 came to her, he immediately raised an army.
  3. Main text速総別王 and 女鳥王 climbed Mount 倉椅山* and fled further, but the emperor’s army caught up with them at 蘇邇* in Uda, and they were killed.
  4. Main textThe general 山部大楯連* took the jewelled bracelet (玉釧) from 女鳥王’s arm and gave it to his wife. At a banquet (豊楽), the chief consort 石之日売命* noticed it, and she had 大楯連 put to death for the crime of stripping his lord’s bracelet from her while her skin was still warm.
  5. Main textThe emperor and 八田若郎女* exchanged songs, and the 八田部* was established as a namesake group (御名代) for 八田若郎女.
  6. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the Nihon Shoki, this story is placed in the 2nd month of the 40th year of Emperor Nintoku, and the names are written 雌鳥皇女 and 隼別皇子 (readings unconfirmed). The two were killed at 蒋代野* in Ise while trying to reach the Ise Shrine. The person who took the jewel was the pursuer 佐伯直阿俄能胡*, who escaped the death penalty by offering up his own land (that land is called 玉代). In the Nihon Shoki, the empress at this time is 八田皇女*, who had been made empress in the 38th year.

The story

The emperor sent a song to 八田若郎女*, and 八田若郎女 also answered with a song. He then established the 八田部* as a namesake group (御名代) for 八田若郎女.

The emperor also used his younger brother 速総別王* as a go-between to seek his half-sister 女鳥王* as a wife. But 女鳥王 told 速総別王, “Because the chief consort is so strong, the emperor has not even properly taken 八田若郎女 as his wife. So I do not intend to serve him. I will be your wife,” and the two were married. For this reason 速総別王 gave the emperor no reply.

The emperor went in person to where 女鳥王 lived and sat on the threshold of the palace door. 女鳥王 was at her loom, weaving cloth. When the emperor sang a song, 女鳥王 answered with a song, and in her song the name 速総別 (波夜夫佐和気) was sung. Knowing her feelings, the emperor returned to his palace.

Later, when her husband 速総別王 came, 女鳥王 sang a song. When the emperor heard this song, he immediately raised an army to kill the two. 速総別王 and 女鳥王 fled together and climbed Mount 倉椅山*, and 速総別王 sang two songs. The two fled further from there, but when they reached 蘇邇* in Uda, the emperor’s army caught up with them and killed them.

The general at that time, 山部大楯連*, took the jewelled bracelet (玉釧) that 女鳥王 wore on her arm and gave it to his own wife. Later a banquet (豊楽) was held, and when the women of all the clans came to the palace, 大楯連’s wife came wearing that bracelet on her arm. The chief consort 石之日売命* was herself taking the oak leaves (柏) of great sake and handing them to the women, but when she noticed the bracelet, she did not give a leaf to the wife and sent her away. The chief consort summoned 大楯連 and said, “Those royals were set aside because they were disrespectful, and that was only right. But you, a mere retainer, stripped the bracelet from your lord’s arm while her skin was still warm and gave it to your own wife.” She then had him put to death.

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

因大后之強。不治賜八田若郎女

Because the chief consort is strong, (the emperor) has not even taken 八田若郎女* as his wife (the words of 女鳥王).

天皇聞此歌。即興軍欲殺

When the emperor heard this song, he immediately raised an army to kill them.

到宇陀之蘇邇時。御軍追到而殺也

When they reached 蘇邇* in Uda, the emperor’s army caught up with them and killed them.

所纒己君之御手玉釧。於膚温剥持來

You stripped off the bracelet worn on your lord’s arm while her skin was still warm and brought it away (the words of the chief consort).

Deities and people, and the shrines that enshrine them

  • 大雀命*Emperor Nintoku. He sought 女鳥王* but was refused, and he raised an army to pursue the two.
  • 八田若郎女*A wife who exchanged songs with the emperor. The 八田部* was established as her namesake group (御名代).
  • 速總別王*The emperor’s younger brother. He was entrusted with acting as go-between to 女鳥王*, but became her husband himself.
  • 女鳥王*The emperor’s half-sister. She refused the emperor, became the wife of 速総別王*, and was killed together with him.
  • 山部大楯連*The general sent in pursuit. He took 女鳥王’s jewelled bracelet and gave it to his wife, and was put to death by the chief consort.
  • 石之日賣命*The chief consort. At a banquet she noticed the jewelled bracelet and had 大楯連 put to death.
  • 玉釧*The jewelled bracelet that 女鳥王* wore on her arm. It was the means by which 大楯連’s crime became known.
  • 倉椅山*The mountain the two fugitives climbed. 速総別王 sang of this mountain in his songs.
  • 八田部*The group (部) established as the namesake group (御名代) of 八田若郎女*.

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 11, the 40th year of Emperor Nintoku, spring, 2nd month

In the Nihon Shoki, 隼別皇子*, resting his head on the lap of 雌鳥皇女*, asked, “Which is faster, the wren (鷦鷯) or the falcon (隼)?”, and on hearing “The falcon is faster,” said, “So I come first,” and this deepened the emperor’s resentment. This exchange is not in the Kojiki.

孰捷鷦鷯與隼焉
Nihon Shoki, Book 11, the 40th year of Emperor Nintoku, spring, 2nd month

In the Nihon Shoki, the two fled intending to go to the Ise Shrine, and the pursuers followed them from Mount 素珥山 in 菟田 (readings unconfirmed) and killed them at 蒋代野* in Ise. The Kojiki says they were killed at 蘇邇* in Uda.

欲納伊勢神宮而馳
Nihon Shoki, Book 11, the 40th year of Emperor Nintoku, this year

In the Nihon Shoki, it is the empress who, at the 新嘗 harvest banquet, notices a jewel resembling the princess’s, and the person who took the jewel is 佐伯直阿俄能胡*. 阿俄能胡 was pardoned from the death penalty by offering up his own land, and that land was called “玉代”. In the Kojiki, 山部大楯連* is put to death.
