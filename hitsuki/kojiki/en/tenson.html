The Descent of the Heavenly Grandson

天孫降臨

Once the Central Land of the Reed Plains had been pacified, Ninigi-no-Mikoto, son of Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto, went down in his father's place. Guided by Sarutahiko-no-Kami and accompanied by the deities of the five attendant groups (五伴緒), and having received the curved jewels, the mirror and the sword, he descended to Takachiho in Hyūga in Tsukushi. Afterwards Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto escorted Sarutahiko-no-Kami home, took on his name, and became the origin of the Sarume no Kimi (猿女君).

Read in the Nihon Shoki: The Pacification of the Central Land of Reed Plains and the Descent of the Heavenly Grandson (Section 9) ›

Key points to remember

  1. Main textAmaterasu-Ōmikami and 高木神* at first commanded Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto to go down, but while he was making ready his son Ninigi-no-Mikoto was born, so at Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto's request it was decided that Ninigi-no-Mikoto would go down in his place.
  2. Main textAt the Eightfold Crossroads of Heaven (天八衢), there was a deity shining on both Takamanohara and the Central Land of the Reed Plains. When Ame-no-Uzume-no-Kami questioned him, he gave his name as Sarutahiko-no-Kami, an earthly deity, and answered that he had come to meet the Heavenly Grandson in order to guide him.
  3. Main textNinigi-no-Mikoto was accompanied by the five attendant groups (五伴緒): Ame-no-Koyane-no-Mikoto, Futodama-no-Mikoto (布刀玉命), Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto, Ishikoridome-no-Mikoto (伊斯許理度売命) and Tamanooya-no-Mikoto. He received the eight-shaku curved jewels (八尺勾玉), the mirror and the Kusanagi sword (草那芸剣). Amaterasu-Ōmikami commanded that the mirror be worshipped as her own spirit, just as one would worship her.
  4. Main textNinigi-no-Mikoto pushed through the clouds, passed by way of the Floating Bridge of Heaven, and descended to 久士布流多気* at Takachiho in Hyūga in Tsukushi. Ame-no-Oshihi-no-Mikoto and Amatsukume-no-Mikoto led the way in arms, and Ninigi-no-Mikoto praised this land as a good land “where the morning sun shines straight and the evening sun shines bright,” and built his palace there.
  5. Main textAme-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto escorted Sarutahiko-no-Kami home and took on his name, becoming the origin of the Sarume no Kimi. Also, because she slit with a small knife the mouth of the sea cucumber, which alone did not answer that it would serve the Heavenly Grandson, the Kojiki records that the sea cucumber's mouth is split to this day.
  6. Compared with the Nihon ShokiIn the main text of the Nihon Shoki, section 9, Takamimusubi-no-Mikoto (高皇産霊尊) covers the imperial grandson with a bed-covering (真床追衾) and sends him down; neither the story of the bestowal of the curved jewels, mirror and sword nor Sarutahiko appears. The three treasures, the deities of the five groups (五部神) and Sarutahiko's coming to meet him appear in variant account 1.

The story

Having received the report of pacification, Amaterasu-Ōmikami and 高木神* commanded the crown prince Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto to go down to the earth and rule it. Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto answered, “While I was making ready to go down, a child was born. This child should be sent down.” This child was the younger of the two born to Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto and Yorozuhata-toyoakitsushi-hime-no-Mikoto (万幡豊秋津師比売命), daughter of 高木神; the elder was Ame-no-Hoakari-no-Mikoto (天火明命). Thus the command to rule the Land of Abundant Reed Plains and Fresh Rice Ears was given to 日子番能邇邇芸命 (Ninigi-no-Mikoto).

When Ninigi-no-Mikoto was about to come down from heaven, at the Eightfold Crossroads of Heaven (天八衢; a place where roads divide) there was a deity who shone above on Takamanohara and below on the Central Land of the Reed Plains. Amaterasu-Ōmikami and 高木神* commanded Ame-no-Uzume-no-Kami: “Though you are a delicate woman, you are a deity who wins a staring contest against any deity you face, so go and question him.” That deity answered, “I am an earthly deity, and my name is Sarutahiko-no-Kami. I heard that the child of the heavenly deities was coming down, so I have come to meet him, to serve as his guide.”

The five attendant groups (五伴緒), Ame-no-Koyane-no-Mikoto, Futodama-no-Mikoto (布刀玉命), Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto, Ishikoridome-no-Mikoto (伊斯許理度売命) and Tamanooya-no-Mikoto, were assigned to Ninigi-no-Mikoto. In addition, the eight-shaku curved jewels (八尺勾玉), the mirror and the Kusanagi sword (草那芸剣) were added, along with Tokoyo-no-Omoikane-no-Kami (常世思金神), Tajikarao-no-Kami (手力男神) and Ame-no-Iwatowake-no-Kami (天石門別神). Amaterasu-Ōmikami commanded, “Worship this mirror as my spirit, just as you would worship me. Omoikane-no-Kami, take charge of the affairs of the worship and carry them out.” It is recorded that these two deities were worshipped at “佐久久斯侶，伊須受能宫”. Toyouke-no-Kami (登由宇気神) is regarded as the deity dwelling at 度相* of the Outer Shrine, Ame-no-Iwatowake-no-Kami (天石戸別神) as a deity of the gates, and Tajikarao-no-Kami as the deity dwelling at 佐那那県*; the five attendant groups are regarded as the ancestors of the Nakatomi no Muraji (中臣連), the 忌部首*, the Sarume no Kimi (猿女君), the 作鏡連* and the Tamanooya no Muraji (玉祖連), respectively.

Ninigi-no-Mikoto left the heavenly rock seat (天石位), pushed apart the many-layered clouds, passed by way of the Floating Bridge of Heaven, and descended to 久士布流多気* at Takachiho in Hyūga in Tsukushi. Ame-no-Oshihi-no-Mikoto and Amatsukume-no-Mikoto, carrying stone quivers on their backs, wearing mallet-headed swords (頭椎大刀) and holding bows and arrows, went at the front and served him. The two are regarded as the ancestors of the Ōtomo no Muraji (大伴連) and the Kume no Atai (久米直), respectively. Ninigi-no-Mikoto said that this land faces 韓国*, leads straight to the cape of 笠紗*, and is a land where the morning sun shines straight and the evening sun shines bright, and so it is a very good land; and he dwelt in a palace with thick pillars set on the bedrock deep in the earth and crossed roof-beams (氷椽) soaring high enough to reach Takamanohara.

Ninigi-no-Mikoto commanded Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto: “You who revealed the identity of Sarutahiko-no-Ōkami, who guided me, shall escort him home, take on that deity's name and serve.” It is said that this is why the Sarume no Kimi bear the name of the male deity Sarutahiko and their women are called Sarume no Kimi. While Sarutahiko-no-Kami was at 阿耶訶*, he was fishing when his hand was caught by a 比良夫 shellfish*, and he sank into the sea and drowned. His name when he sank to the bottom is Sokodoku-mitama (底度久御魂), his name when the sea water rose in bubbles is Tsubutatsu-mitama (都夫多都御魂), and his name when the foam burst is Awasaku-mitama (阿和佐久御魂).

Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto, returning after escorting Sarutahiko-no-Kami, gathered all the fish, large and small, and asked, “Will you serve the child of the heavenly deities?” All the fish answered, “We will serve,” but only the sea cucumber did not answer. Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto said, “Is this a mouth that does not answer?” and slit the sea cucumber's mouth with a small knife, and so the sea cucumber's mouth is split to this day. For this reason, it is said, whenever the “swift offerings of the islands” (嶋之速贄) are presented, generation after generation, a share is also given to the Sarume no Kimi.

A plain retelling of what the original text of the Kojiki says (no published translation has been used).

In the original text

僕者國神，名猿田毗古神也

I am an earthly deity, and my name is Sarutahiko-no-Kami (his answer when questioned at the Eightfold Crossroads of Heaven).

此之鏡者，專為我御魂而如拜吾前

(Worship) this mirror wholly as my spirit, just as you would worship me.

朝日之直刺國，夕日之日照國也

It is a land where the morning sun shines straight, a land where the evening sun shines bright (Ninigi-no-Mikoto's words praising the land to which he descended).

此口乎，不答之口

Is this a mouth that does not answer? (the words of Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto to the sea cucumber).

In the Nihon Shoki

Nihon Shoki, Book 2, section 9, main text

In the main text of section 9, Takamimusubi-no-Mikoto (高皇産霊尊) covers the imperial grandson 天津火瓊瓊杵尊 (Ninigi) with a bed-covering (真床追衾) and sends him down. The story of the change from Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto (天忍穂耳尊) to his son, the story of the bestowal of the jewels, mirror and sword, and Sarutahiko do not appear.

以眞床追衾、覆於皇孫天津火瓊瓊杵尊使降之
Nihon Shoki, Book 2, section 9, variant account 1

In variant account 1 of section 9, Amaterasu-Ōmikami (天照大神) gives the imperial grandson the “three treasures,” the Yasakani curved jewels (八坂瓊曲玉), the Yata mirror (八咫鏡) and the Kusanagi sword (草薙剣), together with the deities of the five groups (五部神), and Sarutahiko-no-Ōkami (猿田彦大神) comes out to meet him at the Eightfold Crossroads of Heaven (天八達之衢). Sarutahiko says that he will go to the upper reaches of the Isuzu River (五十鈴川) at 狭長田* in Ise.

八坂瓊曲玉及八咫鏡、草薙劒三種寶物
Nihon Shoki, Book 2, section 9, variant account 4

In variant account 4 of section 9, Ame-no-Oshihi-no-Mikoto, the distant ancestor of the Ōtomo no Muraji, leads 天槵津大来目*, the distant ancestor of the Kume-be (来目部), and goes ahead as guide. In the Kojiki, the two, Ame-no-Oshihi-no-Mikoto and Amatsukume-no-Mikoto, lead the way side by side.

大伴連遠祖天忍日命。帥來目部遠祖天槵津大來目
