Nihon Shoki, Book 12 — Emperor Richū and Emperor Hanzei · page 26 of 50

Emperor Richū, Emperor Hanzei and the revolt of 住吉仲皇子*

履中天皇・反正天皇と住吉仲皇子の反乱

Before his accession, Emperor 去來穗別 (去來穗別天皇; Richū)* had his palace burned by his younger brother 住吉仲皇子* and fled to Yamato, and another younger brother, 瑞齒別皇子*, had 仲皇子 killed and put an end to the revolt. After the accession, the establishment of provincial recorders (国史) and the creation of the Treasury Office (蔵職), among other things, are recorded at the 磐余稚櫻宮 palace*; the following ruler, Emperor 瑞齒別 (瑞齒別天皇; Hanzei)*, reigned for five years at the 柴籬宮 palace* in 丹比*, Kawachi.

Read in the Kojiki: Emperor Richū, Emperor Hanzei and the Revolt of 墨江中王* ›

Key points to remember

  1. Main textAfter the death of Emperor 大鷦鷯 (大鷦鷯天皇)*, when the Crown Prince was about to take 黒媛* as his consort, 住吉仲皇子*, sent as messenger, used the Crown Prince's name to sleep with 黒媛. When this was discovered from the hand bell he left behind, he raised troops, surrounded the Crown Prince's palace and burned it. The Crown Prince, helped by 平群木菟宿禰, 物部大前宿禰 and 阿知使主 (readings unconfirmed), escaped on horseback, and woke up at the 埴生坂 slope* in Kawachi to see the fire at Naniwa.
  2. Main textThe Crown Prince, having entered the 石上振神宮 shrine*, suspected even his younger brother 瑞齒別皇子* and ordered him to go back to Naniwa, kill 仲皇子*, and only then come to him. 瑞齒別皇子 had 刺領巾*, a Hayato attendant of 仲皇子, stab 仲皇子 in the privy, but 木菟宿禰*, saying that one who had killed his own lord could not be allowed to live, killed 刺領巾.
  3. Main textEmperor 瑞齒別 (瑞齒別天皇)* was born at the 淡路宮 palace*, and his teeth were even, like a single bone. It is said that when he was washed with water from the 瑞井 well*, a 多遲 flower (the flower of the knotweed, 虎杖) fell into the well, so he was called 多遲比瑞齒別*. It is recorded that after his accession, at the 柴籬宮 palace* in 丹比*, Kawachi, wind and rain came in due season and the realm was at peace.
  4. Main textIn the 8th month of the 4th year, provincial recorders (国史) were for the first time placed in the provinces, to record words and events and report on conditions in all directions. In the 1st month of the 6th year, the Treasury Office (蔵職) was established and the treasury workers (蔵部) were appointed.
  5. Main textIn the 5th year, the three deities who dwell in Tsukushi appeared in the palace and complained that their people had been taken from them, and on Awaji Izanagi-no-Mikoto (伊奘諾神) declared that he could not bear the smell of blood from the tattoos (黥) of the horse-keepers (飼部). Then the imperial consort died, and the Emperor, regretting that he had not appeased the deities' curse, had the 車持君*, who had taken the 車持部* assigned to the deities, perform purification (祓禊), and dedicated the 車持部 to the three deities.
  6. Compared with the KojikiIn the Kojiki, the one who raises the revolt is 墨江中王*, and the one who carries the Emperor out is 阿知直* alone; the circumstances concerning 黒媛* do not appear. The Hayato who killed 仲皇子 is named 曾婆訶理*, and 水齒別命* holds a banquet to grant him the rank of minister and then cuts off his head, which differs from the Nihon Shoki, where the punishment is carried out by 木菟宿禰*.

What the main text says

Emperor 去來穗別 (去來穗別天皇)* was the Crown Prince of Emperor 大鷦鷯 (大鷦鷯天皇)*, and his mother was 磐之媛命*. He became Crown Prince at the age of fifteen in the 31st year of Emperor 大鷦鷯, and his father died in the 87th year. Before his accession, wishing to take 黒媛*, daughter of 羽田矢代宿禰*, as his consort, he sent his younger brother 住吉仲皇子* as messenger. 仲皇子 used the Crown Prince's name to sleep with 黒媛, and went home leaving his hand bell behind. When the Crown Prince noticed this the next night, he quietly withdrew, but 仲皇子, fearing trouble, surrounded the Crown Prince's palace with troops. Three men, 平群木菟宿禰, 物部大前宿禰 and 阿知使主 (readings unconfirmed), put the Crown Prince on a horse and got him away, and 仲皇子 burned the palace, in which the Crown Prince was no longer present.

The Crown Prince woke at the 埴生坂 slope* in Kawachi, was startled to see the fire at Naniwa, and headed for Yamato by way of 大坂*. At the foot of Asuka hill (飛鳥山) a girl he met told him that the mountain was full of soldiers and that he should go round by the 當摩 road*, and the Crown Prince put this into a song. After crossing the 龍田山 mountain*, he captured with an ambush the fishermen of 野嶋* in Awaji who were pursuing him; they were men sent by 阿曇連濱子* on behalf of 仲皇子*. 倭直吾子篭*, who had sided with 仲皇子 and blocked the road, feared that the Crown Prince would kill him, and was pardoned after presenting his younger sister 日之媛*; the text suggests that the practice of the 倭直* offering court ladies (采女) may have begun at this time. The Crown Prince entered the 石上振神宮 shrine*.

When his younger brother 瑞齒別皇子* followed him, the Crown Prince suspected him and would not meet him, sending word that if he truly had no divided loyalty he should go back to Naniwa and kill 仲皇子*. 瑞齒別皇子, lamenting that he was doing away with one who had no principles in order to follow one who did, went to Naniwa with 木菟宿禰* and gave 刺領巾*, a Hayato attendant of 仲皇子, a brocade robe and trousers to get him to kill 仲皇子. 刺領巾 stabbed 仲皇子 with a spear when he went into the privy, but 木菟宿禰, saying that one who had killed his own lord could not be allowed to live, killed 刺領巾. When 瑞齒別皇子 returned to 石上* at midnight and reported, the Crown Prince treated his brother generously and granted him the 村合屯倉 granary*; on this day 阿曇連濱子* was arrested.

In the 2nd month of the first year, the Crown Prince ascended the throne at the 磐余稚櫻宮 palace*. In the 4th month, the death sentence of 阿曇連濱子*, who had plotted with 仲皇子*, was reduced to the punishment of tattooing, and people called this the 阿曇目 ("阿曇 eye")*. In the 7th month 黒媛*, daughter of 葦田宿禰*, was made imperial consort, and 磐坂市邊押羽皇子, 御馬皇子 and 青海皇女 (readings unconfirmed) were born; the second consort, 幡梭皇女*, bore 中磯皇女*. In the 1st month of the 2nd year, 瑞齒別皇子* was made heir apparent (儲君), and in the 10th month the capital was placed at 磐余*, and 平群木菟宿禰, 蘇賀滿智宿禰, the Ōmuraji (大連) of the Mononobe (物部) and 圓大使主 (readings unconfirmed) together took charge of the government of the country. In the 11th month of the 3rd year, during a boating party on the 磐余市磯池 pond*, cherry blossoms out of season fell into the Emperor's cup; because the blossoms were found and brought back from 掖上室山*, the palace was named the 稚櫻宮 ("young cherry palace")*.

In the 8th month of the 4th year, provincial recorders (国史) were for the first time placed in the provinces, and in the 10th month the 石上溝 canal* was dug. In the 3rd month of the 5th year, the three deities who dwell in Tsukushi appeared in the palace and complained that their people had been taken, but the Emperor only prayed and did not perform rites for them. In the 9th month, when he hunted on Awaji, Izanagi-no-Mikoto (伊奘諾神) declared that he could not bear the smell of blood from the tattoos of the horse-keepers (飼部), so from then on tattooing of the horse-keepers was stopped. Immediately afterward a voice like the wind was heard from the sky, and a messenger reported the death of the imperial consort. The Emperor regretted not having dealt with the deities' curse, charged the 車持君* with his offense, and dedicated the 車持部* to the three deities. In the 1st month of the 6th year, 草香幡梭皇女* was made Empress and the Treasury Office (蔵職) was established, but in the 3rd month the Emperor fell ill and died at the 稚櫻宮 palace* (at the age of seventy), and in the 10th month he was buried in the 百舌鳥耳原陵 tomb*.

Emperor 瑞齒別 (瑞齒別天皇)* was the full younger brother of Emperor 去來穗別 (去來穗別天皇)* and became Crown Prince in the 2nd year of Emperor 去來穗別. He was born at the 淡路宮 palace*; from birth his teeth were like a single bone and he was handsome in appearance, and it is said that when he was washed with water from the 瑞井 well*, a 多遲 flower* fell into the well, so he was called 多遲比瑞齒別*. He ascended the throne in the 1st month of the first year. In the 8th month he made 津野媛*, daughter of 木事*, ancestor of the 大宅臣*, his imperial wife (皇夫人), and 香火姫皇女 and 圓皇女 (readings unconfirmed) were born; her younger sister 弟媛* bore 財皇女 and 高部皇子 (readings unconfirmed). In the 10th month he placed the capital at 丹比* in Kawachi, calling it the 柴籬宮 palace*; wind and rain came in due season, the five grains ripened, and the realm was at peace. In the 1st month of the 5th year, the Emperor died in the main bedchamber.

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

One version (一云)

The main text says that the Crown Prince did not believe the warning of the three men, but this "one version says" (一云) relates that the Crown Prince was drunk and did not get up.

One version (一云)

The main text says that the three men helped the Crown Prince and put him on a horse, but this "one version says" (一云) states that 物部大前宿禰* took the Crown Prince in his arms and mounted the horse.

One version (一云)

The main text names the master of the fishermen of 野嶋* who were made to pursue the Crown Prince as 阿曇連濱子*, but this "one version says" (一云) names him 阿曇連黒友*.

One source says (一日)

A note giving another name, 飯豐皇女*, for 青海皇女*, the daughter borne by 黒媛*.

In the original text

始之於諸國置國史。記言事達四方志。

For the first time provincial recorders were placed in the provinces, to record words and events and report on conditions in all directions.

然亡無道就有道。其誰疑我。

The words of 瑞齒別皇子*: since I am destroying one without principles and following one who has them, who would doubt me?

因此時人曰阿曇目。

(Because 濱子* was tattooed,) people of the time called this the 阿曇目*.

風雨順時五穀成熟。人民富饒。天下太平。

In the reign of Emperor Hanzei, wind and rain came in due season, the five grains ripened, the people were prosperous, and the realm was at peace.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 3, beginning of the section on Emperor Richū (G50)

In the Kojiki, the younger brother 墨江中王* sets fire to the great hall while the Emperor is lying drunk after the banquet (豊明) of the Daijō (大嘗) festival at the Naniwa palace, and the circumstances concerning 黒媛* do not appear. The one who carries the Emperor out is 阿知直*, and the place where he wakes up is given as 多遲比野*.

爾其弟墨江中王。欲取天皇以。火著大殿。
Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Richū, 水齒別命* and the Hayato 曾婆訶理* (G56)

In the Kojiki, the Hayato who kills 墨江中王*, the counterpart of 仲皇子*, is named 曾婆訶理*. 水齒別命*, to reward his deed, holds a banquet to grant him the rank of minister, makes him drink sake from the same cup, and cuts off his head when his face is hidden by it. In the Nihon Shoki it is 木菟宿禰* who kills the Hayato 刺領巾*, and the banquet story could not be confirmed there.

今日與大臣。飮同盞酒。
Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Hanzei (G57)

The Kojiki gives Emperor Hanzei's height as nine shaku two and a half sun (九尺二寸半), and records the length and breadth of his teeth, saying they were like strung jewels. The Nihon Shoki says his teeth were like a single bone and records the origin of his name from the 瑞井 well* and the 多遲 flower*, but does not give his height.

御齒長一寸廣二分。上下等齊。既如貫珠。
