Nihon Shoki, Book 3 — Emperor Jinmu · page 10 of 50

Emperor Jinmu's Eastern Expedition

神武天皇の東征

This page covers the period from the year 甲寅, when the emperor 神日本磐余彦 (Emperor Jinmu) left Hyūga and advanced east through the Seto Inland Sea, until he passed through Naniwa, Kumano, and 菟田* and entered Yamato. Though he lost his elder brother Itsuse-no-Mikoto in the fighting against Nagasunebiko, he received the help of the sword Futsunomitama (韴靈), the 頭八咫烏* crow, and a golden kite, and the account runs up to the submission of Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto in the twelfth month of the year 戊午.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textIn the year 甲寅, the emperor, aged forty-five, stated that since the descent of the heavenly ancestor they had governed this western region, but that distant lands had not yet received the blessings of royal rule and each village had its own lord or chief contending with the others, and he decided to establish his capital in the “beautiful land in the east” of which he had heard from Shiotsuchi-no-Oji (鹽土老翁).
  2. Main textWhen he was defeated by Nagasunebiko at 孔舍衞坂* and Itsuse-no-Mikoto was wounded by an arrow, the emperor, thinking that “for a descendant of the Sun Deity to attack facing the sun is to go against the way of heaven,” withdrew and changed his plan to attacking with the sun at his back. Itsuse-no-Mikoto died at 竃山* in the province of Kii.
  3. Main textThe army, which had collapsed in Kumano from the poisonous breath of a deity, was revived by the sword “Futsunomitama” (韴靈), which 高倉下* obtained from his storehouse after a revelation in a dream, and it then entered 菟田* guided by the 頭八咫烏* crow, which Amaterasu-Ōmikami announced in a dream that she would send.
  4. Main textIn the twelfth month of the year 戊午, a golden, miraculous kite perched on the emperor's bow and dazzled the eyes of Nagasunebiko's army. Nagasunebiko served Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto as his lord, but he did not stop fighting even after the heavenly feathered arrows (天羽羽矢) and quivers (歩靭) had been shown to each other, and Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto killed Nagasunebiko and submitted (he is regarded as the distant ancestor of the Mononobe clan).
  5. Compared with the KojikiThe Kojiki holds that he set out east after consulting with Itsuse-no-Mikoto at the Takachiho palace, and that he stayed eight years at the 高嶋宮* palace in Kibi, whereas the Nihon Shoki holds that the emperor himself proposed the eastern expedition, and that he spent three years at the 高嶋宮 palace in Kibi preparing ships and provisions.
  6. CommentaryAccording to the deity-name database of Kokugakuin University, the “速吸之門”* where he met 椎根津彦 * (槁根津日子 in the Kojiki) is identified with the Akashi Strait if one follows the Kojiki, and with the Hōyo Strait if one follows the Nihon Shoki, so that its location is regarded as differing between the two works. [source, Japanese]

What the main text says

The emperor 神日本磐余彦 (Emperor Jinmu) had the personal name Hikohohodemi (彦火火出見); he was the fourth child of Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto (彦波瀲武鸕鷀草葺不合尊), and his mother was Tamayori-hime (玉依姫), the youngest daughter of the sea deity (海童). At fifteen he became crown prince, and he took as consort 吾平津媛* of the village of 吾田* in the province of Hyūga, and 手研耳命* was born. When he was forty-five, the emperor told his elder brothers and his children that since the heavenly ancestor Ninigi-no-Mikoto (彦火瓊瓊杵尊) had descended, more than 1,792,470 years had passed, but in distant lands each village had its own lords and chiefs who contended with one another. He said that he had heard from Shiotsuchi-no-Oji that in the east there was a beautiful land surrounded by green mountains, and that someone had flown down there in a heavenly rock boat (天磐船); that land, he said, was the center of the realm, and the one who flew down was probably Nigihayahi; and he proposed that they go there and establish the capital. The princes also agreed. This year was 甲寅.

In winter, in the tenth month, the emperor led the princes and a naval force east. At 速吸之門*, a fisherman named 珍彦*, who came in a small boat, declared himself an earthly deity and undertook to act as guide, so the emperor had him grasp the tip of a 椎 pole, drew him into the emperor's ship, and gave him the name 椎根津彦* (first ancestor of the 倭直部)*. At 菟狹* in Tsukushi, 菟狹津彦 and 菟狹津媛 (readings unconfirmed) built a one-pillared raised palace (一柱騰宮) and entertained them, and 菟狹津媛 was given as wife to the attendant 天種子命 * (distant ancestor of the Nakatomi clan). In the eleventh month they reached 崗水門* in Tsukushi and in the twelfth month the 埃宮* palace in Aki; in the third month of the year 乙卯 they moved to Kibi and spent three years at the 高嶋宮* palace preparing ships and provisions. In the second month of the year 戊午 they arrived at the cape of Naniwa, which they named 浪速国 (the land of swift waves) because the current of the tide was swift, and in the third month they reached the port of 青雲白肩之津 at the village of 草香 (readings unconfirmed) in the province of Kawachi.

In the fourth month, since the road to 龍田* was narrow, they tried to cross Mount 膽駒山* to enter the central land (中洲), but Nagasunebiko, declaring that “the children of the heavenly deities are coming in order to seize my land,” met them in battle at 孔舍衞坂*, and a stray arrow struck Itsuse-no-Mikoto. The emperor, thinking that for a descendant of the Sun Deity to attack facing the sun was to go against the way of heaven, withdrew for a time, worshipped the deities of heaven and earth, and decided to attack with the might of the Sun Deity at his back. Because they set up shields and raised war cries (雄誥) at the port of 草香津*, that port was named 盾津 (the Shield Port). In the fifth month, at 山城水門 in 茅渟 (readings unconfirmed), Itsuse-no-Mikoto, in pain from his wound, stroked his sword and raised a war cry, and that place was called 雄水門*. When the army advanced to 竃山* in the province of Kii, Itsuse-no-Mikoto died, and he was buried at 竃山.

In the sixth month they struck down 名草戸畔* at the village of 名草邑*, crossed 狹野*, and arrived at the village of 神邑* in Kumano. When they met a violent storm at sea, 稻飯命* drew his sword, entered the sea, and became 鋤持神*, and Mikeirino-no-Mikoto (三毛入野命) trod on the crests of the waves and departed for the Eternal Land (常世郷). When they struck down 丹敷戸畔* at 熊野荒坂津*, a deity breathed out poisonous vapor and the whole army grew weak and collapsed. 高倉下* of Kumano dreamed that Takemikazuchi-no-Kami (武甕雷神), commanded by Amaterasu-Ōmikami (天照太神), would send down in his stead the sword with which he had pacified the land, and when he presented the sword that stood at the bottom of his storehouse, the emperor and his soldiers woke up. Further, Amaterasu-Ōmikami announced in a dream that she would send the 頭八咫烏*; guided by that crow, 日臣命*, leading 大來目*, opened a mountain road and arrived at 菟田下縣*, so the emperor changed 日臣命's name to 道臣*.

In the eighth month, 兄猾* of 菟田 built a contraption in a new palace intending to kill the emperor, but 弟猾* reported it, and 兄猾, driven into it by 道臣命*, was crushed to death by his own contraption (the origin of the name 菟田血原)*. At the banquet held by 弟猾 the emperor composed a song, and this is called the 來目歌*. At Yoshino he met earthly deities, among them 井光 * (first ancestor of the 吉野首部)*, who came out of a well, shining and with a tail. In the ninth month, looking out from 高倉山* in 菟田, he saw that the 八十梟帥 (the Eighty Bandits) of 国見丘* and the army of 兄磯城* of the village of 磐余邑* were blocking the strategic points. Following the instruction of a heavenly deity in a dream and the advice of 弟猾, he had 椎根津彦 and 弟猾 disguise themselves as an old man and an old woman and fetch earth from the summit of Mount Ame-no-Kagoyama (天香山); with that clay he made eighty flat dishes (八十平瓮) and sacred jars (嚴瓮), worshipped the deities of heaven and earth at the upper reaches of the 丹生川*, and obtained the results of divination in which sweet syrup (飴) formed of itself and fish floated up because of a jar sunk in the river. The emperor himself performed the manifest worship (顯齋) of Takamimusubi-no-Mikoto (高皇産靈尊), making 道臣命 the ritual master and giving him the name 嚴媛*.

In the tenth month, the emperor defeated the Eighty Bandits of 国見丘*, and at 忍坂* 道臣命 prepared a banquet in a great chamber (大室), lured the remaining enemies, and at a signal given by a song cut them all down at once. In the eleventh month, when the 頭八咫烏 was sent as messenger, 兄磯城 shot arrows at it, but 弟磯城* was afraid and submitted, and reported his elder brother's preparations. On the plan of 椎根津彦, a women's army was sent out from 忍坂 to draw the enemy, while a men's army crossed 墨坂* and caught them in a pincer attack from the rear, cutting down 兄磯城 and the others.

In the twelfth month, in the battle against Nagasunebiko, they were unable to win, but as the sky grew dark and hail fell, a golden, miraculous kite perched on the tip (弭) of the emperor's bow, and its light dazzled the eyes of Nagasunebiko's soldiers so that they could not fight (the origin of the name 鵄邑, now 鳥見, readings unconfirmed). Nagasunebiko sent a messenger to say that Kushitama-Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto (櫛玉饒速日命), who had descended from heaven in a heavenly rock boat, had married his younger sister Mikashikiya-hime (三炊屋媛), and Umashimade-no-Mikoto (可美眞手命) had been born, and that he served Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto as his lord. The emperor and Nagasunebiko showed each other a heavenly feathered arrow and a quiver (歩靭), but Nagasunebiko did not change his mind, so Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto killed Nagasunebiko and submitted, leading Nagasunebiko's people. The emperor praised this and favored him, and it is recorded that Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto is the distant ancestor of the Mononobe clan.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

A certain book says (高尾張邑)

Regarding 「高尾張邑」*, where the 赤銅八十梟帥 (the Eighty Bandits of red copper) are, as mentioned in the words of 弟猾*, a small note says that a certain book (或本) gives it as the village of 葛城*.

In the original text

東有美地。青山四周

In the east there is a beautiful land, surrounded on all sides by green mountains. The emperor tells this as something he heard from Shiotsuchi-no-Oji.

今我是日神子孫。而向日征虜此逆天道也

The emperor's reflection: although he was a descendant of the Sun Deity, attacking the enemy while facing the sun went against the way of heaven.

予釼號曰韴靈

The words of Takemikazuchi-no-Kami telling 高倉下* that the name of his sword is Futsunomitama (韴靈).

乃有金色靈鵄。飛來止于皇弓之弭

A golden, miraculous kite came flying and perched on the tip of the emperor's bow.

  • Itsuse-no-Mikoto (五瀬命)The emperor's elder brother. He was struck by a stray arrow at 孔舍衞坂*, died at 竃山* in the province of Kii, and was buried at 竃山.
  • 珍彦 (椎根津彦)*An earthly deity, a fisherman met at 速吸之門*. He became the guide for the sea route and was given the name 椎根津彦; he also served in fetching the earth of Mount Ame-no-Kagoyama and with his plan for the attack on 磯城. First ancestor of the 倭直部*. A gloss says 「椎。此云辭毘」.
  • Nagasunebiko (長髄彦)The chieftain of Yamato who met the emperor's army in battle at 孔舍衞坂*. He looked up to Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto as his lord, and was finally killed by Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto.

  • Mikashikiya-hime (三炊屋媛)Younger sister of Nagasunebiko and wife of Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto. Her other names are 長髄媛 and 鳥見屋媛 (readings unconfirmed).
  • Umashimade-no-Mikoto (可美眞手命)Child of Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto and Mikashikiya-hime. A gloss says 「可美眞手。此云于魔詩莽耐」.
  • 高倉下*A man of Kumano. Following the instruction of a dream, he found the sword (Futsunomitama) in his storehouse and presented it to the emperor.
  • Futsunomitama (韴靈)The sword with which Takemikazuchi-no-Kami had pacified the land, which he sent down into the storehouse of 高倉下*. A gloss says 「韴靈。此云赴屠能瀰哆磨」.
  • 頭八咫烏*The crow that Amaterasu-Ōmikami announced in a dream she would send. It guided the way from Kumano to 菟田* and also served as messenger to the brothers of 磯城*.
  • 道臣命 (日臣命)*Distant ancestor of the Ōtomo clan. For his merit in following the crow and opening a mountain road he was renamed 道臣; he carried out the punishment of 兄猾 and the stratagem at 忍坂, and became the ritual master of the manifest worship.
  • 兄猾 and 弟猾 (readings unconfirmed)Brothers who were chieftains of the district of 菟田*. 兄猾 plotted and brought about his own destruction, and 弟猾 served the emperor. A gloss says 「猾。此云字介志」.
  • 稻飯命 and Mikeirino-no-Mikoto (三毛入野命)The emperor's elder brothers. In a storm at sea on the way to Kumano, 稻飯命* entered the sea and became 鋤持神*, and Mikeirino-no-Mikoto departed for the Eternal Land.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 2 — beginning of the section on Emperor Jinmu (the departure and route of the eastern expedition)

The Kojiki holds that he set out east after consulting with his elder brother Itsuse-no-Mikoto at the Takachiho palace, and stayed one year at the 岡田宮* palace in Tsukushi, seven years at the 多祁理宮 palace in 阿岐 (readings unconfirmed), and eight years at the 高嶋宮* palace in Kibi. The Nihon Shoki holds that the emperor himself spoke of the eastern expedition to his elder brothers and the princes, and that, passing through the 埃宮* palace in Aki, he stayed three years at the 高嶋宮 palace in Kibi.

於吉備之高嶋宮。八年坐
Kojiki, Part 2 — section on Emperor Jinmu (the death in battle of Itsuse-no-Mikoto)

In the Kojiki, Itsuse-no-Mikoto dies at 男之水門* in 紀国 (Kii), crying out, “Am I to die of a wound from a lowly wretch's hand?” (written 「崩」), and his tomb is said to be at 竈山*. In the Nihon Shoki, 雄水門* is the name of the place at 山城水門 in 茅渟 (readings unconfirmed) where he raised a war cry, and Itsuse-no-Mikoto is said to have died at 竃山 in the province of Kii (written 「薨」).

負賤奴之手乎死
Kojiki, Part 2 — section on Emperor Jinmu (the arrival of Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto)

The Kojiki records only that Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto (邇藝速日命) descended from heaven following the child of the heavenly deities, presented the heavenly tokens (天津瑞), and served him; the story of his killing 登美毘古* does not appear. The Nihon Shoki has the scene in which he and Nagasunebiko show each other heavenly feathered arrows and quivers, and the story of Nigihayahi-no-Mikoto killing Nagasunebiko and submitting.
