Nihon Shoki, Book 15 — Emperor Seinei, Emperor Kenzō and Emperor Ninken · page 30 of 50

Emperors Seinei, Kenzō and Ninken, and the brothers 億計 and 弘計 (readings unconfirmed)

清寧・顕宗・仁賢天皇と億計・弘計の兄弟

In the time of the childless Emperor 白髪 (白髪天皇; Emperor Seinei)*, Prince 億計 (億計王) and Prince 弘計 (弘計王) (readings unconfirmed), sons of Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)*, who had been hiding in Harima, were discovered and welcomed to the palace. The text records that the brothers yielded the throne to each other; the younger, Emperor 弘計 (弘計天皇; Emperor Kenzō), ascended first and buried his father's remains, and after him the elder, Emperor 億計 (億計天皇; Emperor Ninken), ascended the throne and the country was governed in peace.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textIn the 11th month of the 2nd year of Emperor Seinei, 來目部小楯*, governor of Harima (播磨國司), discovered the brothers 億計 and 弘計 (readings unconfirmed), sons of Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)*, at a banquet for the new house of the head of the 縮見 granary (縮見屯倉首)* in 赤石郡*. Emperor 白髪 (白髪天皇)*, who had no children, rejoiced at this, and in the 3rd year welcomed the two to the palace, making Prince 億計 Crown Prince and Prince 弘計 an imperial prince.
  2. Main textAt the banquet for the new house, the younger brother, Prince 弘計 (弘計王)*, after reciting the words and song of house-blessing (室壽), declared that he was a descendant of 押磐尊*, who had ruled the realm from the 市邊宮 palace*. 小楯*, astonished, left his seat and bowed twice, gathered the people of the district and built a temporary palace.
  3. Main textWhen Emperor 白髪 (白髪天皇)* died, the brothers yielded the throne to each other, and meanwhile their elder sister, Princess 飯豐青 (飯豐青皇女)*, conducted the government at the 忍海角刺宮 palace* and was called 忍海飯豐青尊*. The elder brother, Prince 億計 (億計王)*, yielded, saying that it was his younger brother's achievement that they had declared themselves and been brought out into the world, and Prince 弘計 (弘計王)* ascended the throne.
  4. Main textIn the first year of Emperor Kenzō, guided by 置目*, an old woman of Ōmi, the Emperor dug up the remains of his father, Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)*, which had been buried at 蚊屋野*. Because they were mixed with the bones of the retainer 佐伯部仲子*, the skulls were told apart by a feature of 仲子, who was said to have lost an upper tooth, and two tombs of the same form were built for the burials. In the 2nd year the Emperor intended to destroy the tomb of Emperor 大泊瀬 (大泊瀬天皇)*, but gave up the idea on the admonition of his elder brother 億計*.
  5. CommentaryIn the 3rd year of Emperor Kenzō, the Moon Deity (月神) and the Sun Deity (日神) each possessed a person and spoke to 阿閇臣事代*, who had been sent to Mimana, asking that rice fields be offered to their ancestor Takamimusubi-no-Kami (高皇産靈). The Kokugakuin University deity-name database explains that in this entry, where Takamimusubi-no-Kami is presented as the ancestor of the Moon Deity and Sun Deity who has "the merit of having forged heaven and earth", a character of a creator deity accompanied by the imagery of smithing can be seen. [source, Japanese]
  6. Main textEmperor 億計 (億計天皇; Emperor Ninken)* ascended the throne at the 石上廣高宮 palace*, and the Empress, Princess 春日大娘 (春日大娘皇女)*, bore Princess 手白香 (手白香皇女)*, 小泊瀬稚鷦鷯尊* (later Emperor Buretsu), and others. The text records that in the 8th year there were no incidents in the country, the officials fulfilled their duties, and the people were content in their work.

What the main text says

Emperor 白髪武廣國押稚日本根子 (白髪武廣國押稚日本根子天皇)* was the third son of Emperor 大泊瀬 (大泊瀬天皇)*, and had white hair from birth. When his father died in the 8th month of the 23rd year of Emperor Yūryaku, 吉備稚媛* had her young son, Prince 星川 (星川皇子)*, seize the office of the Great Treasury (大藏), and Prince 星川 shut the gates of the Great Treasury and used the government's goods as he pleased. The Ōmuraji 大伴室屋 (大伴室屋大連)*, following the testamentary edict, surrounded the Great Treasury with troops and set fire to it, and Prince 星川, 稚媛, 兄君*, elder half-brother of Prince 磐城 (磐城皇子)* by a different father, and others were burned to death. The 吉備上道臣* and others, who had come with forty ships to save Prince 星川, turned back, but the Emperor reproached them and took away their 山部*. In the 10th month, the Ōmuraji 大伴室屋 and others presented the imperial seals to the Crown Prince.

In the 1st month of the first year, the Emperor ascended the throne at 磐余甕栗* and made his mother, 葛城韓媛*, Imperial Lady Dowager (皇太夫人). In the 10th month, when Emperor 大泊瀬 (大泊瀬天皇)* was buried in the 丹比高鷲原陵 tomb*, the Hayato wept day and night beside the tomb and died on the seventh day. In the 2nd month of the 2nd year, the Emperor, grieving that he had no children, established attendants, stewards and quiver-bearers (舎人, 膳夫, 靭負) of the 白髪部*. In the 11th month, when 來目部小楯*, governor of Harima (播磨國司), discovered the brothers 億計 and 弘計 (readings unconfirmed) in 赤石郡* and reported it, the Emperor rejoiced that heaven had granted him two sons; in the 1st month of the 3rd year he welcomed the two to the palace, and in the 4th month made Prince 億計 Crown Prince and Prince 弘計 an imperial prince. By the 4th year the Emperor had received envoys of the foreign lands (諸蕃) as well as the Emishi and the Hayato; in the 1st month of the 5th year he died in the palace and was buried in the 河内坂門原陵 tomb*.

The pre-accession annals of Emperor 弘計 (弘計天皇)* go back to recount the circumstances of the brothers. When their father, Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)*, was shot dead by Emperor 大泊瀬 (大泊瀬天皇)* in the 10th month of the 3rd year of Emperor Ankō, his retainer 日下部連使主* and his son 吾田彦* fled with the brothers to 余社郡* in Tanba Province. 使主 (Omi), still fearing that he would be killed, entered a stone chamber on 縮見山* in Harima and took his own life. Without knowing what had become of him, the brothers moved to 赤石郡* in Harima Province, changed their names to 丹波小子*, served the head of the 縮見 granary (縮見屯倉首)*, and lived by tending cattle and horses. In the 11th month of the 2nd year of Emperor 白髪 (白髪天皇)*, at the banquet for the new house, the brothers yielded the turn to dance to each other, and Prince 弘計 (弘計王), after reciting the words and song of house-blessing (室壽), declared that he was a descendant of 押磐尊*; 小楯*, astonished, bowed twice and reported this to the capital.

After the death of Emperor 白髪 (白髪天皇)*, the elder brother, Prince 億計 (億計王)*, and the younger, Prince 弘計 (弘計王)*, each yielded the throne to the other, and for a long time nothing was decided. Meanwhile their elder sister, Princess 飯豐青 (飯豐青皇女)*, conducted the government at the 忍海角刺宮 palace*, but she died in the 11th month. In the 12th month, Prince 億計 placed the imperial seals on his younger brother's seat and yielded, stating that their declaring themselves and being welcomed was an achievement owed to his younger brother's plan. Prince 弘計 firmly declined, saying that the elder brother should be set up, but on learning that his brother's will would not change he accepted, though he did not ascend the throne at once. In the 1st month of the first year, on the petition of the Great Ministers (大臣), the Ōmuraji (大連) and others, Emperor 弘計 ascended the throne at the 近飛鳥八釣宮 palace* and made 難波小野王* Empress.

In the 2nd month of the first year, the Emperor searched for his father's remains and, guided by 置目*, an old woman of Ōmi, dug up the place at 蚊屋野* where they had been buried. His father's bones were mixed with those of 佐伯部仲子*, but because the wet nurse of Prince 磐坂 (磐坂皇子)* said that 仲子 had lost an upper tooth, the skulls were told apart, and two tombs of the same form were built for the burials. The Emperor had 置目 live near the palace and attached a bell to a rope as a signal for her comings and goings. In the 4th month he praised the merit of 小楯*, who had discovered the two, made him head of the Mountain Office (山官), and gave him the title 山部連*; in the 5th month he spared 狹狹城山君韓■宿禰*, who had taken part in the killing of his father, from the death penalty and made him a tomb keeper.

In the 8th month of the 2nd year, the Emperor told the Crown Prince 億計* that, as revenge for his father, he wished to destroy the tomb of Emperor 大泊瀬 (大泊瀬天皇)* and scatter his bones. 億計 admonished him, giving two reasons: that Emperor 大泊瀬 was an emperor who had ruled the realm, and that he was the father of Emperor 白髪 (白髪天皇)*, who had treated them generously. The Emperor accepted this and had the work stopped. In the 9th month 置目*, grown old, returned to her home, and for the 10th month it is recorded that the realm was at peace and one 斛 of rice cost one silver coin (銀銭一文). In the 2nd and 4th months of the 3rd year, the Moon Deity (月神) and the Sun Deity (日神) possessed people and asked for rice fields, and people of Iki and of Tsushima, respectively, served at their shrines. In the 4th month the Emperor died at the 八釣宮 palace*. In this year 紀生磐宿禰* styled himself a divine sage (神聖) in Mimana and fought Paekche, but his strength gave out and he returned home.

Emperor 億計 (億計天皇)* (personal name 大脚, courtesy name 嶋郎; readings unconfirmed) was the full elder brother of Emperor 弘計 (弘計天皇)*, and after his younger brother's death he ascended the throne at the 石上廣高宮 palace*. In the 2nd month he made Princess 春日大娘 (春日大娘皇女)* Empress, and she bore one son and six daughters. In the 10th month he buried Emperor 弘計 in the 傍丘磐杯丘陵 tomb*. In the 2nd year, Empress 難波小野 (難波小野皇后)*, fearing because she had once behaved disrespectfully toward the Crown Prince 億計 at a banquet, took her own life. In the 5th year the scattered 佐伯部* were gathered, and the descendants of 仲子* were made the 佐伯造*. In the 6th year 日鷹吉士* was sent to Koguryŏ (高麗) to seek craftsmen, and 飽田女*, wife of 麁寸*, who went along with him, lamented at the 御津 harbor* of Naniwa; her complicated family relationships are explained in a note. In that year 日鷹吉士 returned with the craftsmen 須流枳 and 奴流枳 (readings unconfirmed) and others. In the 7th year 小泊瀬稚鷦鷯尊* was made Crown Prince, and for the 8th year it is recorded that the whole country was at peace and the five grains ripened. In the 8th month of the 11th year the Emperor died in the main bedchamber, and in the 10th month he was buried in the 埴生坂本陵 tomb*.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

One version (一云)

It says that the reason 小楯* had come to 赤石郡* was not to prepare the offerings for the Niiname (新嘗) festival himself, but to tour the districts and counties collecting the rice-field tax (田租).

One text (一本)

In a genealogy listing the children of Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)*, it places Princess 飯豐 (飯豐女王)* before Prince 億計 (億計王)*. In the main text's genealogy, Princess 飯豐 is placed fourth, after Prince 億計 and Prince 弘計 (弘計王)*.

A certain text says (或本云)

It says that Emperor 弘計 (弘計天皇)* had palaces in two places, 少郊 and 池野 (readings unconfirmed), and another text says he had a palace at 甕栗*. The main text says that he ascended the throne at the 近飛鳥八釣宮 palace*.

A certain text says (或本云)

It says that Emperor 億計 (億計天皇)* had palaces in two places, 川村 and 縮見高野 (readings unconfirmed), and that the pillars of those halls have not rotted even now. The main text says that he ascended the throne at the 石上廣高宮 palace*.

One text says (一本云)

It gives the daughter borne by 大糠娘*, daughter of 和珥臣日觸*, as Princess 山田大娘 (山田大娘皇女)* (also called Princess 赤見, 赤見皇女)*. The main text says that 糠君娘*, daughter of 和珥臣日爪*, bore Princess 春日山田 (春日山田皇女)*, and a note says that, though the names differ slightly, they are the same person.

In the original text

於市邊宮治天下天萬國萬押磐尊御裔僕是也

The words with which Prince 弘計 (弘計王)* declared himself at the banquet for the new house, meaning: "I am a descendant of 押磐尊*, who ruled the realm from the 市邊宮 palace*."

仲子者上齒堕落。以斯可別

The words of the wet nurse of Prince 磐坂 (磐坂皇子)*, meaning: "仲子* had lost an upper tooth, so he can be told apart by that."

稻斛銀錢一文。牛馬被野

The entry for the 10th month of the 2nd year of Emperor Kenzō, meaning that one 斛 of rice cost one silver coin and that the land was so prosperous that cattle and horses covered the fields.

我祖高皇産靈有預鎔造天地之功

The words of the Moon Deity, who had possessed a person, meaning: "My ancestor Takamimusubi-no-Kami (高皇産靈) has the merit of having forged heaven and earth."

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Seinei (the discovery of the two princes)

In the Kojiki, the two princes are discovered after Emperor Seinei has died and there is no successor; 小楯*, on hearing their declaration, tumbles off his seat, and 飯豐王*, who is given as the younger sister of 市邊忍齒別王*, rejoices and has the two come up to the palace. In the Nihon Shoki they are discovered during Emperor Seinei's reign, the Emperor himself joyfully welcomes them, and Prince 億計 (億計王)* is made Crown Prince. The Nihon Shoki also makes Princess 飯豐青 (飯豐青皇女)* the elder sister of Emperor 弘計 (弘計天皇)*.

自床墮轉而
Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Kenzō (the old woman 置目 (置目老媼)* and the remains of the prince's father)

In the Kojiki, the old woman 置目 (置目老媼)* comes forward and says that the bones of 市邊王* can be identified by the teeth, and the tomb is built on the mountain east of 蚊屋野*. In the Nihon Shoki, the clue for telling them apart is the missing upper tooth of 佐伯部仲子*, the one who points this out is the wet nurse of Prince 磐坂 (磐坂皇子)*, and two tombs are built.

亦以其御齒可知
Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Kenzō (the tomb of Emperor 大長谷 (大長谷天皇)*)

In the Kojiki, the elder brother 意祁命* goes himself and digs only a little at the side of the tomb of Emperor 大長谷 (大長谷天皇)*, explaining that this is enough to show the disgrace. In the Nihon Shoki, the elder brother 億計* admonishes the Emperor with two reasons, and the Emperor has the work itself stopped.
