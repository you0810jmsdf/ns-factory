Nihon Shoki, Book 2 — Age of the Gods (part 2) · page 9 of 50

The Birth of 神日本磐余尊* (Section 11)

神日本磐余彦尊の誕生（第十一段）

This is the last section of the Age of the Gods. It records that Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto (波瀲武鸕鷀草葺不合尊) took his aunt Tamayori-hime (玉依姫) as consort, fathered four sons, Itsuse-no-Mikoto (五瀨命), 稻飯命*, Mikeirino-no-Mikoto (三毛入野命), and 神日本磐余尊*, and died at the 西洲之宮* palace. The main text is very short, and the four variant accounts transmit differences in the order and names of the four brothers.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textUgayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto took as consort his aunt (his mother's younger sister) Tamayori-hime, and four sons were born: Itsuse-no-Mikoto, 稻飯命*, Mikeirino-no-Mikoto, and 神日本磐余尊*. In the main text, 神日本磐余尊 is the youngest child.
  2. Main textThe main text records that Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto died at the 西洲之宮* palace and was buried in the 吾平山上陵* mausoleum in Hyūga.
  3. Variant accountThe four variant accounts differ slightly in the order of the brothers: 磐余尊* is placed fourth in variant account 1, also fourth in variant account 2 (though the order of 稻飯命 and 三毛野命 is switched), third in variant account 3, and second in variant account 4.
  4. Variant accountVariant account 1 explains that 狹野尊* was his name when young, and that the title 神日本磐余尊 was added because he later pacified the realm and governed the Eight Islands (八洲). In variant accounts 2 to 4, “火火出見” is attached to the name of 磐余尊.
  5. Compared with the KojikiThe Kojiki also lists four children by Tamayori-hime (玉依毘売命): Itsuse-no-Mikoto (五瀬命), 稻冰命, 御毛沼命, and 若御毛沼命 (readings unconfirmed), and gives 神倭伊波礼毗古命* as another name of the youngest, 若御毛沼命. The Kojiki says that 御毛沼命 went into the Eternal Land (常世国) and 稻冰命 into the sea plain, but this does not appear in section 11 of the Nihon Shoki.
  6. CommentaryThe deity-name database of Kokugakuin University draws attention to the element shared by “彦火火出見” in Emperor Jinmu's name and “日子穂々手見,” another name of 火遠理命*, and introduces a theory that regards Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto (鵜葺草葺不合命) as a deity added later in order to make Emperor Jinmu a descendant of Amaterasu-Ōmikami in the fifth generation. In section 11 of the Nihon Shoki as well, “火火出見” is included in the name of 磐余尊 in variant accounts 2 to 4. [source, Japanese]

What the main text says

Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto took as consort Tamayori-hime, who was his aunt (his mother's younger sister). The main text lists in order the children born of this marriage.

First Itsuse-no-Mikoto was born, next 稻飯命*, next Mikeirino-no-Mikoto, and next 神日本磐余尊*; the children were four sons in all.

After a long time, Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto died at the 西洲之宮* palace. He was then buried in the 吾平山上陵* mausoleum in Hyūga.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

Variant account 1

The order is the same as in the main text, but the fourth child is called 狹野尊*, with the other name 神日本磐余尊*. It explains that 狹野 was his name when young, and that because he later pacified the realm and governed the Eight Islands, the title 神日本磐余尊 was added.

Variant account 2

The order is Itsuse-no-Mikoto, 三毛野命*, 稻飯命*, and 磐余尊*, so that the second and third are switched compared with the main text. Another name of 磐余尊 is given as 神日本磐余火火出見尊*.

Variant account 3

The order is Itsuse-no-Mikoto, 稻飯命, 神日本磐余火火出見尊, and 稚三毛野命 (readings unconfirmed), placing 磐余尊 third and making 稚三毛野命 the youngest child.

Variant account 4

The order is Itsuse-no-Mikoto, 磐余火火出見尊*, 稻飯命*, and Mikeirino-no-Mikoto, placing 磐余尊 second.

In the original text

以其姨玉依姫爲妃

He took as consort his aunt (his mother's younger sister) Tamayori-hime.

所稱狹野者。是年少時之號也

Variant account 1: he is called “狹野” because that was his name when young.

亦號神日本磐余火火出見尊

Variant account 2: he is also called 神日本磐余火火出見尊.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, end of Part 1 — the genealogy of Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto

The Kojiki also says that Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto (鵜葺草葺不合命) married his aunt Tamayori-hime (玉依毘売命) and fathered four children, in the order Itsuse-no-Mikoto (五瀬命), 稻冰命, 御毛沼命, and 若御毛沼命 (readings unconfirmed), and, like the main text of the Nihon Shoki, makes 若御毛沼命, who corresponds to Emperor Jinmu, the youngest.

生御子，名五濑命，次，稻冰命。次，御毛沼命。次，若御毛沼命
Kojiki, end of Part 1 — the genealogy of Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto

The Kojiki records that 御毛沼命* crossed over to the Eternal Land (常世国), treading on the crests of the waves, and that 稻冰命* entered the sea plain as the land of his late mother, but this passage is absent from the main text and the variant accounts of section 11 of the Nihon Shoki. On the other hand, the passage in the main text of the Nihon Shoki on the death of Ugayafukiaezu-no-Mikoto (鸕鷀草葺不合尊) and the 吾平山上陵* mausoleum could not be confirmed in the corresponding part of Part 1 of the Kojiki.

御毛沼命者，跳浪穗渡坐于常世国
