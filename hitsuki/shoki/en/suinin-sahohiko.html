Nihon Shoki, Book 6 — Emperor Suinin · page 15 of 50

The Accession of Emperor Suinin, the Rebellion of 狭穂彦王, and 誉津別命 (readings unconfirmed)

垂仁天皇の即位、狭穂彦王の反乱と誉津別命

This page covers the period from the accession of Emperor Suinin, through the rebellion of 狭穂彦王*, elder brother of the empress 狭穂姫*, and his end in the rice-stack fortress, to the time when the prince 誉津別命*, who did not speak, gained the power of speech on seeing a swan (鵠). In between, it also records the beginning of the enmity between Mimana and Silla, the arrival of the Silla prince Ame-no-Hiboko (天日槍), the contest of strength between 野見宿禰 and 当麻蹶速 (readings unconfirmed), and the entry into the palace of five daughters from Tanba.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textIn the 9th month of the 4th year, 狭穂彦王* asked his younger sister the empress, "Which do you love more, your brother or your husband?" and gave her a dagger with which to stab the emperor. In the 10th month of the 5th year, the empress could not bring herself to stab the emperor as he slept with his head on her lap; her tears fell on the emperor's face, and the emperor's dream of "a small brocade-colored snake, and heavy rain from 狭穂*" led her to confess the plot.
  2. Main text狭穂彦王* piled up rice to make a "rice-stack fortress" (稲城) and resisted. The empress entered the fortress holding the prince 誉津別命*, but when it was attacked with fire she sent the prince out of the fortress, asked that the women's quarters of the palace be entrusted to the five daughters of 丹波道主王*, and died in the fortress together with her brother.
  3. Main text誉津別命* did not speak even when he was thirty and his beard had grown long, but in the 10th month of the 23rd year, seeing a swan (鵠) flying across the sky, he said "是何物耶". 天湯河板挙* caught the swan and presented it; the prince played with it and became able to speak, and the title of 鳥取造 and the groups 鳥取部, 鳥養部 and 誉津部 (readings unconfirmed) were established.
  4. Main textIn the 7th month of the 7th year, 野見宿禰*, summoned from Izumo, fought 当麻蹶速* in a contest of strength in which they kicked at each other with their feet, killed 蹶速, and was given his land. This is regarded as the origin of the "腰折田"* of the village of 当麻邑.
  5. CommentaryKokugakuin University's database of deity names (entry: the Great Deity of Izumo, 出雲大神) introduces an argument that, whereas in the Kojiki 本牟智和気* becomes able to speak when the curse of the Great Deity of Izumo is appeased, the Great Deity of Izumo does not appear in the chronicle of Emperor Suinin in the Nihon Shoki, and the swan story places its emphasis on telling of the merit of the 鳥取造* and the origin of the 鳥取部 and similar groups. [source, Japanese]
  6. CommentaryThe belongings of the Silla prince Ame-no-Hiboko (天日槍), who came in the 3rd month of the 3rd year, are in the main text seven kinds, such as 羽太玉, 出石小刀, 日鏡 and 熊神籬 (readings unconfirmed), which were deposited in Tajima; in one account (一云) they are eight kinds, with the addition of 膽狭浅大刀*. The database of deity names (entry: Izushi-Yamae-no-Ōkami, 伊豆志之八前大神) points out that these differ greatly, in both items and character, from the eight treasures of the Kojiki (jewels, scarves (比礼) and mirrors). [source, Japanese]

What the main text says

活目入彦五十狭茅天皇 * (Emperor Suinin) was the third son of 御間城入彦五十瓊殖天皇 * (Emperor Sujin), and his mother was 御間城姫*, daughter of Ōbiko-no-Mikoto. He was born in the 瑞籬宮* palace in the 1st month of the 29th year of Emperor Sujin, and his father loved him for his unaffected character. At twenty-four he became crown prince on the strength of a sign in a dream, and his father died in the 12th month of the 68th year of Emperor Sujin. He acceded to the throne in the 1st month of his 1st year; in the 10th month he buried his father in the 山辺道上陵* tomb, and in the 11th month he made the empress the empress dowager. In the 2nd month of the 2nd year, 狭穂姫* became empress and bore 誉津別命*. The emperor doted on this prince and always kept him at his side, but even when he grew up he did not speak. In the 10th month the capital was moved to 纒向*, and the palace was called 珠城宮*.

In this year 蘇那曷叱智*, a man of Mimana, asked to return to his country, so the emperor gave him generous rewards and had him carry one hundred bolts of red silk for the king of Mimana. But people of Silla seized them on the way, and it is said that the enmity between Mimana and Silla began at this time. In the 3rd month of the 3rd year, the Silla prince Ame-no-Hiboko (天日槍) came, bringing seven items such as jewels, a small sword, a halberd, a mirror and a 熊神籬*; these were deposited in the province of Tajima and made sacred objects.

In the 9th month of the 4th year, 狭穂彦王*, the empress's elder brother by the same mother, plotted to endanger the state. He asked the empress which she loved more, her brother or her husband, and when the empress answered her brother, he argued that favor that relies on beauty does not last long, and gave her a dagger, telling her to kill the emperor in his sleep. The empress trembled with fear but could not admonish her brother either, and kept the dagger hidden in her clothing.

In the 10th month of the 5th year, the emperor took a midday nap at the 高宮* palace in 来目*, with his head on the empress's lap. Thinking of her brother's plan, the empress shed tears, which fell on the emperor's face. When the emperor woke and said he had dreamed that a small brocade-colored snake wound around his neck and heavy rain came from 狭穂* and wet his face, the empress could no longer hide it and confessed the plot, and the emperor said it was not the empress's crime. The emperor had 八綱田* attack 狭穂彦*, but 狭穂彦 shut himself up in a rice-stack fortress (稲城) made of piled rice, and the empress, too, entered the fortress holding 誉津別命*. When 八綱田 set fire to the fortress, the empress sent the prince out of the fortress, asked that the five daughters of 丹波道主王* be taken into the women's quarters of the palace, and died in the fortress together with her brother. The emperor praised 八綱田's achievement and gave him the name 倭日向武日向彦八綱田*.

In the 7th month of the 7th year, because 当麻蹶速* of the village of 当麻邑 was boasting everywhere that no one was stronger than he, the emperor summoned 野見宿禰* of Izumo and had them compete in strength. The two raised their feet and kicked at each other, and 野見宿禰 stamped on 蹶速, broke his ribs and his back, and killed him. 蹶速's land was given to 野見宿禰, who then remained and served at the court.

In the 15th year, the five daughters from Tanba entered the women's quarters of the palace, and in the 8th month 日葉酢媛命* became empress. Only the youngest, 竹野媛*, was sent back to her province on account of her looks; ashamed of this, she fell from her palanquin at 葛野* and died, so that place is called 堕国, and it is said that the present 弟国 is a corruption of that name (readings unconfirmed). The empress bore 五十瓊敷入彦命, 大足彦尊, 大中姫命, 倭姫命 and 稚城瓊入彦命 (readings unconfirmed).

In the 9th month of the 23rd year, the emperor consulted his ministers because Prince 誉津別王*, though thirty years old and with a long beard, still cried like an infant and did not speak. In the 10th month, the prince looked up at a swan (鵠) crossing the sky and said, "What is that?"; the emperor rejoiced and sought someone to catch the swan. 天湯河板挙* pursued the swan and caught it in Izumo, and when he presented it in the 11th month, the prince played with it and at last became able to speak. 天湯河板挙 was granted the title of 鳥取造*, and the groups 鳥取部, 鳥養部 and 誉津部 (readings unconfirmed) were established.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

One account (一云): variant tradition in the "this year" entry of the 2nd year, part 1

It says that in the time of Emperor Sujin, 都怒我阿羅斯等*, a prince of the country of 意富加羅国* with a horn on his forehead, arrived at the bay of 笥飯浦* in the province of Koshi (越国), and named the place 角鹿*. When he returned home after serving Emperor Suinin for three years, he was told to name his country 彌摩那国* after the name of 御間城天皇*, and was given red woven silk; Silla's seizure of it was the beginning of the enmity between the two countries.

One account (一云): variant tradition in the "this year" entry of the 2nd year, part 2

It says that a white stone, a deity worshipped within a district, which 都怒我阿羅斯等* had obtained in place of an ox, turned into a beautiful girl, and when she vanished he followed her and came to Japan. The girl is said to have been worshipped as the deity of the 比売語曾社* shrine in Naniwa and in the district of 国前郡* in Toyo (豊国).

One account (一云): variant tradition in the entry for the 3rd month of the 3rd year

It says that Ame-no-Hiboko (天日槍) first arrived at the village of 完粟邑* in the province of Harima and presented eight items, including the sword 膽狭浅大刀*. Wishing to choose a place to live himself, he went from the Uji River (菟道河) through Ōmi and Wakasa and settled in Tajima, and the text records that his descendants by a woman of Tajima continued down to 清彦* and then 田道間守*.

One account (一云): small note in the entry for the 10th month of the 5th year

Against the note in the main text that makes 丹波道主王* the son of 彦坐王*, it makes him the son of 彦湯産隅王*.

Some say (或曰): entry for the 10th month of the 23rd year

It says that 天湯河板挙* caught the swan not in Izumo but in the province of Tajima.

In the original text

汝孰愛兄與夫焉

The words with which 狭穂彦王* questioned his younger sister the empress: "Which do you love more, your brother or your husband?"

錦色小蛇。繞于朕頚。復大雨從狹穗發而來之濡面。

The dream the emperor had: a small brocade-colored snake wound around his neck, and heavy rain came from 狭穂* and wet his face.

忽積稻作城。其堅不可破。此謂稻城也。

狭穂彦* piled up rice to make a fortress, and it was so solid that it could not be broken. This is called a rice-stack fortress (稲城).

皇子仰觀鵠曰。是何物耶。

The scene in which the prince who had not spoken looks up at a swan (鵠) and says, "What is that?"

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 2, section on Emperor Suinin (the rebellion of 沙本毘古王)*

In the Kojiki, the empress enters the rice-stack fortress while pregnant, the prince is born inside the fortress, and because he was born in the midst of fire he is said to have been named 本牟智和気*. In the Nihon Shoki, 誉津別命* had already been born in the 2nd year, and the empress enters the fortress holding him.

火中所生。故其御名宜稱本牟智和氣御子
Kojiki, Part 2, section on Emperor Suinin (the prince 本牟智和気)*

In the Kojiki, the prince still does not speak even after the swan is caught; a curse of the Great Deity of Izumo is revealed in the emperor's dream, and only when the prince has worshipped the Great Deity of Izumo does he become able to speak. In the Nihon Shoki, he becomes able to speak by playing with the swan, and the Great Deity of Izumo does not appear.

如思爾勿言事
Kojiki, Part 2, section on Emperor Suinin (latter part of the story of the prince 本牟智和気)*

The daughter who is sent back to her province, dies, and becomes the origin of the name "堕国 (弟国)" is, in the Kojiki, 円野比売*, who is said to have tried to hang herself at 相楽* and then to have fallen into a deep pool at 弟国 and died. In the Nihon Shoki, 竹野媛* falls from her palanquin at 葛野* and dies.

遂墮峻淵而死。故號其地。謂墮國。
