Nihon Shoki, Book 19 — Emperor Kinmei (part 1) · page 34 of 50

The Accession of Emperor Kinmei, the Talks on Restoring Mimana, and the Presentation of a Buddha Image

欽明天皇の即位・任那復興の協議と仏像の献上

天國排開廣庭天皇* (Emperor Kinmei), the legitimate heir of 男大迹天皇*, ascended the throne and took the daughters of Soga no Iname as consorts. In the first half of his reign, talks with King Sŏngmyŏng of Paekche about restoring Mimana continued, and in the 10th month of the 13th year King Sŏngmyŏng presented a gilt-bronze image of the Buddha Śākyamuni together with sutras and treatises.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textIn the 10th month of the 13th year, King Sŏngmyŏng of Paekche presented one gilt-bronze image of the Buddha Śākyamuni, together with banners and canopies and sutras and treatises, and submitted a memorial saying that this Dharma was excellent. The emperor was pleased but did not decide for himself; he asked the assembled ministers. Soga no Iname said it should be worshiped, while Mononobe no Okoshi and Nakatomi no Kamako opposed it, saying it would bring on the anger of the gods of the land.
  2. Main textThe emperor entrusted the image to Iname and let him worship it as a trial; Iname enshrined it in his house at 小墾田* and made his house at 向原* into a temple. Later, when an epidemic spread, on the advice of Okoshi and Kamako the image was thrown away into the canal (堀江) at Naniwa and the temple was burned; at that time, though there was neither wind nor cloud, the great hall caught fire.
  3. Main textFrom the 2nd to the 5th year, King Sŏngmyŏng of Paekche repeatedly called together the chiefs (旱岐) of Mimana and the Japanese Office in Mimana (任那日本府) and discussed plans for rebuilding Mimana in accordance with the emperor's edict. King Sŏngmyŏng accused 河内直*, 移那斯*, 麻都* and others of the Japanese Office of being in league with Silla, and asked the emperor to send them back to their home country.
  4. Main textIn the 9th month of the 1st year, when asked how many troops would be needed to attack Silla, Mononobe no Okoshi answered that Silla had long held a grudge because Ōtomo no Kanamura had given four districts of Mimana to Paekche in the 6th year of Emperor Keitai. Kanamura claimed illness and stopped attending court, but the emperor did not treat it as a crime.
  5. Main textIn the 3rd month of the 2nd year, 堅鹽媛*, daughter of Soga no Iname, gave birth to seven sons and six daughters, among them Prince 大兄* (橘豐日尊*) and 豐御食炊屋姫尊*. 堅鹽媛's full younger sister 小姉君* gave birth to four sons and one daughter, including 泥部穴穗部皇子* and 泊瀬部皇子*.
  6. Compared with the KojikiThe section on Emperor Kinmei in the Kojiki contains only genealogy, and entries corresponding to the presentation of the Buddha image or the talks on Mimana could not be confirmed there. In the genealogy as well, the Kojiki calls 小兄比賣* the "姨" (aunt) of 岐多志比賣命*, whereas the Nihon Shoki makes 小姉君* the full younger sister of 堅鹽媛*.

What the main text says

天國排開廣庭天皇* was the legitimate heir of 男大迹天皇*, and his mother was Empress 手白香*. When he was young, he dreamed that if he favored a man called 秦大津父* he would rule the realm, and he found 大津父* at the village of 深草里* in 紀伊郡*, Yamashiro Province. 大津父 told how, on his way back from Ise, he had stopped two wolves fighting on a mountain and let them go; the future emperor took this as the reason for his reward, kept him in close attendance, and on ascending the throne appointed him to the office of the treasury. When Emperor Senka died in the 10th month of his 4th year, the prince tried to entrust the government to Empress 山田*, but the empress declined. On the 甲申 day of the 12th month, the prince ascended the throne and made Ōtomo no Kanamura and Mononobe no Okoshi Great Chieftains and Soga no Iname Great Minister.

In the 1st month of the 1st year, 石姫*, daughter of Emperor Senka, was made empress, and the empress gave birth to Prince 箭田珠勝大兄*, 譯語田渟中倉太珠敷尊* and Princess 笠縫*. In the 7th month, the capital was moved to 磯城嶋* and named the 金刺宮* palace; in the 8th month, people who had come from overseas, such as the 秦人* and the 漢人*, were settled in the provinces and districts and entered in the household registers, and the 秦人 numbered 7,053 households. In the 9th month, at the 難波祝津宮* palace, when the emperor asked how many troops would be needed to attack Silla, Mononobe no Okoshi answered that Silla had held a grudge ever since the four districts were ceded in the reign of Emperor Keitai, and Ōtomo no Kanamura shut himself up in his house at Sumiyoshi. The emperor sent a messenger to console him and did not treat it as a crime.

In the 3rd month of the 2nd year, the emperor took five consorts. The empress's younger sister, Princess 稚綾姫*, gave birth to Prince 石上*, and Princess 日影* gave birth to Prince 倉*. 堅鹽媛*, daughter of Soga no Iname, gave birth to seven sons and six daughters, including Prince 大兄* (橘豐日尊*) and 豐御食炊屋姫尊*; her full younger sister 小姉君* gave birth to four sons and one daughter, including Prince 茨城*, 泥部穴穗部皇子* and 泊瀬部皇子*; and 糠子*, daughter of 春日日柧臣*, gave birth to Princess 春日山田* and Prince 橘麻呂*.

In the 4th month of the 2nd year, the chiefs (旱岐) of Ara, Kara and other places and 吉備臣* of the Japanese Office in Mimana went to Paekche, and King Sŏngmyŏng asked them how the restoration of Mimana, which was the emperor's edict, should be carried forward. King Sŏngmyŏng said that 喙己呑*, 南加羅* and 卓淳* had been destroyed by Silla because each of them had weaknesses within. In the 7th month, hearing that the Japanese Office in Ara had been in league with Silla, he rebuked 河内直*. In the 11th month of the 4th year, 津守連* was sent to convey an edict that the Paekche district governors and fortress commanders in 下韓* should be placed under the Japanese Office, and King Sŏngmyŏng consulted with his ministers.

Paekche repeatedly summoned Mimana and the officials of the Japanese Office, but they did not come, saying they would come after the New Year had passed or after the festival was over, and sent only people of low rank. In the 3rd month of the 5th year, Paekche submitted a memorial asking that 移那斯* and 麻都* be transferred to their home country, on the grounds that they were monopolizing the government of the Japanese Office and coming and going wearing Silla caps. In the 11th month, 吉備臣* of the Japanese Office and the chiefs (旱岐) of Mimana at last assembled, and King Sŏngmyŏng presented three plans: to ask the emperor for three thousand soldiers and repair six fortresses; to keep district governors and fortress commanders stationed in 南韓*; and to send 吉備臣, 河内直*, 移那斯 and 麻都 back to their home country.

In the 12th month of the 5th year, a report came from the land of Koshi that people of 肅愼* had come by boat to Sado Island and settled there, and that they were feared as being like demons. In the 9th month of the 6th year, Paekche made a jōroku (sixteen-foot) Buddha image and composed a prayer asking for blessings for the emperor and all living beings. In the 11th month, 膳臣巴提便*, who had returned from Paekche, told how, when his child was carried off by a tiger, he pursued the tiger, grabbed its tongue and stabbed it to death. From the 8th to the 12th year, Paekche asked for reinforcements for its war with Koguryŏ; the emperor sent 370 men to help build a fortress at 得爾辛*, and in the 12th year granted one thousand koku of wheat seed. In this year, King Sŏngmyŏng, together with the troops of Silla and Mimana, attacked Koguryŏ, took the land of 漠城*, also attacked P'yŏngyang, and recovered his former lands.

In the 4th month of the 13th year, Prince 箭田珠勝大兄* died. In the 5th month, Paekche, Kara and Ara asked for reinforcements, saying that Koguryŏ and Silla had joined hands and were planning to attack. In the 10th month, King Sŏngmyŏng presented a gilt-bronze image of the Buddha Śākyamuni together with banners and canopies and sutras and treatises, and the emperor asked the assembled ministers whether it should be worshiped. Soga no Iname recommended worshiping it, while Mononobe no Okoshi and Nakatomi no Kamako opposed it, so the emperor let Iname worship it as a trial. Later, when an epidemic spread, the image was thrown away into the canal (堀江) at Naniwa and the temple was burned, but though there was neither wind nor cloud, the great hall caught fire. In this year, Paekche abandoned 漢城* and P'yŏngyang, and Silla entered 漢城.

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

A variant account says (一書云)

Concerning the names and order of 小姉君*'s children, two variant accounts are given alongside the main text. The main text states that because the 帝王本紀* had become confused in the course of being copied, one version was chosen as the main text and the differences were recorded in notes.

The Kudara Hongi says (百濟本記云)

In the memorial in the main text, Mimana treats Ara as its elder brother and Ara treats the Japanese Office as heaven, but the Kudara Hongi records that Ara is treated as father and the Japanese Office as the root.

The Kudara Hongi says (百濟本記云)

The main text records only that the Paekche envoy returned in the 10th month of the 5th year, but the Kudara Hongi relates that there was no reply from the emperor to the report made about 河内直*, 移那斯*, 麻都* and others.

The Kudara Hongi says (百濟本記云)

The main text records only that there was a great disturbance in Koguryŏ in the 6th year, but the Kudara Hongi relates that the 細群* and the 麁群* fought at the palace gate and the descendants of the 細群 were executed, and that the king of 狛* died.

The Kudara Hongi says (百濟本記云)

Concerning the great disturbance in Koguryŏ in the 7th year, the Kudara Hongi relates that, of the king's three wives, the 麁群* and the 細群*, maternal relatives of the sons of the middle wife and the junior wife, fought over the throne.

In the original text

是法於諸法中最爲殊勝

This Dharma is the most excellent of all teachings (words from King Sŏngmyŏng's memorial).

西蕃諸國一皆禮之。豐秋日本豈獨背也

The countries of the west all worship it. Can Japan alone turn its back on it? (words of Soga no Iname).

方今改拜蕃神。恐致國神之怒

If we now worship a foreign god, we may bring on the anger of the gods of the land (words of Mononobe no Okoshi and Nakatomi no Kamako).

今寡人與汝戮力并心。翳頼天皇。任那必起

If I and you now unite our strength and hearts and rely on the emperor, Mimana will surely stand again (words of King Sŏngmyŏng).

Deities and people, and the shrines that enshrine them

  • 天國排開廣庭天皇*Legitimate heir of 男大迹天皇*. Emperor Kinmei, who ruled the realm from the 磯城嶋金刺宮* palace. A reading note says "開、此云波羅企".
  • 秦大津父*A man who appeared in the emperor's dream. He told the story of stopping the wolves' fight and, after the accession, was appointed to the office of the treasury.
  • 石姫*Daughter of Emperor Senka and empress. She gave birth to 譯語田渟中倉太珠敷尊* and others.
  • Soga no Iname no Sukune (蘇我稻目宿禰)Great Minister. His daughters 堅鹽媛* and 小姉君* became consorts, and he worshiped the Buddha image that had been presented.
  • Mononobe no Okoshi (物部尾輿)Great Chieftain. He stated the cause of Silla's grudge and opposed the worship of the Buddha image.
  • Nakatomi no Kamako (中臣鎌子)A man who, together with Mononobe no Okoshi, opposed the worship of the Buddha image.
  • 堅鹽媛*Daughter of Soga no Iname. She gave birth to seven sons and six daughters. A reading note says "堅鹽、此云岐施志".
  • 小姉君*Full younger sister of 堅鹽媛*. She gave birth to 泥部穴穗部皇子*, 泊瀬部皇子* and others.
  • King Sŏngmyŏng of Paekche (百濟聖明王)King of Paekche. He led the talks on restoring Mimana and in the 13th year presented a Buddha image and sutras and treatises.
  • 河內直*, 移那斯* and 麻都*People at the Japanese Office in Mimana at Ara. They were accused by Paekche of being in league with Silla.
  • 膳臣巴提便*An envoy to Paekche. He told how he grabbed the tongue of the tiger that had carried off his child with his left hand and stabbed it to death with his right.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Kinmei (genealogy)

The Kojiki calls 小兄比賣* the "姨" (aunt) of 岐多志比賣命* and lists five children. The Nihon Shoki makes 小姉君* the full younger sister of 堅鹽媛* and says she gave birth to four sons and one daughter.

又娶岐多志比賣命之姨。小兄比賣
Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Kinmei (genealogy)

The Kojiki gives three children of 糠子郎女*, daughter of 春日之日爪臣*: 春日山田郎女*, 麻呂古王* and 宗賀之倉王*; the Nihon Shoki gives two children of 糠子*: Princess 春日山田* and Prince 橘麻呂*.

又娶春日之日爪臣之女。糠子郎女
Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Kinmei (opening)

The section on Emperor Kinmei in the Kojiki records only that he lived at the 師木嶋大宮* palace and his genealogy, and entries corresponding to the talks on Mimana or the presentation of the Buddha image could not be confirmed there. The Nihon Shoki gives his palace as the 磯城嶋金刺宮*.
