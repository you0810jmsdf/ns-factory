Nihon Shoki, Book 18 — Emperor Ankan and Emperor Senka · page 33 of 50

Emperor Ankan, Emperor Senka and the Establishment of Miyake Estates

安閑天皇・宣化天皇と屯倉の設置

This book records two reigns: that of 勾大兄* (Emperor Ankan), son of Emperor Keitai, and that of his full younger brother 武小廣國押盾* (Emperor Senka). In Emperor Ankan's reign many miyake (crown estates) were established in various regions, and in Emperor Senka's reign an order was given to gather the grain of the miyake at the government office at 那津之口*.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textBecause Emperor Ankan had no children even after taking four wives, on the advice of Ōtomo no Kanamura he established miyake estates (小墾田屯倉*, 櫻井屯倉* and 難波屯倉*) to preserve the names of his empress and consorts for later generations.
  2. Main textIn the 5th month of the 2nd year of Emperor Ankan, more than twenty miyake estates were established at once in provinces including Tsukushi, Toyo, Hi, Harima, Bingo, Awa, Kii, Tanba, Ōmi, Owari, Kamitsukeno and Suruga.
  3. Main textStories follow of miyake estates being presented in atonement for offenses. The Kuni-no-Miyatsuko of 伊甚* presented the 伊甚屯倉* estate for the offense of frightening the empress, and the Kuni-no-Miyatsuko of Musashi, 笠原直使主*, presented four miyake estates, including 横渟*, in thanks for being confirmed in the position of Kuni-no-Miyatsuko.
  4. Main textIn the 5th month of his 1st year, Emperor Senka issued an edict that "food is the foundation of the realm," had the grain of the miyake estates in various regions transported, built a government office at 那津之口*, and had it prepared for emergencies.
  5. Main textIn the 2nd month of the 1st year of Emperor Senka, Soga no Iname no Sukune was appointed Great Minister. In the 10th month of the 2nd year, because Silla attacked Mimana, 磐* and 狭手彦*, sons of Ōtomo no Kanamura, were sent; 磐 stayed in Tsukushi and 狭手彦 in Mimana.
  6. Compared with the KojikiFor Emperor Ankan, the Kojiki records only his palace, the fact that he had no children, and his tomb, and does not tell of the miyake estates. As for Emperor Senka's children, the Kojiki makes 惠波王* (ancestor of the 韋那君* and the 多治比君*) a child of 川内之若子比賣*, whereas the Nihon Shoki makes Prince 上殖葉*, ancestor of the 丹比公* and the 偉那公*, a child of the empress 橘仲皇女*.

What the main text says

勾大兄廣國押武金日天皇* was the eldest son of 男大迹天皇*, and his mother was 目子媛*. On the 丁未 day of the 2nd month of the 25th year of Emperor Keitai, 男大迹天皇 set up 大兄* as emperor and died that same day. Ōtomo no Kanamura and 物部麁鹿火* were made Great Chieftains as before. In the 1st month of the 1st year, the capital was moved to 勾金橋* in the province of 大倭*; in the 3rd month, Princess 春日山田*, daughter of 億計天皇*, was made empress, and three women, 紗手媛*, 香香有媛* and 宅媛*, were made consorts.

In the 4th month of the 1st year, when 膳臣大麻呂* sought pearls from 伊甚*, the Kuni-no-Miyatsuko of 伊甚 and the others arrived late; when questioned, they fled into the rear palace and frightened the empress so that she fell down. To atone for their offense, the Kuni-no-Miyatsuko and the others presented the 伊甚屯倉* estate. In the 7th month, when an imperial envoy sought land for a miyake for the empress, 大河内直味張*, begrudging his good rice fields, told a lie. In the intercalary 12th month, at 三嶋*, the district chief 飯粒* willingly presented forty chō of land, so Ōtomo no Kanamura rebuked 味張* and removed him as district official, and 味張 promised to provide five hundred mattock laborers each in spring and autumn.

In the 10th month, the emperor grieved that although he had four wives he had no children, and that his name would die out. Ōtomo no Kanamura recommended that, whether or not one has children, one's name should be preserved through things, and that miyake estates be built for the empress and consorts; the 小墾田屯倉*, 櫻井屯倉* and 難波屯倉* estates were given to the consorts. In this month there was an incident in which 幡媛*, daughter of the 廬城部連*, stole a jeweled necklace belonging to Mononobe no Okoshi and presented it to the empress, and a group of workers and land were presented in atonement. Also, 笠原直使主* and 小杵* contended for the position of Kuni-no-Miyatsuko of Musashi; the court made 使主* Kuni-no-Miyatsuko and executed 小杵, so 使主 presented four miyake estates: 横渟*, 橘花*, 多氷* and 倉樔*.

In the 1st month of the 2nd year, rejoicing that abundant harvests had continued and the country had grown rich, the emperor permitted a great five-day feast throughout the realm. In the 5th month, many miyake estates were established in provinces from Tsukushi to Suruga and Kamitsukeno; in the 8th month, groups of dog-keepers were established in the provinces; and in the 9th month, the 櫻井田部連* and others were put in charge of the taxes of the miyake estates. In the 12th month, the emperor died at the 勾金橋宮* palace. He was seventy years old. He was buried in the 舊市高屋丘陵* tomb in Kawachi, and the empress, Princess 春日山田*, and the emperor's younger sister, Princess 神前*, were buried together with him in the same tomb.

武小廣國押盾天皇* was the second son of 男大迹天皇* and the full younger brother of 勾大兄天皇*. When his elder brother died without children, the assembled ministers presented the sword and mirror to him and had him ascend the throne. In the 1st month of the 1st year, the capital was moved to 檜隈廬入野*; in the 2nd month, Ōtomo no Kanamura and 物部麁鹿火* were made Great Chieftains as before, Soga no Iname no Sukune was made Great Minister, and 阿倍火麻呂臣* was made a high official (大夫). In the 3rd month, Princess 橘仲*, daughter of 億計天皇*, was made empress, and the empress gave birth to Princess 石姫*, Princess 小石姫*, Princess 倉稚綾姫* and Prince 上殖葉*.

In the 5th month of the 1st year, declaring that "food is the foundation of the realm," the emperor ordered that, since Tsukushi was the gateway through which countries far and near came to the court, the grain of the miyake estates of 茨田郡*, Owari, 新家*, Iga and elsewhere be transported, and that a government office be built at 那津之口* to prepare for bad years and emergencies. In the 7th month, the Great Chieftain 物部麁鹿火* died. In the 10th month of the 2nd year, because Silla invaded Mimana, 磐* and 狭手彦*, sons of Ōtomo no Kanamura, were sent; 磐 governed in Tsukushi and kept watch against the Three Han, while 狭手彦 pacified Mimana and rescued Paekche. In the 2nd month of the 4th year, the emperor died at the 檜隈廬入野宮* palace. He was seventy-three years old; in the 11th month he was buried in the 身狹桃花鳥坂上陵* tomb, and the empress, Princess 橘*, and her young child were buried together with him.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

One text says (一本云)

The main text says that the 櫻井屯倉* estate was given to 香香有媛*, but one text says that she was given the 茅渟山屯倉* estate as well.

In the original text

朕納四妻。至今無嗣。萬歳之後朕名絶矣

I have taken four wives, but even now I have no heir. After my death my name will die out.

率土之上莫匪王封。普天之下莫匪王域

There is no place on the earth that is not the king's domain, and no place under heaven that is not the king's land (words rebuking 味張*).

食者天下之本也。黄金萬貫不可療飢

Food is the very foundation of the realm. However much gold there may be, it cannot cure hunger.

磐留筑紫執其國政以備三韓

磐* stayed in Tsukushi, governed that land, and kept watch against the Three Han.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Ankan

The section on Emperor Ankan in the Kojiki records only that he lived at the 勾之金箸宮* palace, that he had no children, and that his tomb is at the village of 古市高屋* in Kawachi; the story of the miyake estates could not be confirmed there. The Nihon Shoki says that the empress and the emperor's younger sister were also buried with him in the 舊市高屋丘陵* tomb in Kawachi.

此天皇無御子也
Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Senka (genealogy)

The Kojiki makes 惠波王* (ancestor of the 韋那君* and the 多治比君*) a child of 川内之若子比賣*. The Nihon Shoki makes Prince 上殖葉* (also called 椀子*), ancestor of the 丹比公* and the 偉那公*, a child of the empress 橘仲皇女*, and gives only one child, Prince 火焔*, to 大河内稚子媛*.

惠波王者。〔韋那君。多治比君之祖也。〕
