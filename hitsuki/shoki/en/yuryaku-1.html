Nihon Shoki, Book 14 — Emperor Yūryaku · page 28 of 50

The accession of Emperor Yūryaku and Hitokotonushi-no-Kami (一事主神) of 葛城山*

雄略天皇の即位と葛城山の一事主神

When Emperor 穴穗 (穴穗天皇; Emperor Ankō)* was killed by 眉輪王*, Prince 大泊瀬 (大泊瀬皇子)* struck down, one after another, his elder brothers, 眉輪王, the Great Minister 圓 (圓大臣)*, Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)* and others, and ascended the throne at 泊瀬朝倉*. For the first to seventh years after the accession, the text records the installation of the Empress and consorts, the Emperor's anger at the hunting ground and the condemnation of him as "a greatly wicked emperor" (太惡天皇), the meeting with Hitokotonushi-no-Kami (一事主神) on 葛城山*, the punishment of the Kibi clan, the story of 稚媛*, wife of 田狹臣*, and other matters.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textIn the 8th month of the 3rd year of Emperor Ankō, 眉輪王* stabbed Emperor 穴穗 (穴穗天皇)* to death as the enemy of his father. Prince 大泊瀬* cut down Prince 八釣白彦 (八釣白彦皇子)*, burned the house of the Great Minister 圓 (圓大臣)*, who was sheltering Prince 坂合黒彦 (坂合黒彦皇子)* and 眉輪王, killing all three, and in the 10th month lured Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)* on a hunt and shot him dead; then in the 11th month he ascended the throne at 泊瀬朝倉*.
  2. Main textIn the 2nd month of the 4th year, on 葛城山*, the Emperor met a tall person who closely resembled him, and that person named himself "Hitokotonushi-no-Kami (一事主神)". The two hunted side by side, chasing deer, and the deity escorted the Emperor as far as 來目水*. Seeing this, the people said, "A virtuous emperor" (有徳天皇).
  3. CommentaryThe Kokugakuin University deity-name database notes that the Nihon Shoki says only that Hitokotonushi-no-Kami (一事主神) closely resembled the Emperor in face and figure, and introduces the observation that, because the Emperor refers to himself as "朕" and the deity to himself as "僕", the Emperor is placed in the higher position. [source, Japanese]
  4. Main textFor the 2nd year, the text says that the Emperor, angered that his ministers could not answer about the task of cutting fresh meat at the hunting ground, cut down his driver, and that people condemned him as "a greatly wicked emperor" (太惡天皇). On the other hand, in the story of Hitokotonushi-no-Kami (一事主神) in the 4th year, the people said "a virtuous emperor" (有徳天皇), so diametrically opposed reputations stand side by side in the same book.
  5. Main textIn the 7th year, 吉備下道臣前津屋*, who had been dividing young girls and roosters into the Emperor's side and his own side, making them fight, and killing them when the Emperor's side won, was killed together with seventy members of his family. In the same year, 吉備上道臣田狹*, because he had boasted of his wife 稚媛*, was sent out as governor of Mimana (任那國司), and the Emperor took 稚媛 into the palace. 田狹's son 弟君* set out to attack Silla but turned back on the way and was killed by his wife 樟媛*.
  6. Compared with the KojikiThe story of the boar on 葛城山* is also in the Kojiki, but there it is the Emperor himself who, afraid of the boar, climbs an alder (榛). In the Nihon Shoki the one who climbs the tree is an attendant (舍人); the Emperor tramples the boar to death, and when he is about to cut down the attendant he is admonished by the Empress.

What the main text says

Emperor 大泊瀬幼武 (大泊瀬幼武天皇)* was the fifth son of Emperor 雄朝津間稚子宿禰 (雄朝津間稚子宿禰天皇)*, and it is said that when he was born a mysterious light filled the hall. In the 8th month of the 3rd year of Emperor Ankō, Emperor 穴穗 (穴穗天皇)* held a drinking party at the mountain palace and told the Empress, "I fear 眉輪王*." The young 眉輪王, who was playing below the tower, heard this, and when the Emperor fell asleep with his head on the Empress's lap he stabbed him to death. Prince 大泊瀬*, on receiving the news, suspected his elder brothers, and cut down Prince 八釣白彦 (八釣白彦皇子)*, who stayed silent and did not answer. Prince 坂合黒彦 (坂合黒彦皇子)* and 眉輪王 fled into the house of the Great Minister 圓 (圓大臣)*; the minister offered his daughter 韓媛* and seven residences (宅七区) in 葛城* and asked for pardon, but the prince would not listen and burned the house, and the three were burned to death together.

In the 10th month of the same year, Prince 大泊瀬*, resenting that Emperor 穴穗 (穴穗天皇)* had once intended to pass the country on to Prince 市邊押磐 (市邊押磐皇子)*, lured him out to a hunt at 來田綿蛟屋野* in Ōmi. The prince falsely cried out, "There is a boar!", shot Prince 市邊押磐 dead, and also killed 佐伯部賣輪*, the prince's retainer, who was holding the body and wailing. In this month Prince 御馬 (御馬皇子)* was also captured beside the 磐井 well* at Miwa, and before his execution he cursed that well. In the 11th month the Emperor set up a platform at 泊瀬朝倉* and ascended the throne, making 平群臣眞鳥* Great Minister (大臣) and 大伴連室屋* and 物部連目* Ōmuraji (大連).

In the 3rd month of the first year, Princess 草香幡梭姫 (草香幡梭姫皇女)* was made Empress, and in this month three consorts were installed. 韓媛*, daughter of the Great Minister 葛城圓 (葛城圓大臣)*, bore Prince 白髪 (白髪皇子)* and Princess 稚足姫 (稚足姫皇女)*, and this princess served at the shrine of the Great Deity of Ise (伊勢大神). 稚媛*, daughter of the 吉備上道臣*, bore Prince 磐城 (磐城皇子)* and Prince 星川稚宮 (星川稚宮皇子)*. 童女君*, who had been a court lady (采女), bore a girl after a single night, so the Emperor doubted her and did not raise the child; but the Ōmuraji 物部目 (物部目大連)* persuaded the Emperor by saying that the girl looked like him, and the child was made a princess and her mother a consort.

In the 7th month of the 2nd year, because 池津媛* of Paekche, whom the Emperor intended to summon, had relations with 石河楯*, the Emperor had the two tied to a tree and burned to death. In the 10th month, while hunting at 御馬瀬* in Yoshino, he was angered that his ministers could not answer about the handling of fresh meat, and cut down his driver 大津馬飼*. The Empress Dowager and the Empress had the court lady (采女) 日媛* offer him sake to calm him, and because the Empress Dowager presented 膳臣長野* and others, the cooks' group (宍人部) was established. Because the Emperor took only his own mind as his teacher and wrongly killed many people, the people of the realm condemned him as "a greatly wicked emperor" (太惡天皇); it is said that the only ones the Emperor favored were 身狹村主青* and 桧隈民使博徳* and others of the scribes (史部).

In the 4th month of the 3rd year, 阿閇臣國見* slandered Princess 栲幡 (栲幡皇女)*, who served the Great Deity of Ise (伊勢大神), and 廬城部連武彦* about their relationship. 武彦 was killed by his father, and the princess took the sacred mirror, buried it beside the Isuzu River (五十鈴河), and took her own life. When the Emperor had a search made, a rainbow like a serpent rose over the upper reaches of the river; digging there, they found the sacred mirror, and nearby they also found the princess's body. When her belly was cut open there was only water and stones, so the suspicion against 武彦 was cleared, and 國見 fled into the 石上神宮 shrine*.

In the 2nd month of the 4th year, the Emperor met Hitokotonushi-no-Kami (一事主神) on 葛城山* and they hunted together. In the 8th month, when he was hunting at 河上小野* in Yoshino, a dragonfly caught and carried off a horsefly that had bitten the Emperor's arm, so the Emperor composed a song praising it and named the place 蜻蛉野*. In the 2nd month of the 5th year, on 葛城山, he was attacked by an enraged boar; he was about to cut down an attendant (舍人) who had escaped by climbing a tree, but thought better of it on being admonished by the Empress, and said joyfully, "Everyone hunts birds and beasts, but I return having gained good words." In the same year 加須利君 (蓋鹵王) (readings unconfirmed) of Paekche sent his younger brother 軍君* to Japan, and the pregnant wife who accompanied him gave birth to 嶋君* on the island of 各羅嶋* in Tsukushi. It is said that this was 武寧王*.

In the 6th year, the Emperor composed a song praising the small plain of 泊瀬*, and because Sugaru (■■), who had been ordered to gather silkworms (蚕), mistakenly gathered infants (子) instead, he was given the title 少子部連*. In the 7th month of the 7th year, the Emperor told this 少子部連 that he wished to see the form of the deity of 三諸岳*; the great serpent he caught and brought back rumbled like thunder and its eyes blazed, so the Emperor, frightened, had it released on the mountain and gave him the name 雷 ("Thunder"). In the 8th month, the Emperor heard that 吉備下道臣前津屋* had been dividing young girls and roosters into "the Emperor's" and "mine", making them fight, and killing them when the Emperor's side won, so the Emperor had seventy members of his family killed. In this year, following from the Emperor's taking 稚媛*, wife of 吉備上道臣田狹*, into the palace, there occurred the affair in which 田狹's son 弟君*, on the way to attack Silla, stayed in Paekche and was killed by his wife 樟媛*; and the new craftsmen who had come from Paekche were, by command to 東漢直掬*, moved to and settled at 上桃原, 下桃原 and 眞神原 (readings unconfirmed).

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

Another text says (別本云)

It gives the name of the wife of 田狹臣* as 毛媛*, and makes her the daughter of 玉田宿禰*, son of 葛城襲津彦*. It says that the Emperor, hearing of her beauty, killed her husband and took her for himself. In the main text, 田狹 is kept away by being appointed governor of Mimana (任那國司).

A certain text says (或本云)

It says that 吉備臣弟君* came back from Paekche and presented the 漢手人部, 衣縫部 and 宍人部 groups (readings unconfirmed). In the main text, 弟君 stays in Paekche and is killed by his wife 樟媛*.

Some say (或云)

Concerning the deity of 三諸岳*, it gives one view that it is 大物代主神* and another that it is the 墨坂神* of 菟田*. The main text does not give the deity's name.

A certain text says (或本云)

It gives 吉備下道臣前津屋*, who was killed by the Emperor, as "國造吉備臣山"*.

The New Selection of Paekche (百濟新撰) says

It says that in the year 己巳 蓋鹵王* became king, and that because the Emperor sent 阿禮奴跪* to ask for a woman, Paekche adorned 適稽女郎*, daughter of 慕尼夫人*, and presented her as tribute. She is the 池津媛* of the main text, and the note to the entry for the 4th month of the 5th year also identifies 池津媛 with 適稽女郎.

In the original text

臣元不求天位。唯報父仇而已

The words of 眉輪王*, meaning: "I never sought the imperial throne. I only avenged my father."

天下誹謗言。太惡天皇也

An entry saying that because the Emperor wrongly killed many people, the people of the world condemned him as "a very wicked emperor".

朕是幼武尊也。長人次稱曰。僕是一事主神也

The scene on 葛城山* in which the Emperor first names himself, and then the tall person names himself, saying, "I am Hitokotonushi-no-Kami (一事主神)."

人皆獵禽獸。朕獵得善言而歸

The words of the Emperor after accepting the Empress's admonition, meaning: "Everyone hunts birds and beasts, but I return having hunted good words."

Deities and people, and the shrines that enshrine them

  • Hitokotonushi-no-Kami一言主神Enshrined at: Tosa Jinja
  • 大泊瀬幼武天皇*Fifth son of Emperor 雄朝津間稚子宿禰 (雄朝津間稚子宿禰天皇)*. After his elder brother Emperor 穴穗* was killed, he struck down rival members of the imperial family and ascended the throne at 泊瀬朝倉*.
  • 眉輪王*Son of 大草香皇子* and Princess 中蒂姫 (中蒂姫皇女)*. He stabbed Emperor 穴穗* to death as his father's enemy, and was burned to death in the house of the Great Minister 圓 (圓大臣)*.
  • 圓大臣*The Great Minister of 葛城*. He would not hand over Prince 坂合黒彦* and 眉輪王*, who had fled to him, and offered his daughter 韓媛* and seven residences, but was not pardoned and was burned to death.
  • 市邊押磐皇子*The prince to whom Emperor 穴穗* had intended to pass on the country. He was lured out on a hunt and shot dead by Prince 大泊瀬*.
  • 八釣白彦皇子 and 坂合黒彦皇子 (readings unconfirmed)Elder brothers of Prince 大泊瀬*. They were suspected after the killing of Emperor 穴穗*; Prince 白彦 was cut down and Prince 黒彦 was burned to death in the house of the Great Minister 圓*.
  • 韓媛*Daughter of the Great Minister 圓 (圓大臣)*. She was made a consort in the first year and bore Prince 白髪 (白髪皇子; later Emperor Seinei)* and Princess 稚足姫 (稚足姫皇女)*.
  • 物部目大連*Made Ōmuraji (大連) at the accession. He persuaded the Emperor to recognize the girl borne by 童女君* as a princess.
  • 栲幡皇女 (稚足姫皇女) (readings unconfirmed)A princess who served at the shrine of the Great Deity of Ise (伊勢大神). Slandered, she buried the sacred mirror beside the Isuzu River (五十鈴河) and took her own life.
  • 吉備上道臣田狹*Because he boasted of the beauty of his wife 稚媛*, he was sent out as governor of Mimana (任那國司) and his wife was taken by the Emperor. After that he sought to rely on Silla.
  • 樟媛*Wife of 弟君*, son of 田狹*. Hating that her husband stayed in Paekche intending to rebel, she killed him.
  • 軍君 (崑攴君) (readings unconfirmed)Younger brother of 加須利君 (蓋鹵王) (readings unconfirmed) of Paekche. He was sent to Japan to serve the Emperor.
  • 嶋君 (武寧王) (readings unconfirmed)The child born on the island of 各羅嶋* in Tsukushi to the pregnant wife of 加須利君*, who had accompanied 軍君*. He was sent back to Paekche.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Ankō (the incident of 目弱王* and the revenge of 大長谷王*)

In the Kojiki, 目弱王 (眉輪王) (readings unconfirmed) is seven years old and cuts off the Emperor's head with the long sword beside him; 大長谷王* cuts down his elder brother 黑日子王* and buries 白日子王* alive at 小治田*. In the Nihon Shoki it is Prince 八釣白彦 (八釣白彦皇子)* who is cut down, and Prince 坂合黒彦 (坂合黒彦皇子)* is burned to death together with 眉輪王 and the Great Minister 圓 (圓大臣)*. Also, in the Kojiki 都夫良意美* stabs 目弱王 and then kills himself.

掘穴而隨立埋者
Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Yūryaku (Hitokotonushi-no-Ōkami (一言主大神) of 葛城山*)

In the Kojiki, the retinue of Hitokotonushi-no-Ōkami (一言主大神) appears dressed exactly like the Emperor's procession, and there is a scene in which each side fits arrows to their bows and asks the other's name; the Emperor, afraid, presents long swords, bows and arrows, and the clothing of all his officials. In the Nihon Shoki the Emperor and the deity speak with restraint and hunt side by side, and there is no story of offerings. As for where the deity escorts the Emperor, the Kojiki says the mountain entrance of 長谷*, and the Nihon Shoki says 來目水*.

言離之神。葛城之一言主大神者也
Kojiki, Part 3, section on Emperor Yūryaku (the great boar of 葛城山*)

In the Kojiki, the Emperor himself, chased by a boar on 葛城山*, is frightened, climbs an alder (榛) and composes a song. In the Nihon Shoki the one who climbs the tree is an attendant (舍人); the Emperor tramples the boar to death, and the one who composes a song is the attendant who is about to be executed.
