Nihon Shoki, Book 4 — Emperor Suizei to Emperor Kaika · page 12 of 50

From Emperor Suizei to Emperor Kaika (Including the Rebellion of 手研耳命)

綏靖天皇から開化天皇まで（手研耳命の反逆を含む）

This page covers the eight reigns from the emperor 神渟名川耳 (Emperor Suizei), who took the throne after striking down his elder half-brother 手研耳命* following the death of Emperor Jinmu, to the emperor 稚日本根子彦大日日 (Emperor Kaika). Apart from the rebellion of 手研耳命, it consists mainly of short entries recording, with years and months, each emperor's parents, appointment as crown prince, accession, capital, empress and children, the burial of the previous emperor, and death.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textAfter the death of Emperor Jinmu, his elder half-brother 手研耳命* seized power at will during the mourning period and sought to harm his two younger brothers. 神渟名川耳尊*, together with his elder brother Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto (神八井耳命), attacked 手研耳命 as he slept in a chamber at 片丘*, and, taking the place of his elder brother, whose hands and feet trembled so that he could not shoot, shot him dead himself.
  2. Main textKamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto, saying, “Though I am the elder brother, I was cowardly and could not carry the matter through,” yielded the heavenly throne to his younger brother, and stated that he himself would take the role of worshipping the deities of heaven and earth to help his younger brother. Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto is regarded as the first ancestor of the 多臣*.
  3. Main textEach entry from Suizei to Kaika is written as a short record of the same pattern: parents, the year of appointment as crown prince, accession, honoring the empress as empress dowager, the move of the capital and the name of the palace, the empress and children, the burial of the previous emperor, and death; other names for the empress are often added under “a certain writing says” (一書云) or “an alternative version” (一云).
  4. Main textYamato-totohi-momoso-hime-no-Mikoto (倭迹迹日百襲姫命) and 彦五十狹芹彦命 * (also called 吉備津彦命) were born to a consort of Emperor Kōrei, and 武埴安彦命* to 埴安媛*, a consort of Emperor Kōgen; Ōbiko-no-Mikoto (大彦命) is regarded as the first ancestor of seven clans including the 阿倍臣*, and 彦太忍信命* as the grandfather of Takeuchi-no-Sukune (武内宿禰). These are people who appear in the section on Emperor Sujin in Book 5.
  5. Compared with the KojikiThe Kojiki holds that the elder half-brother 當藝志美美命* took as wife 伊須氣余理比賣*, Jinmu's empress, and that the empress warned her children of the danger by means of songs, but the Nihon Shoki has no such story and holds that the younger brothers perceived 手研耳命's scheme by themselves.
  6. CommentaryThe deity-name database of Kokugakuin University states that in the Nihon Shoki the name of Kotoshironushi-no-Kami appears as the father of Himetatara-isuzu-hime (媛蹈鞴五十鈴媛), Emperor Jinmu's empress, and of 五十鈴依媛* in the preface to the record of Annei, and as the grandfather of 鴨王* in the preface to the record of Itoku, and that these are traditions of Kotoshironushi-no-Kami not recorded in the Kojiki. [source, Japanese]

What the main text says

The emperor 神渟名川耳* was the third child of the emperor 神日本磐余彦 (Emperor Jinmu), and his mother was Himetatara-isuzu-hime-no-Mikoto (媛蹈■五十鈴媛命; one character is missing in the original text), eldest daughter of Kotoshironushi-no-Kami. When he was forty-eight, the emperor 神日本磐余彦 died, and he devoted himself to the mourning and funeral, but his elder half-brother 手研耳命*, being older and experienced in government, had been entrusted with affairs. 手研耳命 seized power at will during the mourning period and schemed to harm his two younger brothers. This was the year 太歳己卯. In winter, in the eleventh month, 神渟名川耳尊 and his elder brother Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto learned of the plot, and when the matter of the mausoleum was finished, they had 弓部稚彦* make a bow, 倭鍛部天津眞浦* make arrowheads, and the arrow-makers (矢部) make arrows. The two burst in on 手研耳命 as he slept alone on a large bed in a chamber at 片丘*, but Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto's hands and feet trembled and he could not shoot, so 神渟名川耳尊 took his elder brother's bow and arrows and shot; the first arrow struck the chest and the second the back, and 手研耳命 died. Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto yielded the throne to his younger brother, stating that he himself would worship the deities of heaven and earth and help his younger brother, and he is regarded as the first ancestor of the 多臣*.

In the first month of the 1st year, 神渟名川耳尊 took the throne and made his capital at 葛城*, calling it the 高丘宮* palace. This was the year 太歳庚辰. In the first month of the 2nd year, he made 五十鈴依媛*, the emperor's aunt, empress, and the emperor 磯城津彦玉手看* was born. In the fourth month of the 4th year Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto died and was buried north of Mount Unebi (畝傍山), and in the first month of the 25th year (「廿五年」 in the original) 磯城津彦玉手看尊 was made crown prince. In the fifth month of the 33rd year (「卅三年」 in the original) the emperor fell ill and died. He was eighty-four years old.

The emperor 磯城津彦玉手看 (Emperor Annei), whose mother was 五十鈴依媛命*, youngest daughter of Kotoshironushi-no-Kami, took the throne in the seventh month after his father's death, and in the tenth month of the 1st year buried his father in the 倭桃花鳥田丘上陵* mausoleum. This was the year 太歳癸丑. In the 2nd year he moved the capital to 片鹽*, making it the 浮孔宮* palace, and in the 3rd year he made 渟名底仲媛命 * (also called 渟名襲媛) empress, and 息石耳命 and the emperor 大日本彦耜友 (readings unconfirmed) were born. In the 11th year 大日本彦耜友尊 was made crown prince, and his younger brother 磯城津彦命* is regarded as the first ancestor of the 猪使連*. He died in the twelfth month of the 38th year, at the age of fifty-seven. The next emperor, 大日本彦耜友 (Emperor Itoku), whose mother was 渟名底仲媛命, daughter of 鴨王*, a grandson of Kotoshironushi-no-Kami, took the throne in the second month of the 1st year, and in the year 太歳辛卯 buried his father in the 畝傍山南御陰井上陵* mausoleum. In the 2nd year he moved the capital to the land of 輕*, making it the 曲峽宮* palace, and made 天豐津媛命* empress, and the emperor 觀松彦香殖稻* was born; in the 22nd year he appointed the crown prince, and he died in the ninth month of the 34th year.

The emperor 觀松彦香殖稻 (Emperor Kōshō) had as his mother 天豐津媛命*, daughter of 息石耳命*. In the tenth month of the year after his father's death he buried his father in the 畝傍山南纎沙谿上陵* mausoleum; he took the throne in the first month of the 1st year, and in the seventh month moved the capital to 掖上*, making it the 池心宮* palace. This was the year 太歳丙寅. In the 29th year he made 世襲足媛* empress, and 天足彦國押人命 and the emperor 日本足彦國押人 (readings unconfirmed) were born, and in the 68th year 日本足彦國押人尊 was made crown prince. The elder brother 天足彦國押人命 is regarded as the first ancestor of the 和珥臣* and others. He died in the eighth month of the 83rd year. The emperor 日本足彦國押人 (Emperor Kōan), whose mother was 世襲足媛, younger sister of 瀛津世襲*, distant ancestor of the Owari no Muraji (尾張連), took the throne in the first month of the 1st year (太歳己丑); in the 2nd year he moved the capital to the land of 室*, making it the 秋津嶋宮* palace; in the 26th year he made his niece 押媛* empress, and the emperor 大日本根子彦太瓊* was born; in the 38th year he buried his father in the 掖上博多山上陵* mausoleum; in the 76th year he appointed the crown prince; and he died in the first month of the 102nd year.

The mother of the emperor 大日本根子彦太瓊 (Emperor Kōrei) was 押媛*, and it is recorded that she was perhaps the daughter of 天足彦國押人命*. In the ninth month of the year his father died, he buried his father in the 玉手丘上陵* mausoleum; in the twelfth month he moved the capital to 黒田*, making it the 廬戸宮* palace; and he took the throne in the first month of the 1st year (太歳辛未). In the 2nd year he made 細媛命* empress, and the emperor 大日本根子彦國牽* was born; from the consort 倭國香媛* were born Yamato-totohi-momoso-hime-no-Mikoto, 彦五十狹芹彦命 (also called 吉備津彦命), and 倭迹迹稚屋姫命 (readings unconfirmed), and from another consort 彦狹嶋命 and 稚武彦命 (readings unconfirmed). 稚武彦命 is regarded as the first ancestor of the Kibi no Omi (吉備臣). In the 36th year 彦國牽尊 was made crown prince, and he died in the second month of the 76th year.

The emperor 大日本根子彦國牽 (Emperor Kōgen), whose mother was 細媛命*, daughter of 大目*, district chief of 磯城 (磯城縣主), took the throne in the first month of the 1st year (太歳丁亥); in the 4th year he moved the capital to the land of 輕*, making it the 境原宮* palace; and in the 6th year he buried his father in the 片丘馬坂陵* mausoleum. In the 7th year he made 欝色謎命* empress, and Ōbiko-no-Mikoto (大彦命), the emperor 稚日本根子彦大日日*, and 倭迹迹姫命* were born; from the consort 伊香色謎命* was born 彦太忍信命*, and from the consort 埴安媛*, daughter of 河内青玉繋*, was born 武埴安彦命*. Ōbiko-no-Mikoto is regarded as the first ancestor of seven clans including the 阿倍臣 and the 膳臣 (readings unconfirmed), and 彦太忍信命 as the grandfather of Takeuchi-no-Sukune. In the 22nd year he appointed the crown prince, and he died in the ninth month of the 57th year.

The emperor 稚日本根子彦大日日 (Emperor Kaika), whose mother was 欝色謎命*, younger sister of 欝色雄命*, distant ancestor of the 穗積臣*, took the throne in the eleventh month of the year his father died. In the tenth month of the 1st year he moved the capital to the land of Kasuga (春日), making it the 率川宮* palace (太歳甲申), and in the 5th year he buried his father in the 劒池嶋上陵* mausoleum. In the 6th year he made 伊香色謎命*, who was his stepmother (庶母), empress, and the emperor 御間城入彦五十瓊殖* was born; before that, 彦湯産隅命* had been born from the consort 竹野媛* of 丹波*, and 彦坐王* from the consort 姥津媛*. In the 28th year he made 御間城入彦尊 crown prince at the age of nineteen; he died in the fourth month of the 60th year and was buried in the tenth month in the 春日率川坂本陵* mausoleum.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

A certain writing says (Emperor Suizei's empress)

In contrast to the main text, which makes the empress 五十鈴依媛*, one writing makes her 川派媛*, daughter of the district chief of 磯城 (磯城縣主), and another writing makes her 糸織媛*, daughter of 大日諸*, district chief of Kasuga (春日縣主).

A certain writing says (Emperor Annei's empress)

In contrast to the main text, which makes the empress 渟名底仲媛命*, one writing makes her 川津媛*, daughter of 葉江*, district chief of 磯城, and another writing makes her 糸井媛*, daughter of 大間宿禰*.

An alternative version (Emperor Annei's princes)

In contrast to the main text, which gives two princes, the alternative version (一云) gives three: the first 常津彦某兄, the second the emperor 大日本彦耜友, and the third 磯城津彦命 (readings unconfirmed).

An alternative version (Emperor Itoku's empress and princes)

In contrast to the main text, which makes the empress 天豐津媛命*, one alternative version makes her 泉媛*, daughter of 猪手*, younger brother of 葉江, district chief of 磯城, and another alternative version makes her 飯日媛*, daughter of 太眞稚彦*, district chief of 磯城. In addition, a note of an alternative version, 「天皇母弟武石彦奇友背命」, is attached (this note runs over into the next paragraph).

An alternative version (Emperor Kōshō's empress)

In contrast to the main text, which makes the empress 世襲足媛*, one alternative version makes her 渟名城津媛*, daughter of 葉江, district chief of 磯城, and another alternative version makes her 大井媛*, daughter of 倭國豐秋狹太雄*.

An alternative version (Emperor Kōan's empress)

In contrast to the main text, which makes the empress 押媛*, one alternative version makes her 長媛*, daughter of 葉江, district chief of 磯城, and another alternative version makes her 五十坂媛*, daughter of 五十坂彦*, district chief of 十市 (十市縣主)*.

An alternative version (Emperor Kōrei's empress)

In contrast to the main text, which makes the empress 細媛命*, one alternative version makes her 春日千乳早山香媛*, and another alternative version makes her 眞舌媛*, daughter of an ancestor of the district chiefs of 十市 and others.

An alternative version (Emperor Kōgen's princes)

To the entry giving the empress's children as three is attached a note of an alternative version: 「天皇母弟少彦男心命也」.

An alternative version (Emperor Kaika's mausoleum)

In contrast to the main text, which gives the mausoleum as the 春日率川坂本陵*, the alternative version gives it as the 坂上陵*, and also records his age as 「時年百十五」.

In the original text

苞藏禍心。圖害二弟

手研耳命*, harboring an evil heart, schemed to harm his two younger brothers.

神八井耳命則手脚戰慄不能放矢

Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto's hands and feet trembled, and he could not shoot the arrow.

汝特挺神武。自誅元惡

Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto's words praising his younger brother: “With your outstanding martial valor, you yourself struck down the chief of evil.”

彦太忍信命。是武内宿禰之祖父也

彦太忍信命* is the grandfather of Takeuchi-no-Sukune.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 2 — end of the section on Emperor Jinmu (the rebellion of 當藝志美美命)

In the Kojiki, the elder half-brother 當藝志美美命* took as wife 伊須氣余理比賣*, Jinmu's empress, and tried to kill his three younger brothers, so 伊須氣余理比賣 warned her children by means of songs. The Nihon Shoki has neither the story of his marrying the empress nor the songs, and holds that 神渟名川耳尊 and Kamuyaimimi-no-Mikoto learned of the plot by themselves and forestalled it.

娶其嫡后伊須氣余理比賣之時
Kojiki, Part 2 — section on Emperor Suizei

The Kojiki gives the age of 神沼河耳命 * (Emperor Suizei) as forty-five and places his tomb at 衝田岡*. The Nihon Shoki holds that he died at eighty-four, and records his mausoleum, in the entry for the 1st year of the next emperor, as the 倭桃花鳥田丘上陵*.

天皇御年肆拾伍歳
Kojiki, Part 2 — section on Emperor Kōrei

The Kojiki holds that Emperor Kōrei's sons Ōkibitsuhiko-no-Mikoto (大吉備津日子命) and Wakahiko-Takekibitsuhiko-no-Mikoto (若日子建吉備津日子命) pacified the province of Kibi, using 針間* as the entrance. The section on Emperor Kōrei in Book 4 of the Nihon Shoki only records the births of 彦五十狹芹彦命 (吉備津彦命) and 稚武彦命 (readings unconfirmed) and that 稚武彦命 is the first ancestor of the Kibi no Omi; no passage on the pacification of Kibi could be confirmed.
