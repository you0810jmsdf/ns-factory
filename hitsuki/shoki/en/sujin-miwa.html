Nihon Shoki, Book 5 — Emperor Sujin · page 13 of 50

Emperor Sujin and the Deity of Miwa: 大田田根子*

崇神天皇と三輪の神（大田田根子）

This page covers the period from the accession of 御間城入彦五十瓊殖天皇 * (Emperor Sujin) to his 9th year, when an epidemic and the people's defection led him to reform the worship of the deities. It records that he had Amaterasu-Ōmikami and Yamato-Ōkunitama-no-Kami (倭大國魂神) worshipped outside the palace, and that when, at the request of Ōmononushi-no-Kami, he had the deity's son 大田田根子* worship him, the epidemic ceased and the land became peaceful.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textIn the 5th year an epidemic killed more than half of the people, and in the 6th year the peasants wandered from their homes and some rebelled. The emperor, in awe of the divine power of the two deities Amaterasu-Ōmikami and Yamato-Ōkunitama (倭大國魂), whom he had been worshipping side by side inside the great hall of the palace, moved them outside. He entrusted Amaterasu-Ōmikami to 豐鍬入姫命* to be worshipped at 笠縫邑* in Yamato, and entrusted Yamato-Ōkunitama-no-Kami (日本大國魂神) to 渟名城入姫命*, but 渟名城入姫命 lost her hair, grew thin, and was unable to perform the worship.
  2. Main textIn the 2nd month of the 7th year, the emperor gathered the eighty myriad deities at 神淺茅原* and performed divination, and Ōmononushi-no-Kami possessed Yamato-totohi-momoso-hime-no-Mikoto and declared that he should be worshipped. When this still had no effect, the emperor purified himself and prayed; Ōmononushi-no-Kami then appeared in a dream and told him that if he had his son 大田田根子* worship him, the land would at once be at peace and the countries beyond the sea would submit of their own accord.
  3. Main text大田田根子*, who was found at 陶邑 in 茅渟縣 (readings unconfirmed), answered that his father was Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami and his mother was 活玉依媛*, daughter of 陶津耳*. In the 11th month of the 7th year, 大田田根子 was made the master of worship for Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami and 長尾市* the master of worship for Yamato-Ōkunitama-no-Kami (倭大國魂神); the eighty myriad deities were worshipped and heavenly shrines, earthly shrines, divine lands and shrine households (天社, 國社, 神地, 神戸) were established, after which the epidemic ceased and the five grains ripened.
  4. Main textIn the 12th month of the 8th year, on the day 大田田根子* was made to worship the Great Deity, 活日*, the sake steward, offered sacred sake and sang, and the ministers and the emperor exchanged songs containing the phrase "瀰和能等能". 大田田根子 is regarded as the founding ancestor of the present-day Miwa no Kimi and others.
  5. Compared with the KojikiThe Kojiki also has the story of the epidemic and 意富多多泥古*, but there the place where he is found is 美努村* in Kawachi, and 意富多多泥古 names himself a descendant counted down from Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami. In the Nihon Shoki he is found at 陶邑 in 茅渟縣 (readings unconfirmed) and names himself the son of Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami.
  6. CommentaryAccording to Kokugakuin University's database of deity names, 墨坂 (Sumisaka) was a main route linking Yamato and Ise, and 大坂 (Ōsaka) a main route linking Yamato and Kawachi, and the worship of Sumisaka-no-Kami and Ōsaka-no-Kami in the 9th year is thought to have been the worship of boundary deities in order to keep the epidemic from entering. What the main text says

御間城入彦五十瓊殖天皇 * (Emperor Sujin) was the second son of 稚日本根子彦大日日天皇*, and his mother was 伊香色謎命*, daughter of 大綜麻杵*, a distant ancestor of the Mononobe clan. He became crown prince at nineteen. He was intelligent by nature and fond of bold strategies from his youth; when he grew up he was broad-minded and prudent, deeply revered the deities of heaven and earth, and had the will to rule the realm. When his father died in the 4th month of the 60th year, he acceded to the throne in the 1st month of his 1st year, and in the 2nd month he made 御間城姫* his empress. The empress bore 活目入彦五十狹茅天皇* and others; the consort 遠津年魚眼眼妙媛* bore Toyokiirihiko-no-Mikoto and 豐鍬入姫命*; and the consort 尾張大海媛* bore 八坂入彦命, 渟名城入姫命 and 十市瓊入姫命 (readings unconfirmed). This year was the sexagenary year (太歳甲申). In the 9th month of the 3rd year he moved the capital to 磯城* and called it the 瑞籬宮* palace, and in the 10th month of the 4th year he issued an edict calling on all his ministers and officials to serve with full loyalty, so that he might bring peace to the realm by following in the footsteps of the imperial ancestors.

In the 5th year there was much pestilence in the land, and more than half of the people died. In the 6th year the peasants wandered from their homes and some rebelled, so that it became hard to govern by virtue, and the emperor apologized to the deities of heaven and earth for his offences from early morning until night. Before this, the two deities Amaterasu-Ōmikami and Yamato-Ōkunitama (倭大國魂) had been worshipped within the emperor's great hall, but he was in awe of their power and uneasy living together with them. He therefore entrusted Amaterasu-Ōmikami to 豐鍬入姫命* to be worshipped at 笠縫邑* in Yamato, and set up the sacred enclosure called 磯堅城神籬*. He entrusted Yamato-Ōkunitama-no-Kami (日本大國魂神) to 渟名城入姫命* to be worshipped, but 渟名城入姫命 lost her hair, grew thin, and was unable to perform the worship.

In the 2nd month of the 7th year, the emperor declared in an edict that the disasters continuing in his reign might be because his government was bad and he had incurred the censure of the deities, and he went out to 神淺茅原*, gathered the eighty myriad deities and performed divination. Then a deity possessed Yamato-totohi-momoso-hime-no-Mikoto and declared that if the emperor revered and worshipped him, the land would become peaceful of itself, naming himself Ōmononushi-no-Kami, the deity dwelling within the land of Yamato. The emperor worshipped him as instructed, but it had no effect, so he bathed, observed abstinence, purified the inside of the palace, and prayed to be instructed in a dream. That night a noble person appeared in his dream, standing facing the door of the hall; he named himself Ōmononushi-no-Kami and said that the land was not at peace because it was his will, and that if the emperor had his son 大田田根子* worship him, it would at once be at peace and the countries beyond the sea would submit of their own accord.

In the 8th month, three people — 倭迹速神淺茅原目妙姫*, 大水口宿禰 * (a distant ancestor of the 穗積臣), and 伊勢麻績君* — reported that they had all had the same dream, in which a noble person had told them to make 大田田根子命* the master of worship for Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami and 市磯長尾市* the master of worship for Yamato-Ōkunitama-no-Kami (倭大國魂神). The emperor proclaimed throughout the realm that 大田田根子 should be sought, and he was found at 陶邑 in 茅渟縣 (readings unconfirmed). When the emperor, at 神淺茅原*, asked whose son he was, 大田田根子 answered that his father was Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami and his mother was 活玉依媛*, daughter of 陶津耳*. Divination found it auspicious to make 伊香色雄*, ancestor of the Mononobe no Muraji, the one who apportions the offerings to the deities, but found it inauspicious to worship other deities at the same time.

In the 11th month, the emperor ordered 伊香色雄* to use the ritual objects made by the eighty craftsmen (八十手) of the Mononobe, and made 大田田根子* the master of worship for Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami and 長尾市* the master of worship for Yamato-Ōkunitama-no-Kami (倭大國魂神). After that, divination about worshipping other deities proved auspicious, so the eighty myriad deities were worshipped separately, and heavenly shrines and earthly shrines (天社, 國社) and divine lands and shrine households (神地, 神戸) were established. With this the epidemic ceased for the first time, the land gradually became calm, the five grains ripened, and the peasants prospered.

In the 4th month of the 8th year, 活日*, a man of the village of 高橋邑*, was made sake steward of the Great Deity. In the 12th month, on the day the emperor had 大田田根子* worship the Great Deity, 活日 himself offered sacred sake to the emperor and composed a song. A banquet was held at the shrine, and at its end the ministers sang a song containing the phrase "瀰和能等能"; the emperor answered with a song containing the same phrase, opened the gate of the shrine and went out. 大田田根子 is regarded as the founding ancestor of the present-day Miwa no Kimi and others. In the 3rd month of the 9th year, a divine person instructed the emperor in a dream to worship Sumisaka-no-Kami with eight red shields and eight red spears, and Ōsaka-no-Kami with eight black shields and eight black spears, and in the 4th month he worshipped them as instructed.

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

One account (一云): the consort 尾張大海媛*

Whereas the main text gives the consort as 尾張大海媛*, one account (一云) in a small note gives her as 八坂振天某邊*, daughter of 大海宿禰*.

Another account (亦云): the mother of 大田田根子*

Besides giving 活玉依媛*, the mother of 大田田根子*, as the daughter of 陶津耳*, the text records, as "亦云" (another account), a tradition that she was the daughter of 奇日方天日方武茅渟祇*.

In the original text

我是倭國城内所居神。名爲大物主神

The deity who possessed Yamato-totohi-momoso-hime-no-Mikoto names himself: "I am a deity dwelling within the land of Yamato, and my name is Ōmononushi-no-Kami."

若以吾兒大田田根子。令祭吾者則立平矣

The message in the dream: if you have my son 大田田根子* worship me, the land will at once be at peace.

父曰大物主大神。母曰活玉依媛

The names of his father and mother that 大田田根子* gave in answer to the emperor.

於是疫病始息

With this the epidemic ceased for the first time.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 2, section on Emperor Sujin (the worship of the deities and the story of Mount Miwa)

In the Kojiki too, in the midst of the epidemic Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami appears in the emperor's dream and tells him to have 意富多多泥古* worship him, but 意富多多泥古 is found at 美努村* in Kawachi and names himself a descendant several generations down from Ōmononushi-no-Ōkami. The Kojiki further records the story in which a needle threaded with hemp yarn is stuck into the clothing of the man who visits 活玉依毘賣*, and by following the thread he is found to be the deity of Mount Miwa (美和山); this could not be confirmed in this part of the Nihon Shoki.

於河内之美努村。見得其人貢進
Kojiki, Part 2, section on Emperor Sujin (the worship of the deities)

In the Kojiki, the worship of Sumisaka-no-Kami with red shields and spears and of Ōsaka-no-Kami with black shields and spears is recorded as part of the same series of rites to quell the epidemic as the worship performed by 意富多多泥古*. In the Nihon Shoki they are worshipped with eight red shields and eight red spears and with eight black shields and eight black spears, in accordance with a separate dream instruction in the 3rd month of the 9th year.

於宇陀墨坂神。祭赤色楯矛
Kojiki, Part 2, section on Emperor Sujin (genealogy of consorts and children)

In the Kojiki there is only a small note in the genealogy saying that 豐鉏比賣命* worshipped at the shrine of the Great Deity of Ise; the story of Amaterasu-Ōmikami and Yamato-Ōkunitama-no-Kami (倭大國魂神) being brought out of the great hall to be worshipped could not be confirmed. In the Nihon Shoki, 豐鍬入姫命* worships Amaterasu-Ōmikami at 笠縫邑* in Yamato.
