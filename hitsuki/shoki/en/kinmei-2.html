Nihon Shoki, Book 19 — Emperor Kinmei (part 2) · page 35 of 50

The Death of King Sŏngmyŏng in Battle and the Fall of Mimana

聖明王の戦死と任那の滅亡

From the 14th year of Emperor Kinmei onward, Paekche kept asking for reinforcements for its wars with Koguryŏ and Silla, and in the 15th year King Sŏngmyŏng was killed in a battle with Silla. In the 23rd year Silla destroyed the government post (官家) of Mimana, and on his deathbed the emperor left word to the Crown Prince to attack Silla and rebuild Mimana.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textIn the 12th month of the 15th year, when 餘昌*, a prince of Paekche, built the fort of 久陀牟羅塞* in Silla territory, his father King Sŏngmyŏng set out to reward his son for his labors, but his road was cut off by the Silla army and he was beheaded by 苦都*, a slave who kept horses in the village of 佐知村*.
  2. Main textIn the 1st month of the 23rd year, Silla attacked and destroyed the government post (官家) of Mimana. In the 6th month the emperor issued an edict condemning Silla, and in the 7th month he sent 紀男麻呂宿禰* and others; but the deputy general 河邊臣瓊缶* was defeated and captured, and 調吉士伊企儺* refused to surrender and was killed.
  3. Main textIn the 4th month of the 32nd year, the emperor, whose illness had grown grave, took the Crown Prince by the hand and left word that he should attack Silla and rebuild Mimana. The emperor died that month and in the 9th month was buried in the 檜隈坂合陵* tomb.
  4. Main textIn the 6th month of the 14th year, the court asked Paekche to send doctors of medicine, doctors of divination and doctors of the calendar in rotation, and to send books of divination, calendars and medicines. In the 2nd month of the 15th year, Paekche sent, as replacements, a Doctor of the Five Classics, monks, a doctor of divination, a doctor of the calendar, a doctor of medicine, herbalists and musicians.
  5. Main textIn the 5th month of the 14th year, a shining camphor log was found floating in the sea of 茅渟*, and the emperor ordered painters to make two Buddha images from it. The main text identifies these as the light-emitting camphor-wood images now at 吉野寺*.
  6. Compared with the KojikiThe section on Emperor Kinmei in the Kojiki records only genealogy, summing up that he had twenty-five royal children, four of whom ruled the realm. Entries corresponding to the death of King Sŏngmyŏng, the fall of Mimana, or the emperor's death and tomb could not be confirmed there.

What the main text says

In the 1st month of the 14th year, Paekche asked for reinforcements. In the 5th month, a report came from Kawachi Province that in the sea of 茅渟* there was a Buddhist chanting sound resounding like thunder and something shining like the sun; 溝邊直* went into the sea, found a shining camphor log and presented it, and the emperor had two Buddha images made from it. In the 6th month, 内臣* was sent to Paekche with gifts of horses, ships, and bows and arrows, and the court asked for doctors of medicine, divination and the calendar to be sent in rotation and for books of divination, calendars and medicines to be sent. In the 7th month, Soga no Iname had 王辰爾* record the taxes on ships; 王辰爾 was made head of ships and was granted the title 船史*. In the 8th month, Paekche submitted a memorial asking that reinforcements be sent quickly, saying that Silla and 狛* were in league and intended to take Ara and cut off the route to Japan.

In the 10th month, 餘昌*, a prince of Paekche, raised all the troops of the country, advanced toward Koguryŏ, and built a stronghold at 百合野*. After exchanging names with the Koguryŏ cavalry, he fought them, killed a Koguryŏ champion, and pursued the king of Koguryŏ to the top of 東聖山*. In the 1st month of the 15th year, 渟中倉太珠敷尊* was installed as Crown Prince. Paekche asked 内臣* in Tsukushi how many reinforcements there would be, and 内臣 answered that one thousand soldiers, one hundred horses and forty ships would be sent. In the 2nd month, a Doctor of the Five Classics, monks, a doctor of divination, a doctor of the calendar, a doctor of medicine, herbalists and musicians were sent from Paekche as replacements, and in the 5th month 内臣 led a naval force across to Paekche.

According to a memorial from Paekche in the 12th month, together with the troops of Wa (倭) they attacked the Silla fortress of 函山城*, and burned the fortress down with the fire arrows of 物部莫奇委沙奇* of 竹斯*. However, 餘昌* ignored the warnings of his old ministers, entered Silla and built the fort of 久陀牟羅塞*, and his father King Sŏngmyŏng went in person to reward his hard-pressed son. Hearing of this, Silla cut off the road, and 苦都*, a slave who kept horses, captured 明王* and cut off his head. 餘昌, who was surrounded, was able to escape and return because a Kuni-no-Miyatsuko of Tsukushi kept shooting arrows and broke through the encirclement, and 餘昌 named this Kuni-no-Miyatsuko 鞍橋君*. One of the Silla generals, fearing the anger of the emperor of Japan, stopped the others from destroying Paekche.

In the 2nd month of the 16th year, 餘昌* sent Prince 惠* to report the death of King Sŏngmyŏng. 蘇我臣* told 惠 how, in the reign of 大泊瀬天皇*, when Paekche was attacked by Koguryŏ, the emperor ordered the head of the Department of Divinities to receive a plan from the gods, invited the deity who founded the country (建邦之神) and sent it to the rescue. He then said that he had heard Paekche had stopped worshiping that deity, but that if it repaired the deity's shrine and worshiped it, the country would prosper. In the 8th month, 餘昌 wished to take holy orders for his father's sake, but was stopped by his ministers and instead had one hundred people take holy orders. During this period, the 白猪屯倉* estate was established in five districts of Kibi, an estate in 兒嶋郡* in Bizen, and the 韓人大身狹屯倉* and 高麗人小身狹屯倉* estates and others in 高市郡* in the province of Yamato (倭国). In the 1st month of the 17th year, 惠 was given weapons and fine horses and sent home, and in the 3rd month of the 18th year 餘昌 succeeded to the throne and became King Widŏk.

In the 21st and 22nd years, Silla's tribute envoys left in anger at their poor treatment, and Silla built a fortress at 阿羅波斯山* to guard against Japan. In the 1st month of the 23rd year, Silla attacked and destroyed the government post (官家) of Mimana, and in the 6th month the emperor issued an edict condemning Silla for betraying the kindness of 氣長足姫尊* (Empress Jingū) and harming Mimana. In the 7th month, 紀男麻呂宿禰* defeated the Silla army, but the deputy general 河邊臣瓊缶*, knowing nothing of the art of war, advanced and was defeated; he was captured together with his wife 甘美媛* and gave up his wife in exchange for his life. 調吉士伊企儺*, though pressed to shout words of disgrace toward Japan, instead kept shouting words reviling the king of Silla and was killed; his son 舅子* also died holding his father, and his wife 大葉子* composed a song. In the 8th month, 大伴連狹手彦* used a plan of Paekche to defeat Koguryŏ, brought back a seven-fold woven curtain (七織帳), an iron house (鉄屋) and other things, and sent 美女媛* and others to Soga no Iname.

In the 26th year, people of Koguryŏ came to Tsukushi and were settled in Yamashiro Province. In the 28th year there were great floods and famine in the provinces. In the 30th year, 膽津*, nephew of 王辰爾*, fixed the registers of the laborers of the 白猪田部* and was granted the title 白猪史*. In the 3rd month of the 31st year, Soga no Iname died. In the 4th month, 江渟臣裾代* reported that the district officials were hiding an envoy of Koguryŏ who had drifted ashore in Koshi, and the emperor had a lodging built in 相樂郡* in Yamashiro Province and had the envoy entertained generously. The Koguryŏ ambassador saw through the lies of 道君*, who had seized the tribute for himself; 膳臣傾子* recovered the tribute and gave it back, and in the 7th month the envoys were brought from Ōmi and received at the 高威館* and 相樂館* lodgings in Yamashiro.

In the 3rd month of the 32nd year, 坂田耳子郎君* was sent to Silla to ask why Mimana had been destroyed. In the 4th month, the emperor's illness grew grave; he hurriedly summoned the Crown Prince, who was away, took him by the hand, and left word that he should attack Silla and rebuild Mimana. That month the emperor died in his inner chamber. In the 5th month a temporary enshrinement (殯) was held at 古市* in Kawachi; in the 8th month a Silla envoy of condolence offered words of mourning at the temporary enshrinement; and in the 9th month he was buried in the 檜隈坂合陵* tomb.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

One text says (一本云)

The main text says that 苦都* cut off King Sŏngmyŏng's head, but one text says that 明王* sat on a folding chair, handed the sword he was wearing to 谷知* (苦都), and had him behead him with it.

One text says (一本云)

The main text says that 苦都* cut off the head and buried it in a pit, but one text says that Silla kept the king's skull and sent the rest of his bones to Paekche with due ceremony, and relates that the skull was buried beneath the steps of the north hall (北廳), called 都堂*.

One text says (一本云)

The main text dates the destruction of the government post of Mimana to the 1st month of the 23rd year, but one text says that Mimana fell in the 21st year, and records that "Mimana" is a collective name for ten countries, including the country of Kara and the country of Ara.

One text says (一本云)

The main text dates 狹手彦*'s attack on Koguryŏ to the 8th month of the 23rd year, but one text says that in the 11th year 大伴狹手彦連*, together with Paekche, drove the Koguryŏ king 陽香* away to 比津留都*.

One text says (一本云)

Concerning the names of the 大身狹屯倉* and 小身狹屯倉* estates, one text explains that they were so named because people of Han (韓人) and of Koguryŏ (高麗人) from various places were made the field laborers of the respective estates.

In the original text

王頭不合受奴手

It is not fitting for a king's head to fall into the hands of a slave (words of King Sŏngmyŏng).

新羅打滅任那官家

Silla attacked and destroyed the government post (官家) of Mimana.

夫勝不忘敗。安必慮危

Even in victory do not forget defeat; even in safety think of danger (words issued to the army by 紀男麻呂*).

汝須打新羅。封建任那

You must attack Silla and rebuild Mimana (the emperor's last words).

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Kinmei (end of the genealogy)

The Kojiki gives Emperor Kinmei twenty-five royal children in all and sums up that four of them — 沼名倉太玉敷命*, 橘之豐日命*, 豐御氣炊屋比賣命* and 長谷部之若雀命* — ruled the realm. This part of the Nihon Shoki records that 渟中倉太珠敷尊* was installed as Crown Prince in the 15th year.

凡此天皇之御子等。并廿五王
Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Kinmei

In the section on Emperor Kinmei in the Kojiki, neither entries on his death, temporary enshrinement and tomb nor entries corresponding to the death of King Sŏngmyŏng or the fall of Mimana could be confirmed. The Nihon Shoki records his death in the 4th month of the 32nd year and his burial in the 檜隈坂合陵* tomb.
