Nihon Shoki, Book 16 — Emperor Buretsu · page 31 of 50

Emperor Buretsu and the Fall of the Heguri Clan

武烈天皇と平群氏の滅亡

After the death of 億計天皇* (Emperor Ninken), the Crown Prince 小泊瀬稚鷦鷯尊* used the troops of Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji to destroy the Great Minister 平群眞鳥*, who had taken the government of the country into his own hands, together with his son Shibi, and then ascended the throne at 泊瀬列城*. After his accession, cruel deeds are recorded year by year, and in the 12th month of the 8th year he died at the 列城宮* palace without any children.

Key points to remember

  1. Main textThe Nihon Shoki describes this emperor as one who was versed in punishments and trials and made the laws clear, yet who also "repeatedly did evil and did not do a single good thing"; it writes that he watched cruel punishments in person, so that everyone in the country trembled with fear.
  2. Main textWhen 億計天皇* died in the 11th year of Emperor Ninken, the Great Minister 平群眞鳥* monopolized the government of the country and sought to become king of Japan. The Crown Prince wished to marry 影媛*, daughter of the Great Chieftain 物部麁鹿火*, but 影媛 was already involved with Shibi, son of 眞鳥, and at the song-gathering place at 海柘榴市* the Crown Prince and Shibi exchanged songs.
  3. Main textLearning that Shibi had already won 影媛*, the Crown Prince had Shibi killed at 乃樂山* by the troops of Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji, and in the 11th month burned the house of 眞鳥* and killed him. It is said that as he died, 眞鳥 cursed the salt of every region but forgot only the salt of the sea at 角鹿*, so that the salt of 角鹿 became food for the emperor, while the salt of the other seas became something the emperor avoided.
  4. Main textIn the 12th month, Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji offered the Crown Prince the honorific title of sovereign, and the Crown Prince ascended the throne at 泊瀬列城* and made Kanamura Great Chieftain. From the 2nd year on, cruel deeds are recorded year by year, such as cutting open the belly of a pregnant woman, tearing out people's nails and making them dig up yams, and making people climb trees and then cutting the trees down.
  5. Main textIn the 9th month of the 6th year, the emperor said that to pass on the country it is precious to establish a child, but that he had no heir; so, to leave his name behind, he established the 小泊瀬舍人*. In the 12th month of the 8th year, the emperor died at the 列城宮* palace.
  6. Compared with the KojikiThe section on Emperor Buretsu in the Kojiki records only his palace and his reign of eight years, the fact that because he had no crown prince he established the 小長谷部*, and the location of his tomb; accounts of the fall of the Heguri clan or of his cruel deeds could not be confirmed there.

What the main text says

小泊瀬稚鷦鷯天皇* was the Crown Prince of 億計天皇*, and his mother was Empress 春日大娘*. He became Crown Prince in the 7th year of 億計天皇. When he grew up, he was fond of the principles of punishment and made the laws clear; he would sit in court until the sun set and without fail bring hidden false accusations to light, and in trials he grasped the true facts. At the same time, it is said, he repeatedly did evil and did not do a single good thing, and he watched every kind of cruel punishment in person, so that everyone in the country trembled with fear.

When 億計天皇* died in the 8th month of the 11th year, the Great Minister 平群眞鳥臣* monopolized the government of the country and sought to become king of Japan; saying it was for the Crown Prince, he had a palace built and lived in it himself. The Crown Prince wished to marry 影媛*, daughter of the Great Chieftain 物部麁鹿火*, and sent a messenger, but because 影媛 was already involved with Shibi, son of 眞鳥*, she answered that she would meet him at 海柘榴市*. When the Crown Prince asked the house of the Heguri Great Minister for official horses, the Great Minister answered as if mocking him and never sent the horses. At the song-gathering place where they had arranged to meet, while the Crown Prince was holding the sleeve of 影媛, Shibi pushed in between the two of them, and the Crown Prince and Shibi exchanged a number of songs.

The Crown Prince learned that Shibi had already won 影媛*, realized the insolence of 眞鳥* and his son, and became very angry. That night he went to the house of Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji and discussed gathering troops, and Kanamura killed Shibi at 乃樂山* with several thousand soldiers. 影媛 followed all the way to that place, watched him being killed, and composed a song lamenting in grief. Even when she was about to return home after burying Shibi, she wept, saying, "Today I have lost my beloved husband," and composed another song.

In the 11th month, Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji asked the Crown Prince to let him strike down 眞鳥*, led troops to surround the house of the Great Minister, and set it on fire. 眞鳥, resenting that his plans had failed, pointed at salt and cursed it as he was killed together with his sons and younger kin; but because he forgot to curse only the salt of the sea at 角鹿*, it is said, the salt of 角鹿 became food for the emperor, while the salt of the other seas became something the emperor avoided. In the 12th month, Kanamura returned the government to the Crown Prince and offered him the honorific title of sovereign; the Crown Prince set up an altar at 泊瀬列城*, ascended the throne, and made Kanamura Great Chieftain. In the 3rd month of the 1st year, he made 春日娘子* his empress; it is recorded that her father's name is not known.

In the 9th month of the 2nd year, he cut open the belly of a pregnant woman to look at the fetus. In the 10th month of the 3rd year, he tore out people's nails and made them dig up yams; in the 11th month he ordered the Great Chieftain 大伴室屋* to send out able-bodied men of 言濃國* and had them build the shape of a fortress at 水派邑*, which was called 城上*. In the 4th month of the 4th year, he pulled out people's hair, made them climb up trees, and enjoyed watching them fall to their deaths when the trees were cut down. In this year in Paekche, 末多王* was removed by the people of the country because he had acted tyrannically toward the people, and 嶋王* was set up and became King Muryŏng.

In the 6th month of the 5th year, he made people go into the sluice of a pond and enjoyed stabbing them to death with a three-bladed spear as they were washed out. In the 9th month of the 6th year, because he had no heir of his own, the emperor decided to establish the 小泊瀬舍人* to pass on his name, and in the 10th month he did not let the Paekche envoy 麻那君* go home, on the grounds that tribute had not been paid for several years. In the 2nd month of the 7th year, he made people climb trees, shot them down with a bow, and laughed; in the 4th month, the king of Paekche newly sent 斯我君*. In the 3rd month of the 8th year, he engaged in a cruel amusement that humiliated women; it is also recorded that he made ponds and gardens and gathered birds and beasts, loved hunting, and indulged in drinking with the palace attendants, forgetting the people's cold and hunger. In the 12th month, the emperor died at the 列城宮* palace.

A plain retelling of what the main text of the Nihon Shoki says (no published translation has been used).

Variant accounts

After its main text, the Nihon Shoki often sets out other versions, introduced with words such as "a certain book says" (一書曰).

One text says (一本云)

It says that Shibi stayed at the house of 影媛* and was killed during that night. In the main text, Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji kills Shibi at 乃樂山* with several thousand soldiers.

The Kudara Shinsen says (百濟新撰云)

It says that because 末多王* was lawless and acted tyrannically toward the people, the people of the country removed him and King Muryŏng was set up. It gives King Muryŏng's personal name as 斯麻王*, says he was the son of Prince 混支* and an elder half-brother of 末多王 by a different mother, and says he was so named because he was born on an island (嶋*) in Tsukushi while 混支 was on his way to Wa (倭). In a note, the compilers of the Nihon Shoki say that since 嶋王* was the son of 蓋鹵王* and 末多王 was the son of 混支王*, it is not clear why he is called an "elder half-brother by a different mother."

One text (一本)

It gives the first line of the Crown Prince's first song, "之裒世", as "彌儺斗".

One text (一本)

It gives "耶賦能之魔柯枳" in the Crown Prince's song as "耶陛哿羅哿枳".

In the original text

又頻造諸惡。不修一善

It means: "He also repeatedly did many evil deeds and did not do a single good thing."

戮鮪臣於乃樂山

The passage stating that the troops of Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji killed Shibi no Omi at 乃樂山*.

唯忘角鹿海鹽、不以爲詛

The passage stating that when 眞鳥* cursed the salt as he died, he forgot only the salt of the sea at 角鹿* and did not curse it.

傳國之機、立子爲貴。朕無繼嗣

The edict of the 9th month of the 6th year. It means: "The key to passing on the country lies in establishing a child, but I have no heir."

  • 平群眞鳥臣*Great Minister. After the death of 億計天皇* he monopolized the government of the country and sought to become king, and was attacked and killed by Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji.

  • 影媛*Daughter of the Great Chieftain 物部麁鹿火*. She was sought by the Crown Prince but was involved with Shibi, and composed songs lamenting his death.
  • 物部麁鹿火大連*Father of 影媛*.
  • Ōtomo no Kanamura no Muraji (大伴金村連)On the Crown Prince's orders he struck down Shibi and 眞鳥*, and on the day of the accession he was made Great Chieftain.
  • 春日娘子*Made empress in the 3rd month of the 1st year. It is recorded that her father's name is not known.
  • 末多王*A king of Paekche. In the 4th year he was removed by the people of the country because he had acted tyrannically toward the people.
  • 嶋王* (King Muryŏng, 武寧王)The king of Paekche who was set up after 末多王* was removed.
  • 麻那君* and 斯我君*Envoys sent from Paekche. 麻那君* was detained in the 6th year; 斯我君* was newly sent in the 7th year, and his son 法師君* is regarded as the ancestor of the 倭君*.

In the Kojiki

Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Buretsu

The section on Emperor Buretsu in the Kojiki records only that he ruled the realm for eight years from the 列木宮* palace at 長谷*, that because he had no crown prince he established the 小長谷部*, and that his tomb is at 石坏岡* in 片岡*; the accounts of cruel deeds found in the Nihon Shoki could not be confirmed there.

坐長谷之列木宮。治天下捌歲也
Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Buretsu

The Kojiki also says that he established a name-bearing group because he had no heir, but its name is 小長谷部*. The Nihon Shoki says that he established the 小泊瀬舍人* by the edict of the 9th month of the 6th year.

无太子。故爲御子代。定小長谷部也
Kojiki, Part 3 — section on Emperor Seinei (the song-gathering with 志毘臣*)

The story of exchanging songs at a song-gathering with 志毘臣* of the Heguri clan over a woman, and then killing 志毘臣, is in the section on Emperor Seinei in the Kojiki; there the rival is 袁祁命* (Emperor Kenzō) and the woman is 大魚*, daughter of 菟田首* and others. In the Nihon Shoki, in this book, the Crown Prince (Emperor Buretsu) and Shibi, son of 眞鳥*, contend over 影媛*, daughter of the Great Chieftain 物部麁鹿火*.

平群臣之祖。名志毘臣。立于歌垣
